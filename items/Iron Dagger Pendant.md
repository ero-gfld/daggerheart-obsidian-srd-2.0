# Iron Dagger Pendant

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to tell the pendant a creature’s name. The pendant gently pulls you toward that creature’s current location until your next long rest.
