# Paragon’s Chain

**_Item_**

As a [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can meditate on an ideal or principle you hold dear and focus your will into this chain. Once per long rest, you can spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to roll a d20 as your [Hope Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) for rolls that directly align with that principle.
