# Corrector Sprite

This tiny sprite sits in the curve of your ear canal and whispers helpful advice during combat. Once per [short rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
