# Insomniac’s Periapt

When you take a rest without clearing [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), you gain a +2 bonus to attack and [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) until your next rest.
