# Elusive Amulet

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can activate this amulet to become *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until you move. While *Hidden* in this way, you remain unseen even if an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) moves to where they would normally see you.
