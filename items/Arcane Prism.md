# Arcane Prism

Position this prism in a location of your choosing and activate it. All allies within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of it gain a +1 bonus to their [Spellcast Rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). While activated, the prism can’t be moved. Once the prism is deactivated, it can’t be activated again until your next [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME).
