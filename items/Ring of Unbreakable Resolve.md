# Ring of Unbreakable Resolve

Once per session, when the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) spends a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can spend 4 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to cancel the effects of that spent Fear.
