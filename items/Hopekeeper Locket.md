# Hopekeeper Locket

During a [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), if you have 6 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can spend a Hope to imbue this locket with your bountiful resolve. When you have 0 Hope, you can use the locket to immediately gain a Hope. The locket must be re-imbued before it can be used this way again.
