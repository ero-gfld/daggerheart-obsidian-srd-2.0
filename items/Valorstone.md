# Valorstone

You can attach this stone to armor that doesn’t already have a feature. The armor gains the following feature. ***Resilient:*** Before you mark your last [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR), roll a d6. On a result of 6, reduce the severity by one threshold without marking an Armor Slot.
