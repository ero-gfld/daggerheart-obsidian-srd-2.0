# Two-Faced Aegis Brooch

Once per rest, flip a coin. On heads, you become [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to the next [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) you take. On tails, you become immune to the next [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) you take.
