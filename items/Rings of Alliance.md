# Rings of Alliance

Two creatures can wear this pair of rose gold rings. Once per session, you can initiate a [Tag Team Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) with whoever is wearing the other ring without spending [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or counting against your session limit for Tag Team Rolls.
