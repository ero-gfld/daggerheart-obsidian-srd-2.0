# Gravity Arbalest

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

*[Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)* · *[Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT)* · *d8+6 mag* · *[Two-Handed](../core_rules/Equipment.md#WEAPONS)*

### FEATURE

***Magnetic:*** When you make an attack with this weapon, you can spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to force all adversaries within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target to make a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (16). Creatures who fail must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and are pulled into Melee range of the target.
