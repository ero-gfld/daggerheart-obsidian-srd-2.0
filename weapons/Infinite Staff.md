# Infinite Staff

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Physical_ _Weapon_

*[Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)* · *[Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT)* · *d10+9 phy* · *[Two-Handed](../core_rules/Equipment.md#WEAPONS)*

### FEATURE

***Extending:*** You can increase the range of this weapon up to [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT). You gain a −1 penalty to [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each step you increase the range by (such as Melee to Very Close, Very Close to Close, or Close to Far).
