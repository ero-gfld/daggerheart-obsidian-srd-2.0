# Legendary Arcane Rifle

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

*[Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)* · *[Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT)* · *d10+12 mag* · *[Two-Handed](../core_rules/Equipment.md#WEAPONS)*

### FEATURE

***Aimed:*** Your attack has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) if the target is within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of you or within Melee range of one of your allies. You can mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to ignore this penalty.
