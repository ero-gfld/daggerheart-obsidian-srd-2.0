# Snapthorn Seed

You can throw this seed at a point you can see. It explodes into a tangle of binding vines that temporarily *Restrains* all creatures within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point.
