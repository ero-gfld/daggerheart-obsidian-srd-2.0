# Sprite Bottle

When you mark your last [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), this bottle shatters to release the Sprite inside. The Sprite clears all your Hit Points before fading from the Mortal Realm.
