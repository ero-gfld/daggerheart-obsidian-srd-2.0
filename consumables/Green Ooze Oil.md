# Green Ooze Oil

You can coat your weapon in this oil. The next successful attack you make with this weapon deals an extra 1d8 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and temporarily *Corrodes* the target. While *Corroded,* the target gains a −2 penalty to their [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
