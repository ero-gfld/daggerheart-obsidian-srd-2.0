# Jar of Lost Voices

You can open this jar to release a deafening echo of voices for a number of minutes equal to your [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203). Creatures within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range unprepared for the sound take 6d8 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
