# Fulgurite Shard

Choose a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. All targets within Close range of that point must succeed on a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (16) or take 3d6 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and mark 1d4 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) as lightning crackles through the area.
