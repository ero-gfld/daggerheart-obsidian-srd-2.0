# Drakemantle

You can use this enchanted ancient dragon hide to gain draconic characteristics until the end of the scene, when the hide falls aways in tatters. Until then, you can fly and gain a +5 bonus to your [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and a +1 bonus to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
