# Vial of Darksmoke

When an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) attacks you, use this vial and roll a number of d6s equal to your [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203). Add the highest result to your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) against the attack.
