# Berserker’s Brew

When you drink this dram of liquid, you gain a bonus to your [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) and a penalty to your Finesse and Knowledge equal to your Instinct (minimum 1). This effect lasts until you make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH) or until your next rest.
