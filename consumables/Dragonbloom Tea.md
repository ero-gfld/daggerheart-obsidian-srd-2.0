# Dragonbloom Tea

You can drink this tea to unleash a fiery breath attack. Make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll against all adversaries in front of you within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against take d20 [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency.
