# Mythic Dust

You can apply this dust to a weapon that deals [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to add a d12 to your next [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) with that weapon.
