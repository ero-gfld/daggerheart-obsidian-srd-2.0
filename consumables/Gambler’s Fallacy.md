# Gambler’s Fallacy

You can spend any number of [handfuls](../core_rules/Equipment.md#GOLD) of gold by placing them into this slotted ceramic jar shaped like a pig. When you throw the jar at a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, it explodes and deals 1d20 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each handful of gold spent to all creatures within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point. All gold within the jar is destroyed.
