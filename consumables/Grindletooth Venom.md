# Grindletooth Venom

You can apply this venom to a weapon that deals [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to add a d6 to your next [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) with that weapon.
