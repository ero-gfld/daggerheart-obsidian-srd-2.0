# Pipeweed

When you choose the Clear [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) during a [short rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can smoke this non-intoxicating leaf to clear an additional Stress. Any other [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who chose the Clear Stress downtime move also gain this benefit.
