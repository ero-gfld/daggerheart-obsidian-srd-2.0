# Major Arcane Shard

You can make a [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to throw this shard at a group of [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against take 4d20 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
