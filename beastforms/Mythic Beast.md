# Mythic Beast

**_Tier 4_** (Upgraded Tier 1 or Tier 2 Options)

***Evolved:*** Pick a Tier 1 or Tier 2 [Beastform](../classes/Druid.md#BEASTFORM%20OPTIONS) option and become a larger, more powerful version of that creature. While you’re in this form, you retain all traits and features from the original form and gain the following bonuses:

- A +9 bonus to [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING)

- A +2 bonus to the trait used by this form

- A +3 bonus to [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION)

- Your damage die increases by one size (d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10, etc.)
