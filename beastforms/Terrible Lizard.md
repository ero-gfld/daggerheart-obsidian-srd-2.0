# Terrible Lizard

**_Tier 4_** (Brachiosaurus, Tyrannosaurus, etc.)

*[Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3 | [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) +2* *[Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) Strength d12+10 phy*

**Gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on:** attack, deceive, scare, track

***Devastating Strikes:*** When you deal [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) to a target within Melee range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force them to mark an additional Hit Point.

***Massive Stride:*** You can move up to Far range without rolling. You ignore rough terrain (at the GM’s discretion) due to your size.
