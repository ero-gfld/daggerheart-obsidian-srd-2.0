# Pouncing Predator

**_Tier 2_** (Cheetah, Lion, Panther, etc.)

*[Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +1 | [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) +3* *[Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) Instinct d8+6 phy*

**Gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on:** attack, climb, sneak

***Fleet:*** **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to move up to Far range without rolling.

***Takedown:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move into Melee range of a target and make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against them. On a success, you gain a +2 bonus to your Proficiency for this attack and the target must mark a Stress.
