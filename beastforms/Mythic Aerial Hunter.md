# Mythic Aerial Hunter

**_Tier 4_** (Dragon, Pterodactyl, Roc, Wyvern, etc.)

*[Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3 | [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) +4* *[Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) Finesse d10+11 phy*

**Gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on:** attack, deceive, locate, navigate

***Carrier:*** You can carry up to three willing allies with you when you move.

***Deadly Raptor:*** You can fly at will and move up to Far range as part of your action. When you move in a straight line into Melee range of a target from at least Close range and make an attack against that target in the same action, you can reroll all damage dice that rolled a result lower than your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
