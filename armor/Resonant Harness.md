# Resonant Harness
**_Tier 4_** _Armor_

* *[Base Thresholds](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR):* 15 / 40  
* *Base Score:* 7

### FEATURE

***Vitreous:*** When you would take Severe or greater damage, you can mark 2 [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) to negate that damage. If you do, you gain a −5 penalty to your [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) until you choose to repair your armor as a [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME).
