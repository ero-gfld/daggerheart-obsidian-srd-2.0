# Vengeance

Play the Vengeance if you want to strike down enemies who harm you or your allies.

**FOUNDATION FEATURES**

***At Ease:*** Gain an additional [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) slot.

***Revenge:*** When an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range succeeds on an attack against you, you can **mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force the attacker to mark a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURE**

***Act of Reprisal:*** When an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) damages an ally within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you gain a +1 bonus to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for the next successful attack you make against that adversary.

**MASTERY FEATURE**

***Nemesis:*** **Spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to *Prioritize* an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) until your next rest. When you make an attack against your *Prioritized* adversary, you can swap the results of your Hope and [Fear Dice](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW). You can only *Prioritize* one adversary at a time.

**BACKGROUND QUESTIONS**

*Answer any of the following background questions. You can* *also create your own questions.*

- Who from your community did you fail to protect, and why do you still think of them?

- You’ve been tasked with protecting something important and delivering it somewhere dangerous. What is it, and where does it need to go?

- You consider an aspect of yourself to be a weakness. What is it, and how has it affected you?

**CONNECTIONS**

*Ask your fellow players one of the following questions for* *their character to answer, or create your own questions.*

- How did I save your life the first time we met?

- What small gift did you give me that you notice I always carry with me?

- What lie have you told me about yourself that I absolutely believe?
