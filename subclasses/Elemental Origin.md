# Elemental Origin

Play the Elemental Origin if you want to channel raw magic to take the shape of a particular element.

**[SPELLCAST](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) TRAIT**

[Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)

**FOUNDATION FEATURE**

***Elementalist:*** Choose one of the following elements at character creation: air, earth, fire, lightning, water.

You can shape this element into harmless effects. Additionally, **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and describe how your control over this element helps an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) you’re about to make, then either gain a +2 bonus to the roll or a +3 bonus to the roll’s damage.

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURE**

***Natural Evasion:*** You can call forth your element to protect you from harm. When an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against you succeeds, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** and describe how you use your element to defend you. When you do, roll a **d6** and add its result to your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) against the attack.

**MASTERY FEATURE**

***Transcendence:*** Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can transform into a physical manifestation of your element. When you do, describe your [transformation](../core_rules/Transformations.md) and choose two of the following benefits to gain until your next rest:

- +4 bonus to your [Severe threshold](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS)

- +1 bonus to a character trait of your choice

- +1 bonus to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING)

- +2 bonus to your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION)
