# Nightwalker

Play the Nightwalker if you want to manipulate shadows to maneuver through the environment.

**[SPELLCAST](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) TRAIT**

[Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)

**FOUNDATION FEATURE**

***Shadow Stepper:*** You can move from shadow to shadow. When you move into an area of darkness or a shadow cast by another creature or object, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to disappear from where you are and reappear inside another shadow within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. When you reappear, you are *Cloaked.*

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURES**

***Dark Cloud:*** Make a **Spellcast Roll (15).** On a success, create a temporary dark cloud that covers any area within Close range. Anyone in this cloud can’t see outside of it, and anyone outside of it can’t see in. You’re considered *Cloaked* from any adversary for whom the cloud blocks line of sight.

***Adrenaline:*** While you're *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS),* add your level to your [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

**MASTERY FEATURES**

***Fleeting Shadow:*** Gain a permanent +1 bonus to your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION). You can use your “Shadow Stepper” feature to move within Very Far range.

***Vanishing Act:*** **Mark a Stress** to become *Cloaked* at any time. When *Cloaked* from this feature, you automatically clear the *Restrained* condition if you have it. You remain *Cloaked* in this way until you roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or until your next rest.
