# Stalwart

Play the Stalwart if you want to take heavy blows and keep fighting.

**FOUNDATION FEATURES**

***Unwavering:*** Gain a permanent +1 bonus to your [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Iron Will:*** When you take [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), you can **mark an additional [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR)** to reduce the severity.

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURES**

***Unrelenting:*** Gain a permanent +2 bonus to your damage thresholds.

***Partners-in-Arms:*** When an ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range takes damage, you can **mark an Armor Slot** to reduce the severity by one threshold.

**MASTERY FEATURES**

***Undaunted:*** Gain a permanent +3 bonus to your damage thresholds.

***Loyal Protector:*** When an ally within Close range has 2 or fewer Hit Points and would take damage, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to sprint to their side and take the damage instead.
