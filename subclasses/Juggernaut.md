# Juggernaut

Play the Juggernaut if you want to pulverize your opponents with crushing blows.

**FOUNDATION FEATURES**

***Rugged:*** Gain a permanent +3 bonus to your [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) threshold.

***Overwhelm:*** When you succeed on an attack against a target, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to throw the target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range or to force them to mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURES**

***Surrounded:*** When you make an attack with a Melee weapon, you can **spend any number of Hope** to target an equal number of additional creatures within Melee range.

***Eye for an Eye:*** Once per rest when an adversary within Melee range forces you to mark any number of Hit Points, you can **mark a Stress** to force them to mark the same number of Hit Points.

**MASTERY FEATURE**

***Pummeljoy:*** When you critically succeed on a Melee weapon attack, you gain an additional Hope, clear an additional Stress, and gain a +1 bonus to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for that attack.

***Not Done Yet:*** When you take Severe damage, you can gain a Hope or clear a Stress.
