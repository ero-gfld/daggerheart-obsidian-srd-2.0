# Pact of the Endless

Play the Pact of the Endless if you want to stand strong against enemies and avoid death.

**[SPELLCAST](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) TRAIT**

[Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)

**FOUNDATION FEATURES**

***Patron’s Mantle:*** **Spend a Favor** to cloak yourself in a terrifying aspect of your Patron that lasts until you take [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or the scene ends. While this effect is active, you gain a bonus to your damage thresholds equal to your tier and have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to intimidate a target.

***Deathless Embrace:*** Once per rest, **spend any number of Favor** to roll an equal number of Patron Dice. For each result of 4 or higher, clear a Hit Point.

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURES**

***Harrowing Invocation:*** When an adversary targets you or an ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with an attack, you can **spend a Favor** to give them disadvantage on the roll. If the adversary fails the roll, they must also mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Damage Sink:*** Once per rest, you can **spend a Favor** to halve incoming damage.

**MASTERY FEATURES**

***Dark Aegis:*** Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) when you would take damage, you can **spend a Favor** instead of marking Hit Points.

***Draining Bane:*** When an adversary targets you or an ally within Very Close range with an attack, you can **spend a Favor** to *Drain* them. When you do, they must mark a Stress, and you can clear a Stress. While *Drained*, the target uses a **d12** instead of a d20 for [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) (including for advantage or disadvantage) until they fail a roll.
