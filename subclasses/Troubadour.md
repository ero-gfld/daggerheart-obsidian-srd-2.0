# Troubadour

Play the Troubadour if you want to play music to bolster your allies.

**[SPELLCAST](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) TRAIT**

[Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)

**FOUNDATION FEATURE**

***Gifted Performer:*** You can play three different types of songs, once each per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME); describe how you perform for others to gain the listed benefit:

- ***Relaxing Song:*** You and all allies within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range clear a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

- ***Epic Song:*** Make a target within Close range temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS).*

- ***Heartbreaking Song:*** You and all allies within Close range gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

**SPECIALIZATION FEATURE**

***Maestro.*** Your rallying songs steel the courage of those who listen. When you give a Rally Die to an ally, they can gain a Hope or clear a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

**MASTERY FEATURE**

***Virtuoso.*** You are among the greatest of your craft and your skill is boundless. You can perform each of your “Gifted Performer” feature’s songs twice per long rest.
