# Castle Siege

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An active siege with an attacking force fighting to gain entry to a fortified castle.*

**Impulses:** Bleed out the will to fight, breach the walls, build tension

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Mercenaries (Harrier, Sellsword, Spellblade, Weaponmaster), Noble Forces ([Archer Squadron](../adversaries/Archer%20Squadron.md), Conscript, [Elite Soldier](../adversaries/Elite%20Soldier.md), [Knight of the Realm](../adversaries/Knight%20of%20the%20Realm.md))

## FEATURES

***Secret Entrance - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can find or recall a secret way into the castle with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. *How do they get in without revealing the pathway to the attackers?* *Are any of the defenders monitoring this path?*

***Siege Weapons (Environment Change) - Action:*** *Consequence* *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. The attacking force deploys siege weapons to try to raze the defenders’ fortifications. Activate the countdown when the siege begins (for a protracted siege, make this a long-term countdown instead). When it triggers, the defenders’ fortifications have been breached and the attackers flood inside. You gain 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), then shift to the [Pitched Battle](Pitched%20Battle.md) [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md) and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it. *What siege weapons are being deployed? Are they magical, mundane,* *or a mixture of both? What defenses must the characters overcome to* *storm the castle?*

***Reinforcements! - Action:*** Summon a [Knight of the Realm](../adversaries/Knight%20of%20the%20Realm.md), a number of [Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) equal to the number of [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), and two [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) of your choice within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a chosen PC as reinforcements. The Knight of the Realm immediately takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). *Who are they targeting first? What formation do they take?*

***Collateral Damage - Reaction:*** When an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) is defeated, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have a stray attack from a siege weapon hit a point on the battlefield. All targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS).

- Targets who fail take **3d8+3** physical or [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
- Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). *What debris is scattered by the attack? What is broken by the strike* *that can’t be easily mended?*
