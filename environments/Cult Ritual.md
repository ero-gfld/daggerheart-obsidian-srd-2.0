# Cult Ritual

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A Fallen cult assembles around a sigil of the defeated gods and a bonfire that burns a sickly shade of green.*

**Impulses:** Profane the land, unite the Mortal Realm with the Circles Below

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Cult of the Fallen ([Cult Adept](../adversaries/Cult%20Adept.md), [Cult Fang](../adversaries/Cult%20Fang.md), [Cult Initiate](../adversaries/Cult%20Initiate.md), [Secret-Keeper](../adversaries/Secret-Keeper.md))

## FEATURES

***Desecrated Ground - Passive:*** Cultists dedicated this place to the Fallen Gods, and their foul influence seeps into it. Reduce the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’ [Hope Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) to a **d10** while in this [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md). The desecration can be removed with a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6). *How do the PCs first notice that something is wrong about this place?* *What fears resurface while hope is kept at bay?*

***Blasphemous Might - Action:*** A portion of the ritual’s power is diverted into a cult member to fight off interlopers. Choose one [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) to become *Imbued* with terrible magic until the scene ends or they’re defeated. An *Imbued* adversary immediately takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) and gains one of the following benefits, or all three if you **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)**:

- They gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on all attacks.
- They deal an extra **1d10** damage on a successful attack.
- They gain the following feature: ***Relentless (2) - Passive.*** This [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them. *How does the enemy change in appearance? What fears do their* *blows bring to the surface?*

***The Summoning - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. When the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) enter the scene or the cult begins the ritual to summon a demon, activate the countdown. Designate one [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) to lead the ritual. The countdown ticks down when a PC rolls with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). When it triggers, summon a [Minor Demon](../adversaries/Minor%20Demon.md) within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the ritual’s leader. If the leader is defeated, the countdown ends with no effect as the ritual fails. *What will the cult do with this leashed demon if they succeed? What* *will they try to summon next?*

***Complete the Ritual - Reaction:*** If the ritual’s leader is targeted by an attack or spell, an ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to be targeted by that attack or spell instead. *What does it feel like to see such devotion turned to the pursuit of fear* *and domination?*
