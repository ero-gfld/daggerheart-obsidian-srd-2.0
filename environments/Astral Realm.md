# Astral Realm

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Traversal](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A psychic realm of energy and emotion, where the strong in spirit can reshape the rules of reality.*

**Impulses:** Attack their psyches, summon their subconscious fears, warp their perceptions

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Demon of Despair](../adversaries/Demon%20of%20Despair.md), [Elemental Spark](../adversaries/Elemental%20Spark.md), *[Pain Priest](../adversaries/Pain%20Priest.md)*, Psychic Vampires ([Head Vampire](../adversaries/Head%20Vampire.md), *[Night Children](../adversaries/Night%20Children.md)*)

## FEATURES

***Out-of-Body Experience - Passive:*** Because the party isn’t physically in this realm, [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), and [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls automatically fail. Additionally, when a character would mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they must lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) instead. If a character can’t mark a Stress, they must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH). *How does the projection of your mental self-image differ from your* *physical appearance? Which one do you consider the “real” you?*

***Mental Manifestations - Passive:*** With a successful [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll, [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can alter the psychic [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md), such as by inverting gravity or conjuring a rapidly self-assembling staircase. A PC can use a mental manifestation once per scene to gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on a roll to navigate the Astral Realm. *Is the construct you manifest a memory from real life or a figment of* *your own imagination? What key detail do you focus on to make it* *more stable?*

***Matter Is Illusion - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (10)*. Time and distance are relative here, and simply moving through the space requires an act of will. To cross the Astral Realm, the party must complete the countdown using only [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), or [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls. On a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) takes **3d8** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). *Do you think or feel your way through this place? What difference* *does it make here?*

***Astral Adversaries - Passive:*** [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) in the Astral Realm are [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), as they are psychic projections instead of flesh and blood. *How does an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)’s astral form differ from their physical form?* *Why are these psychic projections drawn to the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)?*
