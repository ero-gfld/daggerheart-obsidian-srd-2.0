# Masquerade Ball

***Tier 2 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A high society gala where masked revelers trade secrets and favors.*

**Impulses:** Dance the night away, eat and be merry, make secret deals, trade valuable information

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential Adversaries:** Operatives ([Masked Thief](../adversaries/Masked%20Thief.md), [Master Assassin](../adversaries/Master%20Assassin.md), Spy), Revelers (Courtesan, [Merchant Baron](../adversaries/Merchant%20Baron.md), [Royal Advisor](../adversaries/Royal%20Advisor.md))

## FEATURES

***Costumes Required - Passive:*** Everyone at the ball is expected to arrive in costume. PCs have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls to convince others they are someone—or something—they’re not. *What do the PCs come dressed as? What do their costumes reveal* *about their true selves?*

***“Care to Dance?” - Passive:*** While dancing, a PC can make an Agility Roll instead of a Presence Roll to influence others. *What’s notable or unique about the dances that are in fashion here?* *Where did the PCs learn how to perform them?*

***Mistaken Identity - Action:*** A very important person confuses a PC with an even more important person. The target can play into the misunderstanding with a Presence [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a success, the PC can ask the VIP to perform a favor, relay a key piece of information, or make an introduction. On a failure, the PC marks a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) as their true identity is discovered, and they are threatened with ejection from the party. *Who does the VIP mistake them for? What doors does their mistake* *open? What dangers does it bring?*

***Cloak and Dagger - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have an operative try to steal something valuable or assassinate someone important. A PC can make an Instinct Roll to try to notice them before they achieve their objective. On a success, the PC gains the opportunity to intervene. On a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), the PC is erroneously implicated in the crime. *Who does the operative work for? Why did they send them to the* *masquerade?*
