# Grand Feast

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A community-wide celebration featuring way too much drink, food, and fun.*

**Impulses:** Bring everyone together, celebrate a momentous occasion, encourage revelrous abandon

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Apprentice Assassin](../adversaries/Apprentice%20Assassin.md), Guard, Merchant, [Petty Noble](../adversaries/Petty%20Noble.md), Sellsword, Spy

## FEATURES

***Loose Lips & Fast Friends - Passive:*** Everyone is here to have a good time. [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls to socialize with other revelers. *What is everyone celebrating? Where’s the party? Who’s footing the* *bill?*

***The Evening’s Entertainment - Action:*** The host of the feast kicks off the entertainment portion of the evening. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to initiate a performance, competition, or other lighthearted diversion. A PC who succeeds becomes an *Honored* *Guest*. Until the feast ends, an *Honored Guest* can claim a gift from the host, then clear the condition. *What does the host want to see or hear more than anything else?* *What can they offer the PCs in return?*

***“There’s Always That One…” - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to *Trap* a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in conversation with the most unpleasant person at the party until the PC succeeds on an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to extricate themself from this social black hole. While *Trapped*, a PC can’t make action rolls except to try to clear this condition. When a *Trapped* PC fails an action roll, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). *What makes this dud so unpleasant? What’s something the PC cares* *about that the dud constantly diminishes or dismisses? What boring* *or awful thing can the dud not shut up about?*

***Last Call - Reaction:*** After a long night, the partygoers start filtering out. When the feast concludes, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who participated in the festivities clear all [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). Additionally, each *Honored Guest* clears all [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and gains an additional Hope, then clears the condition. Each PC who overindulged must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *Hungover* until their next rest. While *Hungover*, a PC gains a −1 penalty to [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). *What crazy stories do the PCs share with one another as they leave* *the party? Who do the PCs see unexpectedly canoodling afterward?*
