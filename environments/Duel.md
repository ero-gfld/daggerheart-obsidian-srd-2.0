# Duel

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) challenges one of the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to single combat as the rest of the party aids them from the sidelines.*

**Impulses:** Focus on one [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), goad them into overcommitting, inflict an embarrassing defeat, sideline the rest of the party

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** Special (see “Relative [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)”) | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Any

## FEATURES

***“I Demand Satisfaction.” - Passive:*** When an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) challenges a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to one-on-one combat, the PC chooses whether to accept. If they reject the challenge, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If a PC accepts an adversary’s challenge, activate a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6) and a [Consequence Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (4). If the Progress Countdown triggers first, the PC wins the Duel and gains a favor or concession from their adversary. If the Consequence Countdown triggers first, the adversary wins the Duel and claims a favor or concession from the PC. *Why was this PC chosen? What do they expect to win if they prove* *victorious?*

***Relative Strength - Passive:*** The [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) of this [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md) equals that of the [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) who issued the challenge. *Who issues the challenge? What are they fighting for?*

***Rules of Engagement - Passive:*** During the Duel, the challenged [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) is the only one who can make [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). Other PCs can only aid the challenged PC indirectly: They can [Help an Ally](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), initiate [group action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or [Tag Team Rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS), and use any of their features that don’t directly affect the challenging [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md). *Where does this conflict take place? How does the [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)* *offer the rest of the party an opportunity to aid their ally without* *interfering?*

***Dirty Tricks - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the challenging [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) or one of their allies cheat. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can notice their underhanded action with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) and respond in kind. *What’s off-limits in this conflict? How does the adversary’s ally bend* *the rules without breaking them, or break the rules without getting* *caught? How can the PCs rebalance the scales?*
