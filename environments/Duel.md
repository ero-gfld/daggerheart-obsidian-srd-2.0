# Duel

***Tier 2 [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An adversary challenges one of the PCs to single combat as the rest of the party aids them from the sidelines.*

**Impulses:** Focus on one PC, goad them into overcommitting, inflict an embarrassing defeat, sideline the rest of the party

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** Special (see “Relative [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)”) | **Potential Adversaries:** Any

## FEATURES

***“I Demand Satisfaction.” - Passive:*** When an adversary challenges a PC to one-on-one combat, the PC chooses whether to accept. If they reject the challenge, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If a PC accepts an adversary’s challenge, activate a Progress [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6) and a Consequence Countdown (4). If the Progress Countdown triggers first, the PC wins the Duel and gains a favor or concession from their adversary. If the Consequence Countdown triggers first, the adversary wins the Duel and claims a favor or concession from the PC. *Why was this PC chosen? What do they expect to win if they prove* *victorious?*

***Relative Strength - Passive:*** The [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) of this environment equals that of the adversary who issued the challenge. *Who issues the challenge? What are they fighting for?*

***Rules of Engagement - Passive:*** During the Duel, the challenged PC is the only one who can make action rolls. Other PCs can only aid the challenged PC indirectly: They can Help an Ally, initiate [group action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or Tag Team Rolls, and use any of their features that don’t directly affect the challenging adversary. *Where does this conflict take place? How does the environment* *offer the rest of the party an opportunity to aid their ally without* *interfering?*

***Dirty Tricks - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the challenging adversary or one of their allies cheat. A PC can notice their underhanded action with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Reaction Roll and respond in kind. *What’s off-limits in this conflict? How does the adversary’s ally bend* *the rules without breaking them, or break the rules without getting* *caught? How can the PCs rebalance the scales?*
