# Imperial Court

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The majestic domain of a powerful empire, lavishly appointed with stolen treasures.*

**Impulses:** Justify and perpetuate imperial rule, seduce rivals with promises of power and comfort

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Bladed Guard](../adversaries/Bladed%20Guard.md), Courtesan, [Knight of the Realm](../adversaries/Knight%20of%20the%20Realm.md), Monarch, Spy

## FEATURES

***All Roads Lead Here - Passive:*** While in the Imperial Court, a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls made to take actions that don’t fit the imperial way of life or support the empire’s dominance. *How does the way language is used make even discussing alternative* *ways of living difficult? What obvious benefits for loyalty create* *friction when you try to discuss alternatives?*

***Rival Vassals - Passive:*** The [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can find imperial subjects, vassals, and supplicants in the court, each vying for favor, seeking proximity to power, exchanging favors for loyalty, and elevating their status above others’. Some might be desperate to undermine their rivals, while others might even be open to discussions that verge on sedition. *How do they benefit from vassalage, and what has it cost them?* *What exploitation drives them to consider opposing the unstoppable?*

***The Gravity of Empire - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to present a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) with a golden opportunity or offer to satisfy a major goal in exchange for obeying or supporting the empire. The target must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, they must mark all their [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or accept the offer. If they have already marked all their Stress, they must accept the offer or exile themselves from the empire. On a success, they must mark **1d4** Stress as they’re taxed by temptation. *What do the PCs want so desperately they might consider throwing* *in with this ruthless power? How did imperial agents learn the PC’s* *greatest desires?*

***Imperial Decree - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to tick down a long-term [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) related to the empire’s agenda by **1d4**. If this triggers the countdown, a proclamation related to the agenda is announced at court as the plan is executed. *What display of power or transfer of wealth was needed to expedite* *this plan? Whose lives were disrupted or upended to make this* *happen?*

***Eyes Everywhere - Reaction:*** On a result with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **spend a Fear** to have someone loyal to the empire overhear seditious talk within the court. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) to notice that the group has been overheard so they can try to intercept the witness before the PCs are exposed. *How has the empire compromised this witness? Why is their first* *impulse to protect the empire, even if doesn’t treat them well?*
