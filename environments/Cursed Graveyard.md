# Cursed Graveyard

***Tier 1 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A sprawling cemetery filled with crumbling headstones, twisted trees possessed by the dead, and crypts overgrown with moss and ivy.*

**Impulses:** Bury them six feet deep, choke with fog, lure with promises of entombed treasure, unleash the restless dead

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **Potential Adversaries:** Corrupted Spirits ([Deeproot Defender](../adversaries/Deeproot%20Defender.md), *Phantom*), Living Dead ([Skeleton Warrior](../adversaries/Skeleton%20Warrior.md), [Zombie Pack](../adversaries/Zombie%20Pack.md), [Patchwork Zombie Hulk](../adversaries/Patchwork%20Zombie%20Hulk.md))

## FEATURES

***Room for One More - Passive:*** If a PC marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) while in the graveyard, they become a *Specter* instead of making a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH). While a *Specter*, a PC can communicate with the living but can’t interact with the physical world, and they must stay within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of their corpse or cross through the veil of death. This condition is cleared when the PC clears a HP. *What new information or experience does a PC gain from becoming* *a specter? How can the party reunite the PC’s spirit with their flesh?* *What happens if their ritual goes wrong?*

***Hallowed Ground - Passive:*** At the center of the graveyard is a seemingly undisturbed chapel. On a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll, a PC can discover this structure is sanctified against the cemetery’s denizens. While inside the chapel, the PCs have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to fight or drive off spirits and undead. *Who sanctified this place, and what happened to them? What holy* *relics can be found here?*

***Misty Miasma - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to fill an area within Far range with a low-lying ectoplasmic fog that seeps up through the soggy earth. When a PC rolls with Fear while in the miasma, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). *What horrors do the PCs see in the fog? What disorienting smell does* *the miasma emit?*

***The Dead Arise - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have undead hands burst forth from the ground beneath a PC. The target must succeed on an Agility or Presence [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark a Stress and become temporarily *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* as hands grasp them. *Who were these people in life? Why were they buried here? Why won’t* *they stay dead?*

***The Dead Don’t Die Easy - Reaction:*** When an adversary would mark their last HP, roll a **d6**. On a result of 5 or 6, the adversary doesn’t mark it. *Who commanded this land to reject death? How do the dead come* *back different here?*
