# Chaos Realm

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Traversal](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An otherworldly space where the laws of reality are unstable and dangerous.*

**Impulses:** Annihilate certainty, consume power, defy logic

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Outer Realms Monstrosities (Abomination, Corruptor, Thrall)

## FEATURES

***Impossible Architecture - Passive:*** Up is down, down is right, right is starward. Gravity and directionality themselves are in flux, and any attempt to move through this realm is an odyssey unto itself, requiring a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8). On a failure, a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) in addition to the roll’s other consequences. *What does it feel like to move in a space so alien to the Mortal Realm?* *What landmark or point do you fixate on to maintain your balance?* *What bizarre landmarks do you traverse on your journey?*

***Everything You Are This Place Will Take from You - Action:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 1d4)*. Activate the countdown. When it triggers, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or their highest trait is temporarily reduced by **1d4** unless they mark a number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) equal to its value. Any lost trait points are regained if the PC critically succeeds or escapes the Chaos Realm. *How does this place try to steal from you that which makes you* *legendary? What does it feel like to have this power taken from you?*

***Unmaking - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, they take **4d10** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). *What glimpse of other worlds do you catch while this place tries to* *unmake you? What core facet of your personality does the unmaking* *try to erase?*

***Outer Realms Predators - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon an [Outer Realms Abomination](../adversaries/Outer%20Realms%20Abomination.md), an [Outer Realms Corruptor](../adversaries/Outer%20Realms%20Corruptor.md), and **2d6** [Outer Realms Thralls](../adversaries/Outer%20Realms%20Thrall.md), who appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a chosen [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in defiance of logic and causality. Immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) one of these [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md), and you can **spend an additional Fear** to automatically succeed on that [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)’s standard attack. *What half-consumed remnants of the shattered world do these* *monstrosities cast aside in pursuit of living flesh? What jagged* *reflections of former personhood do you catch between moments* *of unquestioning malice?*

***Disorienting Reality - Reaction:*** On a result with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can ask the [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to describe which of their fears the Chaos Realm evokes as a vision of reality unmakes and reconstitutes itself to the PC. The PC loses a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If it is their last Hope, you gain a Fear. *What moment do they see? If it’s a memory, how is it warped by this* *place? How hard will it be to hold on to the real memory?*
