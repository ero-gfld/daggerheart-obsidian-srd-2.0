# Abandoned Grove

***Tier 1 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A former druidic grove lying fallow and fully reclaimed by nature.*

**Impulses:** Draw in the curious, echo the past

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **Potential Adversaries:** Beasts (Bear, [Dire Wolf](../adversaries/Dire%20Wolf.md), [Glass Snake](../adversaries/Glass%20Snake.md)), Grove Guardians ([Minor Treant](../adversaries/Minor%20Treant.md), [Sylvan Soldier](../adversaries/Sylvan%20Soldier.md), [Young Dryad](../adversaries/Young%20Dryad.md))

## FEATURES

***Overgrown Battlefield - Passive:*** There has been a battle here. A PC can make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to identify evidence of that fight. On a [success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), learn all three pieces of information below. On a success with Fear, learn two. On a failure, a PC can mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to learn one and gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the next [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to investigate this environment. A PC with an appropriate background or [Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207) can learn an additional detail and ask a follow-up question about the scene and get a truthful (if not always complete) answer.

- Traces of a battle (broken weapons and branches, gouges in the ground) litter the ground.
- A moss-covered tree trunk is actually the corpse of a treant.
- Still-standing trees are twisted in strange ways, as if by powerful magic. *Why did these groups come to blows? Why is the grove unused now?*

***Barbed Vines - Action:*** Pick a point within the grove. All targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point must succeed on an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **1d8+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and become *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* by barbed vines. *Restrained* lasts until they’re freed with a successful Finesse or Strength roll or by dealing at least 6 damage to the vines. *How many vines are there? Where do they grab you? Do they pull you* *down or lift you off the ground?*

***You Are Not Welcome Here - Action:*** A [Young Dryad](../adversaries/Young%20Dryad.md), two [Sylvan Soldiers](../adversaries/Sylvan%20Soldier.md), and a number of [Minor Treants](../adversaries/Minor%20Treant.md) equal to the number of PCs appear to confront the party for their intrusion. *What are the grove guardians concealing? What threat to the forest* *could the PCs confront to appease the [Dryad](../adversaries/Dryad.md)?*

***Defiler - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon a [Minor Chaos Elemental](../adversaries/Minor%20Chaos%20Elemental.md) drawn to the echoes of violence and discord. They appear within Far range of a chosen PC and immediately take the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). *What color does the grass turn as the elemental appears? How does* *the chaos warp insects and small wildlife within the grove?*
