# Megastorm

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An extreme weather event suddenly manifests, forcing the party to balance their own survival against the safety of others.*

**Impulses:** Blow hurricane winds, choke with mudslides, crumble structures, flood, throw debris, light up the sky

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Greater Water Elemental](../adversaries/Greater%20Water%20Elemental.md), [Storm Titan](../adversaries/Storm%20Titan.md)

## FEATURES

***Stormfront - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8).* When the storm arrives, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) rolls with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). When it triggers, the storm disperses or moves on to a different area. *Where is the safest place to weather the storm? What are the people* *here willing to risk their lives for?*

***Hazardous Conditions - Passive:*** The Megastorm’s extreme wind, fog, and cloud cover make navigating the entire area difficult. [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can’t move as part of an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) without succeeding on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). *Who else lost in the gyre? How can you lead them out?*

***Bomb Cyclone - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon an extreme weather event, such as a thunderstorm or tornado, at a point of your choice. Each target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point must succeed on a [reaction roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) using a trait you choose to avoid being pummeled by hail, thrown into the air, blasted by lightning, or a similar effect. Targets who fail take **2d10+2** damage of a type you choose. *Is this the worst weather any of the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) have experienced? What in* *the immediate area can the PCs use to protect themselves?*

***Stormheart - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to reveal the living battery of the Megastorm. Summon a Storm Giant or [Greater Water Elemental](../adversaries/Greater%20Water%20Elemental.md) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), then immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them. Until this [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) is defeated, the “Stormfront” [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) doesn’t tick down. When this adversary is defeated, the Megastorm ends. *Did this creature conjure the storm for their own purposes, or do they* *serve an even greater entity? Why did they designate this place for* *destruction?*

***Panic in the Streets - Action:*** The Megastorm is sowing fear and chaos among the locals. Describe how the storm puts an [NPC](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#RUNNING%20GM%20NPCS) in danger. If the [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who next takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) doesn’t use their action to rescue the endangered NPC, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). *Who’s taking advantage of the situation? Who’s supposed to be in* *charge here, and what happened to them?*
