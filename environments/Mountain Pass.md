# Mountain Pass

***Tier 2 [Traversal](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*Stony peaks that pierce the clouds, with a twisting path winding its way up and over through many switchbacks.*

**Impulses:** Exact a chilling toll in supplies and stamina, reveal magical tampering, slow down travel

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **Potential Adversaries:** Beasts (Bear, [Giant Eagle](../adversaries/Giant%20Eagle.md), [Glass Snake](../adversaries/Glass%20Snake.md)), [Chaos Skull](../adversaries/Chaos%20Skull.md), [Minotaur Wrecker](../adversaries/Minotaur%20Wrecker.md), [Mortal Hunter](../adversaries/Mortal%20Hunter.md)

## FEATURES

***Engraved Sigils - Passive:*** Large markings and engravings have been made in the mountainside. A PC with a relevant background or [Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207) identifies them as weather magic increasing the power of the icy winds. A PC who succeeds on a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll can recall information about the sigils, potential information about their creators, and the knowledge of how to dispel them. If a PC critically succeeds, they recognize that the sigils are of a style created by ridgeborne enchanters and they gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on a roll to dispel the sigils. *Who laid this enchantment? Are they nearby? Why did they want the* *weather to be more daunting?*

***Avalanche - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to carve the mountain with an icy torrent, causing an avalanche. All PCs in its path must succeed on an Agility or Strength [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or be bowled over and carried down the mountain. A PC using rope, pitons, or other climbing gear gains advantage on this roll. Targets who fail are knocked down the mountain to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, take **2d20** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), and must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). Targets who succeed must mark a Stress. *How do the PCs try to weather the avalanche? What approach do* *the characters take to find one another when their companions go* *hurtling down the mountainside?*

***Raptor Nest - Reaction:*** When the PCs enter the raptors’ hunting grounds, two [Giant Eagles](../adversaries/Giant%20Eagle.md) appear at Very Far range of a chosen PC, identifying the PCs as likely prey. *How long has it been since the eagles last found prey? Do they have* *eggs in their nest, or unfledged young?*

***Icy Winds - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 4)*. When the PCs enter the mountain pass, activate the countdown. When it triggers, all characters traveling through the pass must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or mark a Stress. A PC wearing clothes appropriate for extreme cold gains advantage on these rolls. *What parts of the PC’s bodies go numb first? How do they try to keep* *warm as they press forward?*
