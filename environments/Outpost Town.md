# Outpost Town

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A small town on the outskirts of a nation or region, close to a dungeon, tombs, or other adventuring destinations.*

**Impulses:** Drive the desperate to certain doom, profit off of ragged hope

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Jagged Knife Bandits](../adversaries/Jagged%20Knife%20Bandit.md) (Hexer, Kneebreaker, Lackey, Lieutenant, Shadow, Sniper), [Masked Thief](../adversaries/Masked%20Thief.md), Merchant

## FEATURES

***Rumors Abound - Passive:*** Gossip is the fastest-traveling currency in the realm. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can inquire about major events by making a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. What they learn depends on the outcome of their roll, based on the following criteria:

- **[Critical Success](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Learn about two major events. The [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can ask one follow-up question about one of the rumors and get a truthful (if not always complete) answer.
- **[Success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Learn about two events, at least one of which is relevant to the character’s background.
- **[Success with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Learn an alarming rumor related to the character’s background.
- **Any Failure:** The locals respond poorly to their inquiries. The [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to learn one relevant rumor. *What news do the PCs have that they could pass along to curious* *travelers? What do the locals think about these events?*

***Society of the Broken Compass - Passive:*** An adventuring society maintains a chapterhouse here, where heroes trade boasts and rumors, drink to their imagined successes, and scheme to undermine their rivals. *What boasts do the adventurers here make, and which do you think* *are true?*

***Rival Party - Passive:*** Another adventuring party is here, seeking the same treasure or leads as the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). *Which PC has a connection to one of the rival party members? Do* *they approach the PC first or do they wait for the PC to move?*

***It’d Be a Shame If Something Happened to Your Store - Action:*** The [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) witness as agents of a local crime boss shake down a general goods store. *What trouble does it cause if the PCs intervene?*

***Wrong Place, Wrong Time - Reaction:*** At night, or when the party is alone in a back alley, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to introduce a group of thieves who try to rob them. The thieves appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a chosen [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) and include a [Jagged Knife Kneebreaker](../adversaries/Jagged%20Knife%20Kneebreaker.md), as many Lackeys as there are PCs, and a Lieutenant. For a larger party, add a Hexer or Sniper. *What details show the party that these people are desperate former* *adventurers?*
