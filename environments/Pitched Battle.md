# Pitched Battle

***Tier 3 [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A massive combat between two large groups of armed combatants.*

**Impulses:** Seize people, land, and wealth, spill blood for greed and glory

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **Potential Adversaries:** Mercenaries (Sellsword, Harrier, Spellblade, Weaponmaster), Noble Forces ([Archer Squadron](../adversaries/Archer%20Squadron.md), Conscript, [Elite Soldier](../adversaries/Elite%20Soldier.md), [Knight of the Realm](../adversaries/Knight%20of%20the%20Realm.md))

## FEATURES

***Adrift on a Sea of Steel - Passive:*** Traversing a battlefield during an active combat is extremely dangerous. A PC must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to move at all, and can only go up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range on a success. If an adversary is within Melee range of them, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to make an Agility Roll to move. *Do the combatants mistake you for the enemy or consider you* *interlopers? Can you tell the difference between friend and foe in* *the fray?*

***Raze and Pillage - Action:*** The attacking force raises the stakes by lighting a fire, stealing a valuable asset, kidnapping an important person, or killing the populace. *What is valuable here? Who is most vulnerable?*

***War Magic - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** as a mage from one side uses large-scale destructive magic. Pick a point on the battlefield within Very Far range of the mage. All targets within Close range of that point must make an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **3d12+8** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark a Stress. *What form does the attack take—fireball, raining acid, a storm of* *blades? What tactical objective is this attack meant to accomplish,* *and what comes next?*

***Reinforcements! - Action:*** Summon a [Knight of the Realm](../adversaries/Knight%20of%20the%20Realm.md), a number of Tier 3 Minions equal to the number of PCs, and two adversaries of your choice within Far range of a chosen PC as reinforcements. The Knight of the Realm immediately takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). *Who are they targeting first? What formation do they take?*
