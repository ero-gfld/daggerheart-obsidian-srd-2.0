# Haunted City

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An abandoned city populated by the restless spirits of eras past.*

**Impulses:** Misdirect and disorient, replay apocalypses both public and personal

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Ghosts ([Spectral Archer](../adversaries/Spectral%20Archer.md), [Spectral Captain](../adversaries/Spectral%20Captain.md), [Spectral Guardian](../adversaries/Spectral%20Guardian.md)), ghostly versions of other adversaries (see “Ghostly Form”)

## FEATURES

***Buried Knowledge - Passive:*** The city has countless mysteries to unfold. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who seeks knowledge about the fallen city can make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to learn about this place and discover (potentially haunted) loot.

- **[Critical Success](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Gain valuable information and a related useful item.
- **[Success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Gain valuable information.
- **[Success with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW):** Uncover vague or incomplete information.
- **Any Failure:** Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to find a lead after an exhaustive search. *What greater secrets does the city contain? Why have so many* *ghosts lingered here? What doomed adventurers have met a bad* *fate here already?*

***Ghostly Form - Passive:*** [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) who appear here are of a ghostly form. They have [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects. *What injuries to their physical form speak to their cause of death?* *What unfulfilled purpose holds them in the Mortal Plane?*

***Dead Ends - Action:*** The ghosts of an earlier era manifest scenes from their bygone era, such as a street festival, a revolution, or a heist. These hauntings change the layout of the city around the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), blocking the way behind them, forcing a detour, or presenting them with a challenge, such as mistaking them for rival thieves during the heist. *What do the ghosts want from you? What do you need from them?*

***Apocalypse Then - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to manifest the echo of a past disaster that ravaged the city. Activate a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (5) as the disaster replays around the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). To complete the [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) and escape the catastrophe, the PCs must overcome threats such as rampaging fires, stampeding civilians, collapsing buildings, or crumbling streets, while recalling history and finding clues to escape the inevitable. *Is this the disaster that led the city to be abandoned? What is known* *about this disaster, and how could that help the PCs escape?*
