# Raiding Party

***Tier 1 [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A pirate ship disgorges its crew into a port town or shoreline village to loot and plunder.*

**Impulses:** Light it on fire, overwhelm with speed and numbers, take everything that isn’t nailed down

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **Potential Adversaries:** Ship’s Crew ([Pirate Captain](../adversaries/Pirate%20Captain.md), [Pirate Raiders](../adversaries/Pirate%20Raiders.md), [Pirate Tough](../adversaries/Pirate%20Tough.md))

## FEATURES

***Rally the Locals - Passive:*** *Progress [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (5).* When the pirates first appear, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC succeeds on an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to stem the chaos and destruction of the pirate raid. When it triggers, the locals become inspired by the party’s actions and join the fight against the pirates. Each PC gains a **d6** Morale Die, which they can spend to add to an action, reaction, or [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). *How do the pirates threaten or endanger the town besides attacking* *its inhabitants? What defenses can the PCs take advantage of?*

***Parley - Passive:*** On a successful [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll, a PC knows enough pirate law to demand a parley with the [Pirate Captain](../adversaries/Pirate%20Captain.md). Once the parley is issued, the captain pauses the combat to allow a PC to sue for peace or negotiate the terms of surrender with a Presence Roll. *Why do these pirates respect this captain enough to follow them?* *What do they care about more than ill-gotten booty?*

***“Fire the Cannons!” - Action:*** **Spend up to 3 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the pirate ship fire its cannons into the combat. For each Fear spent, choose a point anywhere on the battlefield. Each PC within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of one or more of those points must make an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **1d8+3** physical damage. Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or take half damage. *What collateral damage do these cannonballs cause? Whose lives do* *they wantonly endanger?*

***Take Captives - Reaction:*** When a number of pirates equal to the number of PCs in the scene are defeated, roll a **d6**. On a result of 5 or 6, the pirates change tactics, capturing locals and retreating to the ship so they can ransom the captives later. Activate a Consequence Countdown (4). It ticks down when a PC makes an action roll. When it triggers, the pirates sail away with their victims. *Who do the pirates deem the best captives, and why? What do they* *want in exchange for the captives’ return?*
