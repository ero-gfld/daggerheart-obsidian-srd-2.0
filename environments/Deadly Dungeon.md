# Deadly Dungeon

***Tier 2 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A dimly lit subterranean network of tunnels and chambers filled with monsters, traps, and treasure.*

**Impulses:** Devour the party’s resources, spring the trap, tempt them with treasure, throw monsters at them, turn them around

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential Adversaries:** Dungeon Denizens (Gobstalker, [Green Ooze](../adversaries/Green%20Ooze.md), *[Ravenous Mockery](../adversaries/Ravenous%20Mockery.md)*, *[Rust Eater](../adversaries/Rust%20Eater.md)*), Rival Adventurers ([Giant Brawler](../adversaries/Giant%20Brawler.md), [Masked Thief](../adversaries/Masked%20Thief.md), [War Wizard](../adversaries/War%20Wizard.md))

## FEATURES

***Twists and Turns - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6).* When the party enters the dungeon, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC succeeds on an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to navigate the passages. When it triggers, the PCs reach the deepest, darkest, best-defended part of the dungeon. *What reward lies at the heart of the maze? Who built this labyrinth* *to protect it?*

***Deep Pockets - Passive:*** Once per rest, the PCs can find a cache of treasure inside the dungeon, either on the corpses of previous delvers or behind hidden doors. On a successful roll to navigate the dungeon, a PC can spend up to 5 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to roll that number of **d6s**. Each result rolled gains the party the following loot or equipment: 
1. Loose gemstones, jewelry, baubles, or coins equal to a [handful](../core_rules/Equipment.md#GOLD) of gold 
2. A common item or consumable 
3. An uncommon item or consumable 
4. A rare item or consumable 
5. A legendary item or consumable 
6. A weapon or an armor from the party’s tier

*What were the previous owners like? How do the PCs plan to get their* *purloined items out of the dungeon?*

***Wandering Monster - Reaction:*** When the party enters a new area of the dungeon, you can **spend a Fear** to summon **1d4** adversaries at or below the party’s tier within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and roll a **d6**. On a result of 1 or 2, the PCs notice warning signs and can prepare for the encounter; the first PC to make an action roll gains [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on it. On a result of 3 or 4, the combat occurs as usual. On a result of 5 or 6, the adversaries get the drop on the PCs and immediately take the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT), and you gain a Fear. *How did these creatures come to be here? What were they doing when* *the PCs encountered them?*

***Booby Traps - Reaction:*** When a PC fails an action roll to navigate the dungeon, activate a trap or hazard such as a spike pit, gauntlet of swinging blades, or locked room slowly filling with water. The party must succeed on a [group action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) to deactivate, disarm, or escape it. On a failure, each PC involved in the group action roll takes **2d6+3** physical or [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) (GM’s choice). *What kinds of traps and hazards fill this dungeon? What does their* *construction tell you about whoever designed them? Have any of* *them been triggered prior to the party’s arrival?*
