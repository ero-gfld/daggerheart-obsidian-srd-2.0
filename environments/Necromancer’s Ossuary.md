# Necromancer’s Ossuary

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A dusty crypt with a library, twisting corridors, and abundant sarcophagi, spattered with the blood of ill-fated invaders.*

**Impulses:** Confound intruders, delve into secrets best left buried, manifest unlife, unleash a tide of undead

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Arch-Necromancer](../adversaries/Arch-Necromancer.md)’s Host ([Perfected Zombie](../adversaries/Perfected%20Zombie.md), [Zombie Legion](../adversaries/Zombie%20Legion.md))

## FEATURES

***No Place for the Living - Passive:*** A feature or action that clears [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) requires spending a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to use. If it already costs Hope, a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must spend an additional Hope. *What does it feel like to try to heal in a place so antithetical to life?*

***Centuries of Knowledge - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can investigate the library and laboratory and make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to learn information related to arcana, local history, and the Necromancer’s plans. *What are the names of the tomes? What project is the necromancer* *working on and what does it communicate about their plans?*

***Skeletal Burst - Action:*** All targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a point you choose in this [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md) must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d8+8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from skeletal shrapnel as part of the ossuary detonates around them. *What ancient skeletal architecture is destroyed? What bones stick in* *your armor?*

***Aura of Death - Action:*** Once per scene, roll a **d4**. Each undead within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Necromancer can clear [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) equal to the result rolled. The undead can choose how that number is divided between HP and Stress. *How does their renewed vigor manifest? Do they look more lifelike or,* *paradoxically, are they more decayed but vigorous?*

***They Just Keep Coming! - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon **1d6** [Rotted Zombies](../adversaries/Rotted%20Zombie.md), two [Perfected Zombies](../adversaries/Perfected%20Zombie.md), or a [Zombie Legion](../adversaries/Zombie%20Legion.md), who appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a chosen [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). *Who were these people before they became the necromancer’s pawns?* *What vestiges of those lives remain for the heroes to see?*
