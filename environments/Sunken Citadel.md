# Sunken Citadel

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*The sunken ruins of a long-lost civilization filled with ancient technology and dark magic.*

**Impulses:** Draw them deep, drown them, reveal ancient wonders

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** [Cursed Merfolk](../adversaries/Cursed%20Merfolk.md), [Deep Dweller](../adversaries/Deep%20Dweller.md), *Plesiosaurus*

## FEATURES

***Underwater Environment - Passive:*** This [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md) is underwater (see the “Moving and Fighting Underwater” rules on page 168 of the core rulebook). In addition to those rules, a creature who breathes air tracks their supply with an Air Supply [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS). Its starting value is equal to 3 + their tier, and it ticks down when they take an action. If a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) fails an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) or rolls with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can use a [GM move](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md) to tick down their countdown an additional time. If the roll is a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), you can tick it down twice instead. Once their countdown triggers, a PC must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) whenever they take an action underwater until they resurface. *Have you ever known anyone who drowned? What do you think about* *to keep calm and stretch your air supply?*

***Air Pockets - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can reset their their Air Supply [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) to its starting value by locating a pocket of air with a successful [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. *What signs or structures do you look for? What does it feel like to* *know this might be the last breath you take?*

***Lost Technology - Passive:*** The ruins are carved with strange magical runes and littered with lost technology. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to learn useful information about this lost civilization from any runes or devices they come across. *Who once called this place home? How did their technology work?* *Why were they ultimately destroyed and cast down into the depths?*

***Deepwater Denizens - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon up to two [Deep Dwellers](../adversaries/Deep%20Dweller.md) and **1d6** [Cursed Merfolk](../adversaries/Cursed%20Merfolk.md) who try to bind the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to their will. These [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a PC, then one immediately takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). *Why do these creatures stay here among the ruins? Are they unwilling* *or unable to resurrect this once-prosperous civilization?*
