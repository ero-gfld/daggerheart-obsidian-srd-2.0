# Time Court

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Event](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*One or more [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) are forcibly plucked from the timeline and put on trial for crimes against continuity.*

**Impulses:** Find the truth, mete out justice, nullify their powers

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Judge (Monarch), Jury ([Hallowed Choir](../adversaries/Hallowed%20Choir.md)), Executioners (*[Temporal Enforcers](../adversaries/Temporal%20Enforcer.md)*)

## FEATURES

***Out of Time - Passive:*** The trial takes place in a pocket dimension that exists beyond the influence of the other realms. While here, [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can’t use their features and abilities. Each player must put their [Domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in their [vault](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS). *What besides the courtroom resides in this pocket dimension? What* *powers maintain the universal barrier protecting this place?*

***Trial by Jury - Passive:*** At the end of the proceedings, a jury votes whether to acquit or convict the party. When the court is called to order, have the prosecution present its case against the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) and start a [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (9). It ticks down when a PC makes an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). When it triggers, the PCs and [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) roll their Judgment Dice. If the party’s total result meets or exceeds the GM’s, the jury acquits them. *Which PCs are put on trial? What are they accused of? What* *potential penalties would a guilty verdict bring?*

***Counsel for the Defense - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to argue for their innocence (such as a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to interpret the letter of the law, a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to play on the jury’s emotions, or an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to point out holes in the prosecution’s case). On a success, the party gains a **d6** Judgment Die. On a [critical success](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), they gain two. On a failure, the prosecution rebuts the PC’s argument, and you gain a **d6** Judgment Die. *What is the weakest part of the prosecution’s case? What is the* *strongest part of the party’s defense?*

***Counsel for the Prosecution - Action:*** The prosecution introduces a damning piece of evidence (such as an eyewitness testimony, test results, or a physical exhibit taken from the scene of the crime), and you gain a **d6** Judgment Die. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to attempt a rebuttal of the evidence with a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203), or [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a success, the party gains the Judgment Die instead of you. On a failure, you gain an additional **d6** Judgment Die. *Who leads the prosecution, and what is their relationship to the Time* *Council? What magic or technology does this court rely on to present* *and record evidence?*

***“Order in the Court!” - Reaction:*** When the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) attempt to escape or end the trial through force, summon a number of [Temporal Enforcers](../adversaries/Temporal%20Enforcer.md) equal to the number of PCs in the scene within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) one. *Is this court’s authority worthy of respect? What besides the might* *of the Time Council and its enforcers justifies their control over the* *timeline?*
