# Local Tavern

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A lively tavern that serves as the social hub for its town.*

**Impulses:** Provide opportunities for adventurers, nurture community

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Guards ([Bladed Guard](../adversaries/Bladed%20Guard.md), [Head Guard](../adversaries/Head%20Guard.md)), Mercenaries (Harrier, Sellsword, Spellblade, Weaponmaster), Merchant

## FEATURES

***What’s the Talk of the Town? - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can ask the bartender, staff, or patrons about local events, rumors, and potential work with a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. On a success, they can pick two of the below details to learn—or three if they critically succeed. On a failure, they can pick one and mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) as the local carries on about something irrelevant.

- A fascinating rumor with a connection to a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s background
- A promising job for the party involving a nearby threat or situation
- Local folklore that relates to something they’ve seen
- Town gossip that hints at a community problem *Who has what kind of information? What gossip do the locals start* *spreading about the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)?*

***Sing For Your Supper - Passive:*** A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can perform one time for the guests by making a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. On a success, they earn **1d4** [handfuls](../core_rules/Equipment.md#GOLD) of gold (**2d4** if they critically succeed). On a failure, they mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). *What piece do you perform? What does that piece mean to you?* *When’s the last time you performed it for a crowd?*

***Mysterious Stranger - Action:*** Reveal a stranger concealing their identity, lurking in a shaded booth. *What do they want? What’s their impression of the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)? What* *mannerisms or accessories do they have?*

***Someone Comes to Town - Action:*** Introduce a significant [NPC](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#RUNNING%20GM%20NPCS) who wants to hire the party for something or who relates to a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s background. *Did they know the PCs were here? What do they want in this town?*

***Bar Fight! - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have a bar fight erupt in the tavern. When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) tries to move through the tavern while the fight persists, they must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll or take **1d6+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from a wild swing or thrown object. A PC can try to activate this feature by succeeding on an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) that would provoke tavern patrons. *Who started the fight? What will it take to stop it?*
