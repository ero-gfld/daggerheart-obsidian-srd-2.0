# Burning Heart of the Woods

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*Thick indigo ash fills the air around a towering moss-covered tree that burns eternally with flames a sickly shade of blue.*

**Impulses:** Beat out an uncanny rhythm for all to follow, corrupt the woods

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Beasts (Bear, [Glass Snake](../adversaries/Glass%20Snake.md)), Elementals ([Elemental Spark](../adversaries/Elemental%20Spark.md)), Verdant Defenders (Dryad, [Oak Treant](../adversaries/Oak%20Treant.md), [Stag Knight](../adversaries/Stag%20Knight.md))

## FEATURES

***Chaos Magic Locus - Passive:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) makes a [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS), they must roll two [Fear Dice](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) and take the higher result. *What does it feel like to work magic in this chaos-touched place?* *What do you fear will happen if you lose control of the spell?*

***The Indigo Flame - Passive:*** [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who approach the central tree can make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to try to identify the magic that consumed this [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md).

- **On a success**: They learn three of the below details. On a [success with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), they learn two.
- **On a failure**: They can mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to learn one and gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the next [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to investigate this [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md).
- **Details:** This is a result of Fallen magic. The corruption is spread through the ashen moss. It can be cleansed only by a ritual of nature magic with a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8). *What Fallen cult corrupted these woods? What have they already* *done with the cursed wood and sap from this tree?*

***Grasping Vines - Action:*** Animate vines bristling with thorns whip out from the underbrush to ensnare the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). A target must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they break free, clearing both conditions, with a successful [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll or by dealing 10 damage to the vines. When the target makes a roll to escape, they take **1d8+4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). *What painful memories do the vines bring to the surface as they* *pierce flesh?*

***Charcoal Constructs - Action:*** Warped animals wreathed in indigo flame trample through a point of your choice. All targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of that point must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **3d12+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage instead. *Are these real animals consumed by the flame or merely constructs of* *the corrupting magic?*

***Choking Ash - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 6)*. When the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) enter the Burning Heart of the Woods, activate the countdown. When it triggers, all characters must make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **4d6+5** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage. Protective masks or clothes give [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the reaction roll. *What hallucinations does the ash induce? What incongruous taste* *does it possess?*
