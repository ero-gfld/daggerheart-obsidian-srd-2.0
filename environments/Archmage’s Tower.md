# Archmage’s Tower

***Tier 3 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*An impossibly tall spire that pierces the heavens and resonates with immense power.*

**Impulses:** Collect magical energy, exhaust intruders, reveal the unknown, unleash bursts of magical power

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **Potential Adversaries:** Alchemical Creations (Chimera, Lamia, *Manticore*), *Archmage*

## FEATURES

***Mystical Defenses - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (12)*. To climb the tower, the PCs must complete the countdown. On a failure or a roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), they trigger one of the archmage’s many arcane traps and hazards, taking **3d10** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), the damage dealt gains a bonus equal to the PC’s level. *What do the designs of the archmage’s defenses tell you about their* *specific area of expertise? What signature style or flourish do the* *traps and hazards all share?*

***Archmage’s Aerie - Passive:*** The top floor of the tower is a many-chambered laboratory, library, and workshop containing everything needed to study magic in its highest forms. A PC with a [Spellcast](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) Trait can make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to partake in the archmage’s research. On a success, they gain a +1 bonus to their Spellcast Rolls until their next [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME). On a failure, their investigation goes wrong, and they take **3d8+4** magic damage. *What part of the aerie calls to you? What do the archmage’s notes* *reveal to you?*

***Amalgamated Adversaries - Reaction:*** **Spend a Fear** to summon two adversaries within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a PC. Each adversary can use the other’s features as if they were their own. *Which creatures has the archmage chosen to combine? What do they* *hope to learn in doing so?*
