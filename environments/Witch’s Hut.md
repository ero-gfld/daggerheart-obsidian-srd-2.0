# Witch’s Hut

***Tier 2 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A rustic, windowless hut sitting atop two huge chicken legs.*

**Impulses:** Creep them out, delay them until the witch’s return, lock them inside, move to a different location, tempt them with magical oddities

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential Adversaries:** [Chicken-Foot Hut](../adversaries/Chicken-Foot%20Hut.md), Enchanted Defenses (*Gargoyle, [Ravenous Mockery](../adversaries/Ravenous%20Mockery.md), Scarecrow*), *Valdenhax*, The Witch’s Victims (*Jack-o’-Lanterns, [Redcap Biters](../adversaries/Redcap%20Biters.md)*)

## FEATURES

***Bigger on the Inside - Passive:*** Though the hut’s exterior is a rustic single-room cottage, the interior is an ever-shifting estate of haunting splendor replete with numerous bedrooms, libraries, and indoor gardens, among other mysterious installations. *Is there any pattern to the way the hut rearranges itself? What is the* *most surprising location the PCs could find here?*

***Creepy Doll Collection - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (5).* Throughout the hut, the PCs can see many, many porcelain or wooden dolls with strangely lifelike eyes. When the party first enters a doll’s [line of sight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT), activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC fails a roll or rolls with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). When it triggers, the dolls come to life and attack. Summon a number of living dolls ([Redcap Biters](../adversaries/Redcap%20Biters.md)) equal to twice the number of PCs. *Who were these dolls before? Is there any way to free them from their* *imprisonment?*

***Witches’ Brew - Passive:*** The hut contains a workshop with every tool or component a witch could need, including a large iron cauldron set atop a smoldering fire. A PC who wants to use the workshop can make a [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) to craft a magic potion or poison from the Consumables table.

- **Any Failure:** The experiment blows up in their face—literally. The PC takes **2d10+2** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
- **Any Success:** The PC rolls a number of d12s equal to 1 + their tier, adds the results together, and gains the consumable that matches that value on the Consumables table. If they critically succeeded, they can reroll any d12s showing a result of 1. *What sorts of concoctions does the witch who owns this hut* *specialize in? What would the witch do if they found the PCs using* *their things?*

***Dislocated - Reaction:*** Time moves differently inside the Witch’s Hut. When the door to the hut is closed, roll a **d6**. On a result of 4 or higher, the hut moves to a different—often impossibly far–location that’s only revealed to the PCs when they open the hut’s door again. *Why does the hut move so often? Where does it wish to bring the* *PCs? Is there any way to aim it at a specific location?*
