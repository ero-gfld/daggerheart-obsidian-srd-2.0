# Bustling Marketplace

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md#ENVIRONMENT%20STAT%20BLOCKS%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The economic heart of the settlement, with local artisans, traveling merchants, and patrons across social classes.*

**Impulses:** Buy low, and sell high, tempt and tantalize with wares from near and far

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **Potential [Adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md):** Guards ([Bladed Guard](../adversaries/Bladed%20Guard.md), [Head Guard](../adversaries/Head%20Guard.md)), [Masked Thief](../adversaries/Masked%20Thief.md), Merchant

## FEATURES

***Tip the Scales - Passive:*** [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll by offering a [handful](../core_rules/Equipment.md#GOLD) of gold as part of the interaction. *Will any coin be accepted, or only local currency? How overt are the* *PCs in offering this bribe?*

***Unexpected Find - Action:*** Reveal to the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) that one of the merchants has something they want or need, such as food from their home, a rare book, magical components, a dubious treasure map, or a magical key. *What cost beyond gold will the merchant ask for in exchange* *for this rarity?*

***Sticky Fingers - Action:*** A thief tries to steal something from a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). The PC must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to notice the thief or lose an item of the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s choice as the thief escapes to a [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) distance. To retrieve the stolen item, the PCs must complete a [Progress Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6) to chase down the thief before the thief completes a [Consequence Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (4) and escapes to their hideout. *What drove this person to pickpocketing? Where is the thief’s hideout* *and how has it avoided notice?*

***Crowd Closes In - Reaction:*** When one of the [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) splits from the group, the crowds shift and cut them off from the party. *Where does the crowd’s movement carry them? How do they feel* *about being alone but surrounded?*
