# Alchemist’s Abandoned Workshop

***Tier 1 [Exploration](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A lightning-powered laboratory built by a scientist searching for the secret to immortality.*

**Impulses:** Break down the barrier between life and death, draw down lightning from the heavens, overclock the machinery

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **Potential Adversaries:** Lab Leftovers (Construct, [Minor Fire Elemental](../adversaries/Minor%20Fire%20Elemental.md), [Patchwork Zombie Hulk](../adversaries/Patchwork%20Zombie%20Hulk.md), [Swarm of Rats](../adversaries/Swarm%20of%20Rats.md))

## FEATURES

***Cabinet of Curiosities - Passive:*** The workshop’s shelves, cupboards, and tables are cluttered with macabre bric-a-brac, including body parts in jars of formaldehyde and bloody surgical implements. Once per scene, a PC can make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to look for something useful in the chaos. *What was the alchemist studying? What smell overwhelms you as* *you shuffle through their things?*

***Strange Machinery - Passive:*** The workshop contains numerous pieces of bizarre technology and scientific instrumentation. A PC can make a Knowledge Roll to try to figure out how it works. On a success, they can get it running. On a failure, they break something and take **1d8+4** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) as they’re shocked by electricity and doused in caustic chemicals. *What do these machines do? What do these instruments measure?*

***It’s Alive! - Action:*** *Consequence [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. A thunderstorm gathers overhead, threatening to jolt the lightning rod assembly installed atop the workshop. Activate the countdown and narrate how the storm’s power increases each time it ticks down when a PC makes an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). When it triggers, lightning strikes the rod assembly and overloads the workshop machinery, which explodes after resurrecting one of the alchemist’s abandoned projects. Each target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the machinery must succeed on an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **1d10** magic damage. Summon a [Failed Experiment](../adversaries/Failed%20Experiment.md) at a location within the workshop and gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) for each PC within Very Close range of it. *What was the alchemist trying to achieve with this experiment?* *Where did they get the materials for it?*
