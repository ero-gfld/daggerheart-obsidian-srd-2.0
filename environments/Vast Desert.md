# Vast Desert

***Tier 2 [Traversal](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md)***
*A parched expanse of sandy dunes, desolate salt flats, and windblown rock formations.*

**Impulses:** Dehydrate, show desolate beauty, threaten danger on the horizon

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Potential Adversaries:** [Acid Burrower](../adversaries/Acid%20Burrower.md), Desert Raiders ([Jagged Knife Bandits](../adversaries/Jagged%20Knife%20Bandit.md)), *Landshark*, *Sandwyrm*

## FEATURES

***Endless Sands - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8).* To cross the desert, the party must complete the countdown. It ticks down whenever a PC succeeds on an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to navigate the seemingly endless expanse. *How do the PCs travel through the desert? What beyond the extreme* *distance and lack of water makes this area difficult to cross?*

***Water Supply - Passive:*** When the party starts their journey, place a number of tokens on this stat block equal to 1 + the number of PCs in the scene. Remove a token at the end of each [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) or when a PC fails an action roll to navigate the desert. When a token would be removed but there are none left, each PC must mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). A PC can add a token to this stat block by using a downtime move and making a successful [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to hunt for water. *What sorts of water-preserving flora or terrain features have the PCs* *heard might be found in this desert? How do the PCs collect and carry* *the water they find?*

***Shifting Sands - Passive:*** The ever-shifting dunes and the whipping wind make trailing creatures through this environment difficult. Action rolls to track creatures through the desert are made with [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE). *How do the dunes and landscape shift whenever the heroes rest?* *What is covered or uncovered by the wind?*

***Thunderstorm - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have a torrential downpour soak the land. Each PC must either seek shelter from the storm or collect rainwater. Each PC who seeks shelter marks a Stress. The PCs who collect rainwater make a [group action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS).

- On a [success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), add **1d4** tokens to the Water Supply.
- On a success with Fear, add 1 token to the Water Supply.
- On a failure with Hope, each PC who took part in the group action roll marks a Stress.
- On a failure with Fear, each PC who took part in the group action roll marks a Stress, and the PC who rolled the lowest takes **2d8+4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from the storm. *How does the storm look to the heroes as it rolls in from miles away?* *Is the storm natural or unnatural? How can you tell?*

***Sandstorm - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to summon a sandstorm that disorients and harries travelers. Each PC takes **2d6+3** physical damage. Until the sandstorm dissipates, a PC who makes an action roll must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll to avoid the swirl of rocks and debris or take **2d6+3** physical damage. *What do the dwellers of this desert wear to weather the sandstorms?* *How are the dwelling places the party finds here designed to* *withstand the winds?*
