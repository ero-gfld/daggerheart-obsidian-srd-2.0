# Demon of Jealousy

***Tier 3 [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A fickle creature of spindly limbs and insatiable desires.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Join in on others’ success, take what belongs to others, hold grudges

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 17/30 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Psychic Assault:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+3 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Manipulation +3

## FEATURES

***Unprotected Mind - Passive:*** The Demon’s standard attack deals [direct damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***My Turn - Reaction:*** When the Demon marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack, **spend a number of [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) equal to the HP marked by the Demon** to cause the attacker to mark the same number of HP.

***Rivalry - Reaction:*** When a creature within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range takes damage from a different adversary, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to add a **d4** to the damage roll.

***What’s Yours Is Mine - Reaction:*** When a PC takes Severe damage within Very Close range of the Demon, you can **spend a Fear** to cause the target to make a [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, the Demon seizes one item or consumable of their choice from the target’s inventory.
