# Outer Realms Corruptor

**_[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)._** _A shifting, formless mass seemingly made of chromatic light._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Confuse, distract, overwhelm
- **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 27/47 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3
- **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Corroding Pseudopod:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+5 mag

### FEATURES

**_Will-Shattering Touch - Passive:_** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) takes damage from the Corruptor, they lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

**_Disgorge Reality Flotsam - Action:_** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to spew partially digested portions of consumed realities at all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets must succeed on a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark 2 Stress.
