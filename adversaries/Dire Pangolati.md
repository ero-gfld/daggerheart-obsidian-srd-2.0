# Dire Pangolati

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A carriage-sized pangolin-coati hybrid covered in armor plating. It has a bladed tail it uses to fling razor-sharp scales.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Escape with a meal, slice and dice, wade into danger

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Tail Slash:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Climbing +3, Scent Tracking +3

## FEATURES

***Armor Eater - Passive:*** When the Pangolati makes a successful attack against a PC, that PC must mark an additional [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) to reduce the severity of the damage.

***Blade Spin - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Pangolati quickly spin in a circle. Each target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **2d8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Blade Fling - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Pangolati flick its tail and fling a razor-edged armor plate. Make an attack against a target within Far range. On a success, the attack deals **2d6+10** physical damage.

***Keratin Scales - Reaction:*** When the Pangolati would mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack, you can **mark a Stress** to reduce the number of HP it marks by 1.
