# Poltergeist

***Tier 1 [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A baleful, translucent spirit that possesses inanimate objects.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Flicker candles, rattle chains, shake windows, throw people around the room

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 9 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/None | **HP:** 2 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Thrown Object:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Spooky +3

## FEATURES

***Specter - Passive:*** The Poltergeist has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects.

***Possessor - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to have the Poltergeist inhabit a nonmagical object that is entirely within Close range, infusing the object with a ghostly glow. The next time the Poltergeist would take damage, the object is destroyed instead, and the Poltergeist is expelled from it.

***Ghost Storm - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Poltergeist pull all unequipped nonmagical objects within Very Close range into a violent vortex. Each target in that area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **1d12+2** physical damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.
