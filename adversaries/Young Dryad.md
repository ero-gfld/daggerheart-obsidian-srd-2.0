# Young Dryad

***Tier 1 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An imperious tree-person leading their forest’s defenses.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Command, nurture, prune the unwelcome

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/11 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Scythe:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Leadership +3

## FEATURES

***Voice of the Forest - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) **1d4** allies within range of a target they can attack without moving. On a success, their attacks deal half damage.

***Thorny Cage - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to form a cage around a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and *Restrain* them until they’re freed with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. When a creature makes an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) against the cage, they must mark a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Dryad makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
