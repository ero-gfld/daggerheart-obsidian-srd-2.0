# Cipactli

***Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A gargantuan crocodile that has a body covered in a patchwork of fish scales, toad skin, and dozens of hungry mouths.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Consume, reshape the land, trample

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/60 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Bite:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+12 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hungry +3, Primordial +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Cipactli can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***Many Mouths - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Cipactli succeeds against must also mark a Stress.

***Frenzied Feeding - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to force all PCs within Close range to succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d6** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The Cipactli clears a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) for each creature who took Severe damage from this attack.

***Quaking Footfalls - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to force each PC within Far range to make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who fail must mark 2 Stress and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they take damage. Targets who succeed must mark a Stress.

***Lifeblooded - Reaction:*** When the Cipactli takes Severe damage, its blood splashes to the ground and instantly erupts into a jungle. All creatures within Close range are temporarily *Restrained.*

***Verdant Explosion - Reaction:*** When the Cipactli marks its last HP, its body erupts into dense vegetation that overtakes the landscape.
