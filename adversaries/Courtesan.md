# Courtesan

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An accomplished manipulator and master of the social arts.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Entice, maneuver, secure patrons

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/13 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −3 | **Dagger:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d4+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Manipulation +3, Socialite +3

## FEATURES

***Searing Glance - Reaction:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range makes a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to cast a gaze toward the aftermath. On the target’s failure, they must mark 2 Stress and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the scene ends or they succeed on a social action against the Courtesan. On the target’s success, they must mark a Stress.
