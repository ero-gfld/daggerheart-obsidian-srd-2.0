# Lamia

***Tier 3 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A snake-tailed humanoid monster who stalks the night to absorb the life energy of sleeping people.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Attack the unaware, avoid sunlight, leech life force, put to sleep, slither through tall grass

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 18/35 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **[Curved Dagger](../weapons/Curved%20Dagger.md):** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Snaky +3

## FEATURES

***Constriction - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Lamia wrap their tail around a PC within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and squeeze. The target must succeed on a [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *Constricted* until the Lamia moves or takes Major or greater damage. While *Constricted*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and must mark a Stress when the Lamia takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Life Leech - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to force a *Vulnerable* or *Restrained* target within Melee range to mark **1d4−1** [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). The Lamia then clears an equal number of HP.

***Sleep Toxin - Reaction:*** When the Lamia makes a successful standard attack against a PC, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make them *Vulnerable* until they succeed on a Instinct Roll (19).
