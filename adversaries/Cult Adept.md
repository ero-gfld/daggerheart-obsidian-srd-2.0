# Cult Adept

***Tier 2 [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An experienced mage wielding shadow and fear.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Curry favor, hinder foes, uncover knowledge

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Rune-Covered Rod:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d4+3 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Fallen Lore +2, Rituals +2

## FEATURES

***Enervating Blast - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make a standard attack against a target within range. On a success, the target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Shroud of the Fallen - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to wrap an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range in a shroud of *Protection* until the Adept marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). While *Protected*, the target has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to all damage.

***Shadow Shackles - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** and choose a point within Far range. All targets within Close range of that point are *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* in smoky chains until they break free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or Instinct Roll. A target *Restrained* by this feature must spend a Hope to make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Fear Is Fuel - Reaction:*** Twice per scene, when a PC rolls a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), clear a Stress.
