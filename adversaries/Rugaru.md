# Rugaru

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A wolf-headed humanoid cursed by the gods to prowl the swamps in search of blood.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Blaspheme, go for the throat, stalk, terrorize

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/14 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Snapping Jaws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Swamps +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Rugaru can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Child of Night - Passive:*** While in moonlight, the Rugaru gains a +2 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Bloodthirsty - Passive:*** When a PC marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from the Rugaru’s standard attack, add a token to this stat block. The Rugaru gains a bonus to [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal to the number of tokens on this stat block. Clear all tokens when the Rugaru takes Severe damage.

***Howl at the Moon - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force each PC within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15). Targets who fail lose a Hope. Gain a Fear for each Hope lost. If a PC has no Hope, they must mark a Stress and become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they roll with Hope.

***Flesh Ripper - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal **1d12+2** direct physical damage.
