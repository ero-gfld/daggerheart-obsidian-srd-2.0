# Sellsword

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An armed mercenary testing their luck.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Charge, lacerate, overwhelm, profit

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Longsword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3 phy

## FEATURES

***Minion (4) - Passive:*** The Sellsword is defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) deals to the Sellsword, defeat an additional [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Sellswords within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 3 [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) each. Combine this damage.
