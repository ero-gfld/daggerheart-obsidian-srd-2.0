# Phantom

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The echo of a lost soul in the form of an ectoplasmic shadow.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Create cold spots, swoop through the air, whisper dark tidings

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/None | **HP:** 2 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Chill Touch:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+2 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Spooky +3

## FEATURES

***Incorporeal - Passive:*** The Phantom has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage and can move through solid objects.

***Fear Aura - Passive:*** A PC who takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Phantom must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (10) or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Lingering Haunt - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (1d6).* When the Phantom is defeated, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC rolls with Fear. When it triggers, clear the Phantom’s [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and immediately spotlight them.
