# Rust Eater

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A pony-sized cockroach coated in a corrosive chitin that turns metal into rust.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid detection, eat rust, sniff out metal

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10/18 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Snuffle +3

## FEATURES

***Stifling Pheromones - Passive:*** A PC who makes an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Rust Eater must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Rust Touch - Passive:*** The Rust Eater’s carapace is coated in a corrosive acid that pits and rusts metal. When a PC within Melee range of the Rust Easter succeeds on an attack against it with a metal weapon, reduce the weapon’s damage die by one step (for example, d8 to d6 or d6 to d4). If a weapon’s damage die is reduced below a d4, the weapon crumbles into rust. The Rust Eater then devours the rust pile and clears a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
