# Triceratops

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large dinosaur that has an armored frill and three horns.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Eat ferns, move in herds for protection, patrol the watering hole

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Horns:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Defender +3

## FEATURES

***Leathery Hide - Passive:*** When the Triceratops would be forced to mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can **mark any number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** instead of an equal number of HP.

***Tail Swipe - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to choose up to three targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each target must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **5d6** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Bull Rush - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to choose a target within Close range. The Triceratops moves into Melee range with the target and makes an attack. On a success, deal **3d10** direct physical damage.
