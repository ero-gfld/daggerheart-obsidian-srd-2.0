# Kraken

***Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A legendary beast of the sea, bigger than the largest galleon, with sucker-laden tentacles and a terrifying maw.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Consume, crush, drown, grapple

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **HP:** 11 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Tentacles:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Swimming +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Kraken can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Many Tentacles - Passive:*** While the Kraken has 7 or fewer marked [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they can make their standard attack against two targets within range.

***Grapple and Drown - Action:*** Make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to grab them with a tentacle and drag them beneath the water. The target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Vulnerable* until they break free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll or the Kraken takes Major or greater damage. While *Restrained* and *Vulnerable* in this way, a target must mark a Stress when they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Boiling Blast - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to spew a line of boiling water at any number of targets in a line up to Far range. All targets must succeed on an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d6+9** physical damage. If a target marks an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) to reduce the damage, they must also mark a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Kraken makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
