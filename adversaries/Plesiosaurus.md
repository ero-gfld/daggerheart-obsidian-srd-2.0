# Plesiosaurus

***Tier 3 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A canoe-length marine lizard that has a long neck and four flippers.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Dive deeper, eat fish, swim peacefully

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 18/35 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Lochs +3

## FEATURES

***Only a Rumor - Passive:*** While deep underwater, the Plesiosaurus can’t be located or targeted.

***Wall of Water - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Plesiosaurus breach and crash, sending a wall of water from one side of its body. Each PC within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range on that side of the Plesiosaurus must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (18) or take **3d10** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and be pushed back to Far range.
