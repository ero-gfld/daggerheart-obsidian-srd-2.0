# Giant Rat

***Tier 1 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cat-sized rodent skilled at scavenging and survival.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Burrow, hunger, scavenge, wear down

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −4 | **Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Keen Senses +3

## FEATURES

***Minion (3) - Passive:*** The Rat is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to the Rat, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Giant Rats within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 1 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
