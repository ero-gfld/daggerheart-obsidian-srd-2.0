# Conscript

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A poorly trained civilian pressed into war.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Follow orders, gang up, survive

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Spears:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 6 phy

## FEATURES

***Minion (6) - Passive:*** The Conscript is defeated when they take any damage. For every 6 damage a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) deals to the Conscript, defeat an additional [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Conscripts within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 6 [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) each. Combine this damage.
