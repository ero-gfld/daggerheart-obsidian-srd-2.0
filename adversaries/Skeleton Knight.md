# Skeleton Knight

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large armored skeleton with a huge blade.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Cut down the living, steal skin, wreak havoc

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/13 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Rusty Greatsword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Knight makes a successful attack, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Cut to the Bone - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Knight succeeds against take **1d8+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark a Stress.

***Dig Two Graves - Reaction:*** When the Knight is defeated, they make an attack against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range (prioritizing the creature who killed them). On a success, the target takes **1d4+8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and loses **1d4** [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
