# Fungispunj Sporeling

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A halfling-sized mushroom creature that defends itself from attackers with tiny puffs of neurotoxic spores.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid violence, spray attackers

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Neuro Spore:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Darkness +3

## FEATURES

***Minion (6) - Passive:*** The Sporeling is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 6 damage a PC deals to the Sporeling, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Fungispunj Sporelings within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 4 magic damage each. Combine this damage.

***Form Up - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to combine three Sporelings within a Close area and replace them with a [Fungispunj Sporophore](Fungispunj%20Sporophore.md).
