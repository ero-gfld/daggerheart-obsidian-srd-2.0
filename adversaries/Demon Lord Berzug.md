# Demon Lord Berzug

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The ruler of a deep circle in the Circles Below and a huge hybrid beast with a serpent’s tail, a lizard’s hind legs, a crab’s claw arms, and a two-faced wolf’s head.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Destroy goodness, sow chaos, unseat rivals

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Abyssal Claws:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Infernal [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** Berzug can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Chaos Lash - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have Berzug lash at up to three [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and force each target to make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **4d20** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Gaze into the Abyss - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have Berzug lock eyes with a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The target must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or gain one of the following conditions (roll a **d6** and use the corresponding result):  
1–2: ***Mesmerized.*** The target immediately moves into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of Berzug and is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear the condition with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.  
3–4: ***Confused.*** The target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) each time they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) until they clear the condition with a successful [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.  
5–6: ***Enthralled.*** Until the PC makes a successful Presence Roll to clear the condition, you can [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them during a [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md) as if they were an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md). When you do so, choose a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the *Enthralled* PC. The *Enthralled* PC moves into Melee range of the target and makes a weapon attack against them using a **d20** instead of [Duality Dice](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).

***Crushing Strike - Reaction:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from Berzug’s standard attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force them to mark a number of Stress equal to the number of HP they marked. You gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Horrifying - Reaction:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) fails an attack against Berzug, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
