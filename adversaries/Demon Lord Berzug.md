# Demon Lord Berzug

***Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The ruler of a deep circle in the Circles Below and a huge hybrid beast with a serpent’s tail, a lizard’s hind legs, a crab’s claw arms, and a two-faced wolf’s head.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Destroy goodness, sow chaos, unseat rivals

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **HP:** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Abyssal Claws:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Infernal [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** Berzug can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Chaos Lash - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have Berzug lash at up to three PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and force each target to make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **4d20** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Gaze into the Abyss - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have Berzug lock eyes with a PC within Far range. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll or gain one of the following conditions (roll a **d6** and use the corresponding result):  
1–2: ***Mesmerized.*** The target immediately moves into Melee range of Berzug and is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Restrained* until they clear the condition with a successful Instinct Roll.  
3–4: ***Confused.*** The target must mark a Stress each time they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) until they clear the condition with a successful Knowledge Roll.  
5–6: ***Enthralled.*** Until the PC makes a successful Presence Roll to clear the condition, you can spotlight them during a GM turn as if they were an adversary. When you do so, choose a target within Close range of the *Enthralled* PC. The *Enthralled* PC moves into Melee range of the target and makes a weapon attack against them using a **d20** instead of [Duality Dice](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).

***Crushing Strike - Reaction:*** When a PC marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from Berzug’s standard attack, you can **mark a Stress** to force them to mark a number of Stress equal to the number of HP they marked. You gain a Fear.

***Horrifying - Reaction:*** When a PC fails an attack against Berzug, you gain a Fear.
