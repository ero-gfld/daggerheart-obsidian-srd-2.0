# Arch-Necromancer

***Tier 4 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A decaying mage adorned in dark, tattered robes.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Corrupt, decay, flee to fight another day, resurrect

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 21 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 33/66 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +6 | **Necrotic Blast:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+8 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Forbidden [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3, Wisdom of Centuries +3

## FEATURES

***Dance of Death - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) **1d4** allies. Attacks they make while spotlighted in this way deal half damage, or full damage if you **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)**.

***Beam of Decay - Action:*** **Mark 2 Stress** to cause all targets within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d20+12** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and you gain a Fear. Targets who succeed take half damage. A target who marks 2 or more [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) must also mark **2 Stress** and becomes *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they roll with Hope.

***Open the Gates of Death - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to summon a [Zombie Legion](Zombie%20Legion.md), which appears at Close range and immediately takes the spotlight.

***Not Today, My Dears - Reaction:*** When the Necromancer has marked 7 or more of their HP, you can **spend a Fear** to have them teleport away to a safe location to recover. A PC who succeeds on an Instinct Roll can trace the teleportation magic to their destination.

***Your Life Is Mine - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 2d6)*. When the Necromancer has marked 6 or more of their HP, activate the countdown. When it triggers, deal **2d10+6** direct magic damage to a target within Close range. The Necromancer then **clears a number of Stress or HP** equal to the number of HP marked by the target from this attack.
