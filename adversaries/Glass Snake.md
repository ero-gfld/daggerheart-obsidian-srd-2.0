# Glass Snake

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A clear serpent with a massive head that leaves behind a glass shard trail wherever they go.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Climb, feed, keep distance, scare

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/10 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Glass Fangs:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Armor-Shredding Shards - Passive:*** After a successful attack against the Snake within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, the attacker must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR). If they can’t mark an Armor Slot, they must mark an [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Spinning Serpent - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within Very Close range. Targets the Snake succeeds against take **1d6+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Spitter - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to introduce a **d6** Spitter Die. When the Snake is in the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT), roll this die. On a result of 5 or higher, all targets in front of the Snake within Far range must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **1d4** physical damage. The Snake can take the spotlight a second time this [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md).
