# Skeleton Archer

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A fragile skeleton with a shortbow and arrows.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Perforate distracted targets, play dead, steal skin

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 9 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/7 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Shortbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

## FEATURES

***Opportunist - Passive:*** When two or more [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) are within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a creature, all damage the Archer deals to that creature is doubled.

***Deadly Shot - Action:*** Make an attack against a *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **3d4+8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
