# Guahalan Spirit Beast

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A brightly colored spirit in the shape of an animal.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Protect, sacrifice, surround

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Bite & Claw:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 5 mag

## FEATURES

***Minion (5) - Passive:*** The Spirit Beast is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 5 damage a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) deals to the Spirit Beast, the PC defeats an additional [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within range the attack would succeed against.

***Ethereal Form - Passive:*** The Spirit Beast has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Guahalan Spirit Beasts within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 5 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) each. Combine this damage.

***Beast Bomb - Reaction:*** When the Spirit Beast is defeated, it explodes in white flame and deals 5 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to creatures within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.
