# Guahalan Spirit Beast

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A brightly colored spirit in the shape of an animal.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Protect, sacrifice, surround

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Bite & Claw:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 5 mag

## FEATURES

***Minion (5) - Passive:*** The Spirit Beast is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Spirit Beast, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Ethereal Form - Passive:*** The Spirit Beast has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Guahalan Spirit Beasts within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 5 magic damage each. Combine this damage.

***Beast Bomb - Reaction:*** When the Spirit Beast is defeated, it explodes in white flame and deals 5 magic damage to creatures within Melee range.
