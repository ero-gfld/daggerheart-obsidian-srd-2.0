# Cursed Merfolk

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A half-fish, half-human creature psychically bound to serve the [Deep Dwellers](Deep%20Dweller.md).*

**Motives & Tactics:** Separate enemies, strike from the shadows, use aquatic [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md) to [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE)

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 18/35 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Chained Trident:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Aquatic +3

## FEATURES

***Fish-Tailed - Passive:*** The Merfolk is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* out of the water.

***Gang Up - Passive:*** The Merfolk gains a +2 bonus to [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against targets within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of one or more allies.

***“Get Over Here!” - Reaction:*** When the Merfolk makes a successful standard attack against a target beyond [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to pull them into Melee range. If you do, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make a standard attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against the target.
