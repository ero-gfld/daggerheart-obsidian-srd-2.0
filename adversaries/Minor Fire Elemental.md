# Minor Fire Elemental

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A living flame the size of a large bonfire.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Encircle enemies, grow in size, intimidate, start fires

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/15 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Elemental Blast:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+4 mag

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Elemental can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Scorched Earth - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to choose a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The ground within Very Close range of that point immediately bursts into flames. All creatures within this area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d8** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from the flames. Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Explosion - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to erupt in a fiery explosion. Make an attack against all targets within Close range. Targets the Elemental succeeds against take **1d8** magic damage and are knocked back to Far range.

***Consume Kindling - Reaction:*** Three times per scene, when the Elemental moves onto objects that are highly flammable, consume them to clear a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Elemental makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
