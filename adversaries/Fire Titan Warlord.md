# Fire Titan Warlord

***Tier 4 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A smoldering, battle-obsessed goliath wearing bespoke armor and wielding a massive maul.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Compete against rivals, conquer the weak, craft colossal war machines

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 21 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 33/66 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +6 | **Maul:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Tactics +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Warlord can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Colossus Crafter - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. When the Warlord enters the scene, activate the countdown. It ticks down when the Warlord is spotlighted. When it triggers, summon the [Gargantuan War Machine](Gargantuan%20War%20Machine.md), which appears at the edge of the battlefield and immediately takes the spotlight.

***Release the Hounds - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Warlord summon two [Demonic Hound Packs](Demonic%20Hound%20Pack.md), which appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and immediately take the spotlight.

***Spinning Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within Very Close range and gain a Fear.

***Ground-Breaking - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Warlord crack the earth with a downward swing of their maul and make an attack against a group within Close range. Targets the attack succeeds against take **4d10+10** [direct damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The area becomes rough terrain, and a PC must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (16) to move through it.
