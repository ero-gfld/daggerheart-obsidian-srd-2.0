# Redcap Candlemaker

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A pale, gaunt cult leader wearing a conical red cap sealed on their head with candle wax. They wield a long rod with a candle atop it made from a hanged criminal’s hand.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Cast long shadows, keep out of the fray, show them the light

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/13 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Burning Candlestick:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+2 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Vigilance +3

## FEATURES

***Hand of Glory - Passive:*** When the Candlemaker appears, place 5 tokens on this stat block. Remove a token whenever the Candlemaker marks a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). While this stat block has 1 or more tokens on it, the Candlemaker is *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS).*

***Torchbearer - Passive:*** The light of the Candlemaker’s Hand of Glory inspires bloodlust in their allies. While this stat block has 1 or more tokens on it, each Redcap [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range gains a +1 bonus to their [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Dance in the Flames - Action:*** **Spend a token** from this stat block. The Candlemaker conjures a ball of fire on a group of [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each target must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d10** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage.
