# Monarch

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The sovereign ruler of a nation, wreathed in the privilege of tradition and wielding unmatched power in their domain.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Control vassals, destroy rivals, forge a legacy

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/32 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Warhammer:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** History +3, Nobility +3

## FEATURES

***Execute Them! - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** per [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in the party to have the group condemned for crimes real or imagined. A PC who succeeds on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll can demand trial by combat or another special form of trial.

***Crownsguard - Action:*** Once per scene, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon six [Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md), who appear at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to enforce the Monarch’s will.

***Casus Belli - Reaction:*** *Long-Term [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8)*. **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to activate after the Monarch’s desire for war is first revealed. When it triggers, the Monarch has a reason to rally the nation to war and the support to act on that reason. You gain **1d4** Fear.
