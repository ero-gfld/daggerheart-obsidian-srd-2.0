# Roc

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A giant falcon that nests in the mountains and unleashes storms.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Breathe lightning and speak thunder, protect eggs, rake with talons, spy from afar

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/35 | **HP:** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Beak & Talons:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d12+8 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Craggy Peaks +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Roc can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***Bird of Prey - Passive:*** Once per GM turn, the Roc can move up to [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range before or after it takes an action.

***Here Comes the Boom - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Roc unleash a storm of lightning and thunder. Make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against **1d4+1** targets within Far range. Targets the Roc succeeds against take **3d12** magic damage. Then each target within Far range of the Roc must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (20). Targets who fail take **3d8** physical damage and are pushed back to Close range. Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Crushing Grasp - Reaction:*** When the Roc makes a successful standard attack, you can **mark a Stress** to have the Roc crush the target with its talons, forcing them to mark **1d4** Stress and *Restraining* them until they succeed on a Strength Roll (20).

***Nest Warden - Evolution:*** When the Roc’s eggs are threatened, it gains the following features:

***Wrathful - Passive:*** The Roc gains a +2 bonus to its [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) and a bonus to damage rolls equal to the number of Stress it has marked.

***Electrifying Aura - Passive:*** The Roc has resistance to magic damage. PCs who fail a roll within Very Close range must spend a Hope or mark **1d4** Stress.
