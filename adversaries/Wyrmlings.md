# Wyrmlings

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (3/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A clutch of newly hatched dragons. As newborns, they’re furious, hungry, and stupid.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Consume, shred, swarm

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 25/45 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Tiny Jaws & Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d6+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Dragon Bond +2

## FEATURES

***Horde (2d6+5) - Passive:*** When the Wyrmlings have marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **2d6+5** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Ravenous - Reaction:*** Once per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md) when a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) marks a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack made by the Wyrmlings, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Wyrmlings again.

***Overwhelm - Reaction:*** When the Wyrmlings take [Minor damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against the attacker.
