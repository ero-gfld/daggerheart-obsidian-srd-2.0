# Hydra

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A quadrupedal scaled beast with multiple long-necked heads, each filled with menacing fangs.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Devour, regenerate, terrify

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 19/35 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Bite:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Many-Headed Menace - Passive:*** The Hydra begins with three heads and can have up to five. When the Hydra takes Major or greater damage, they lose a head.

***Relentless (X) - Passive:*** The Hydra can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) X times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md), where X is the Hydra’s number of heads. Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Regeneration - Action:*** If the Hydra has any marked [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), **spend a Fear** to clear a HP and grow two heads.

***Terrifying Chorus - Action:*** All PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose 2 Hope.

***Magical Weakness - Reaction:*** When the Hydra takes [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), they become *Dazed* until the next roll with Fear. While *Dazed*, they can’t use their Regeneration action but are immune to magic damage.
