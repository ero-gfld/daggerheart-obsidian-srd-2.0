# Darkweave Queen

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A wagon-sized spider woven from shadow-silk by a long-forgotten god. Each of her many eyes bears the face of a different victim.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush prey, climb the walls and ceiling, misdirect, paralyze, stash bodies away for later, steal faces

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Spider Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Giant-Sized +3, Hunter +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Queen can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight her.

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When a PC fails an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against the Queen, you gain a Fear.

***Den Mother - Action:*** Once per scene, **spend 2 Fear** to summon up to two Darkweave adversaries (other than Darkweave Queens), who appear within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and immediately take the spotlight.

***Quicker Than She Looks - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to move up to Far range and make a standard attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).

***Darkfang Envenomation - Reaction:*** When the Queen succeeds on a standard attack, you can **spend a Fear** to make the target *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Restrained* until they succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (12) or take a rest.
