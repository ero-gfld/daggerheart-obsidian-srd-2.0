# Entombed Stonemason

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An unfortunate undead laborer chosen by lottery to wall up their empress’s tomb from the inside.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Never stop working, sacrifice yourself for the empress

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Hammer and Chisel:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Builder +2

## FEATURES

***Minion (5) - Passive:*** The Stonemason is defeated when they take any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Stonemason, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range their attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Entombed Stonemasons within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 5 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

***The Work Never Ends - Reaction:*** When the Stonemason is defeated by an attack that deals physical damage, you can **spend a Fear** to clear the Stonemason’s [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
