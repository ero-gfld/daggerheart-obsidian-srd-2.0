# Entombed Skin Beetles

***Tier 2 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (5/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*Flesh-eating beetles that burrow beneath the skin.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Carpet the ground, consume the living, crawl under clothing

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Pincers:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Tombs +3

## FEATURES

***Horde (2d4+1) - Passive:*** When the Beetles have marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **2d4+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Momentum - Passive:*** When the Beetles make a successful attack against a PC, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Omophagous - Action:*** **Mark a HP** to have 5 Beetles burrow under the skin of a PC within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, forcing them to mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and *Infecting* them. While *Infected*, the target takes 6 direct physical damage every time they roll with Fear. This condition is cleared when the PC clears a HP or a Stress.
