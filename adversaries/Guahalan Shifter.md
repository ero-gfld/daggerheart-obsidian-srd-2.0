# Guahalan Shifter

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A shapeshifting masked warrior who has animalistic features.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Escape, profit, throw smoke

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Warclub:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunter +2, Trapper +2

## FEATURES

***Unleash the Beast - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Shifter adopt a more bestial form. While in this form, they gain a bonus to attack and [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal to the number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) they have marked. Additionally, they can move up to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range instead of Close range before taking an action.

***Pack Attack - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to make a standard attack. On a success, the attack gains a bonus to damage equal to the number of other Shifters within Very Close range of the target.
