# Viper

***Tier 1 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A small serpent that envenomates its prey with hinged fangs.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Lie in wait, slither, strike

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Grounded +4

## FEATURES

***Minion (4) - Passive:*** The Viper is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Viper, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Venomous - Passive:*** When the Viper makes a successful standard attack, the target becomes temporarily *Envenomated*. While *Envenomated*, the target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) whenever they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Vipers within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 1 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
