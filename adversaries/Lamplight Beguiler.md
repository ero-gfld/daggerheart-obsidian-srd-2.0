# Lamplight Beguiler

***Tier 3 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A huge aquatic creature that lures unwary prey into eating range with its dangling, shapeshifting head stalk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Entice with secret knowledge, snap the trap shut, speak through a shapeshifted head stalk

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/32 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Jaws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Charm +3, Secrets +3

## FEATURES

***Doppel-Dangler - Action:*** The membranous cluster at the end of the Beguiler’s head stalk can shapeshift into any humanoid form. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to choose a PC within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Their player must tell you who their PC would find most trustworthy. The Beguiler’s head stalk transforms into that person and gains the ability to speak in their voice.

***Entice - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Beguiler make an alluring overture to a PC within Close range. The PC must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (18) or move into Melee range with the Beguiler. If the Beguiler’s head stalk is transformed into a person the PC would find trustworthy, they have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the reaction roll.

***Gulp - Reaction:*** When a PC moves within Melee range, you can **spend a Fear** to make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against them. On a successful attack, the target takes **3d6** physical damage and is *Swallowed*. While *Swallowed*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and takes **3d6** physical damage when the Beguiler takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). The Beguiler disgorges all *Swallowed* creatures when it takes Major or [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
