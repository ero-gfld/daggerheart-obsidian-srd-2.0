# Wyvern

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A two-legged dragon that flies from its mountaintop aerie to devour livestock.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Cast a long shadow, snatch and grab, wheel through the air

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 18/35 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Teeth & Claws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Death from Above +4

## FEATURES

***Double Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Wyvern make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.

***Terrifying Shriek - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Wyvern let out a spine-chilling screech. All [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range are *Terrified* until they succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (18) to clear the condition or the Wyvern is defeated. While *Terrified*, a PC doesn’t gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) when they roll with Hope.

***Clutch - Reaction:*** When the Wyvern makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Wyvern *[Restrain](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* the target in its claws until the PC succeeds on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (18) or the Wyvern takes [Major](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or greater damage. While *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* in this way, the PC has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
