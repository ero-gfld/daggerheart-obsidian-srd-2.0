# Oak Treant

***Tier 3 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A sturdy animate old-growth tree.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Hide in plain sight, preserve the forest, root down, swing branches

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/40 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Branch:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Forest [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3

## FEATURES

***Just a Tree - Passive:*** Before they make their first attack in a fight or after they become *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*, the Treant is indistinguishable from other trees until they next act or a PC succeeds on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to identify them.

***Seed Barrage - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** and make an attack against up to three targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, pummeling them with giant acorns. Targets the Treant succeeds against take **2d10+5** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Take Root - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to *Root* the Treant in place. The Treant is *Restrained* while *Rooted*, and can end this effect instead of moving while they are [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). While Rooted, the Treant has resistance to physical damage.
