# Stonewraith

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A prowling hunter, like a slinking mountain lion, with a slate-gray stone body.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Defend territory, isolate prey, stalk

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 11/22 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Bite and Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Stonesense +3

## FEATURES

***Stonestrider - Passive:*** The Stonewraith can move through stone and earth as easily as air. While within stone or earth, they are *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to all damage.

***Rocky Ambush - Action:*** While *Hidden*, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to leap into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with a target within Very Close range. The target must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or Instinct [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15) or take **2d8** physical damage and become temporarily *Restrained*.

***Avalanche Roar - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to roar while within a cave and cause a cave-in. All targets within Close range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (14) or take **2d10** physical damage. The rubble can be cleared with a Progress [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (8).

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Stonewraith makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
