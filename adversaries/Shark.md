# Shark

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large aquatic predator, always on the move.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Find the blood, isolate prey, target the weak

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Toothy Maw:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Sense of Smell +3

## FEATURES

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Shark makes a successful attack, all PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a Fear.

***Rending Bite - Passive:*** When the Shark makes a successful attack, the target must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without receiving its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage). If they can’t mark an Armor Slot, they must mark an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Blood in the Water - Reaction:*** When a creature within Close range of the Shark marks HP from another creature’s attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Shark, moving them into Melee range of the target and making a standard attack.
