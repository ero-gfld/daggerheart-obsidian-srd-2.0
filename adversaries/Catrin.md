# Catrin

***Tier 3 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A lavishly adorned skeleton that has an endless appetite for gold.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Dominate the conversation, seek attention, steal everything

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/32 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Spirit Flame:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+5 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Opulence +2, Storyteller +2

## FEATURES

***Attracted to Wealth - Passive:*** PCs carrying at least a bag of gold gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Rolls made to influence the Catrin.

***The Weight of Opulence - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to turn the Catrin’s gaze toward the PC carrying the most gold. The target must succeed on an Instinct [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or lose a Hope for each bag of gold they’re carrying. If the PC loses their last Hope or has no Hope to lose, they mark [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) instead.

***Expensive Failure - Reaction:*** When a PC within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range fails an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you can **mark a Stress** to have the Catrin brazenly steal a [handful](../core_rules/Equipment.md#GOLD) of gold from them.
