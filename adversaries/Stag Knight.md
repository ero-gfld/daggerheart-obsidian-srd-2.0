# Stag Knight

***Tier 3 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A knight with huge, majestic antlers wearing armor made of dangerous thorns.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Isolate, maneuver, protect the forest, weed the unwelcome

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 19/36 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Bramble Sword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Forest [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3

## FEATURES

***From Above - Passive:*** When the Knight succeeds on a standard attack from above a target, they deal **3d12+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead of their standard damage.

***Blade of the Forest - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Knight succeeds against take physical damage equal to **3d4** + the target’s [Major threshold](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Thorny Armor - Reaction:*** When the Knight takes damage from an attack within Melee range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **1d10+5** physical damage to the attacker.
