# Whisper Wraith

***Tier 3 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The echoes of broken promises and dreams unfulfilled, manifesting as a floating, translucent shroud of tattered black velvet in a vaguely humanoid shape.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bind limbs, drop from a great height, lift off the ground, swirl through the air, wrap around heads

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/32 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Shadow Touch:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+4 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Dreams +4

## FEATURES

***Greater Specter - Passive:*** The Wraith has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage and can move through solid objects.

***Spooky - Passive:*** When the Wraith makes a successful attack, all PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Nightmare Shroud - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Wraith wrap itself around a PC within Melee range, *Shrouding* the target until they succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to dislodge the Wraith or the Wraith takes Major or greater damage. While *Shrouded,* the target has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attack rolls and marks a Stress when they roll with Fear. Additionally, damage that would be dealt to the Wraith is split evenly between the Wraith and the *Shrouded* target.
