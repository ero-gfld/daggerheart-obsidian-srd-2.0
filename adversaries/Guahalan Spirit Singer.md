# Guahalan Spirit Singer

***Tier 2 [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A shapeshifting summoner who has animalistic features.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Abjure, bolster, summon

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Spirit Flame:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+4 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ritual +3, Ancient History +2

## FEATURES

***Unleash the Beast - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Spirit Singer adopt a more bestial form. While in this form, they gain a bonus to attack and [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal to the number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) they have marked. Additionally, they can move up to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range instead of Close range before taking an action.

***Beast-Caller - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Spirit Singer summon **2d4** [Guahalan Spirit Beasts](Guahalan%20Spirit%20Beast.md) within Very Close range and immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) one.

***Rage of Spirits - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to spotlight any number of Guahalan Spirit Beasts within Very Close range and have each one move into Melee range with the nearest PC and make a standard attack. If any attacks succeed against the same target, combine their damage.

***Spiritual Sacrifice - Reaction:*** When the Spirit Singer would take damage while within Very Close range of a Guahalan Spirit Beast, you can **spend a Fear** to sacrifice the Spirit Beast and negate the damage.
