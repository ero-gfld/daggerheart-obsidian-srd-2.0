# Fallen Warlord: Realm-Breaker

**_Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)._** _A Fallen God, wreathed in rage and resentment, bearing millennia of experience in breaking heroes' spirits._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Corrupt, dominate, punish, break the weak
- **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 36/66 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5
- **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Barbed Whip:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+7 phy
- **[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Conquest +3, History +2, Intimidation +3

### FEATURES

**_Relentless (2) - Passive:_** The [Realm-Breaker](Realm-Breaker.md) can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

**_Firespite Plate Armor - Passive:_** When the Realm-Breaker takes damage, reduce it by **2d10**.

**_Tormenting Lash - Action:_** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. When a target uses armor to reduce damage from this attack, they must mark 2 [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR).

**_All-Consuming Rage - Reaction: Countdown (Decreasing 8):_** When the Realm-Breaker is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS). When it triggers, create a torrent of incarnate rage that rends flesh from bone. All targets within Far range must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d6+10** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage. For each [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) marked from this damage, summon a [Fallen Shock Troop](Fallen%20Shock%20Troop.md) within Very Close range of the target who marked that HP. If the countdown ever decreases its maximum value to 0, the Realm-Breaker marks their remaining HP and all targets within Far range must mark all remaining HP and make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH).

**_Doombringer - Reaction:_** When a target marks HP from an attack by the Realm-Breaker, all PCs within Far range of the target must lose a Hope.

**_I Have Never Known Defeat (Phase Change) - Reaction:_** When the Realm-Breaker marks their last HP, replace them with the [Undefeated Champion](Undefeated%20Champion.md) and immediately spotlight them.
