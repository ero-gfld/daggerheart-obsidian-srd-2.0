# Berserker Initiate

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A raging warrior who imbibes ritualistic concoctions to enhance their form with aspects of a predatory beast.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Coordinate with packmates, dance around the fire, howl at the moon

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Tooth & Claw:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt +3

## FEATURES

***Pack Fury - Passive:*** The Initiate gains a +1 bonus to their [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of their attack’s target.

***Berserker Rage - Reaction:*** The first time in a scene the Initiate marks their last [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), they gain a +4 bonus to damage rolls.
