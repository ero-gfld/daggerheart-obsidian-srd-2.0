# Giant Scorpion

***Tier 1 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A human-sized arachnid with tearing claws and a stinging tail.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, feed, grapple, poison

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/13 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Pincers:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +2

## FEATURES

***Double Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against two targets within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Venomous Stinger - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to deal **1d4+4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and *Poison* them until their next rest or they succeed on a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (16). While *Poisoned*, the target must roll a **d6** before they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). On a result of 4 or lower, they must mark a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Scorpion makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
