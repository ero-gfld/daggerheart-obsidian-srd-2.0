# Fire Titan

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An armor-clad goliath who has ash-gray skin and fiery hair and wields a masterwork greatsword crafted in their own smithy.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Act valorously, make war, think tactically

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 19/35 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Greatsword:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d12+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Smithing +5, Tactics +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Titan can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Masterwork Armor - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (3).* When the Titan first appears, activate the countdown. It ticks down when the Titan takes Major or [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). Until the countdown triggers, reduce incoming damage by **1d12**. When it triggers, the Titan gains a +1 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) and [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Momentum - Passive:*** When the Titan makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

***Hurl Rock - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to choose a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, then make an attack against all targets between the Titan and that point. Targets the Titan succeeds against take **3d12** physical damage.

***Skull Splitter - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to place a token on this stat block. The next time the Titan is spotlighted, you must **spend the token** from this stat block and make an attack against a PC within Close range. On a success, deal **3d20+5** direct physical damage. On a failure, the target marks a Stress.

***Blazing Heart - Reaction:*** When the Titan marks half or more of their HP, you can **spend a Fear** and **mark any number of Stress** to clear a number of HP equal to the number of Stress marked.
