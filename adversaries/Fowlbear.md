# Fowlbear

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A grizzly bear with a feathery hide and the head of a giant goose.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Drive prey into an ambush, fight to the death, hunt in pairs

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Goose Teeth & Bear Claws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Keen Senses +3, Ponds +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Fowlbear can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***Double Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.

***Dread Honk - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Fowlbear unleash a terrifying, ear-splitting goose honk. Each PC within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail must mark a Stress, move away from the Fowlbear to Far range, and become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the Fowlbear deals damage to them. Targets who succeed must mark a Stress or move away from the Fowlbear to Far range.
