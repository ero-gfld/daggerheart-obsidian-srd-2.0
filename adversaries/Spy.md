# Spy

***Tier 2 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A skilled espionage agent with a knack for being in the right place to overhear secrets.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Cut and run, disguise appearance, eavesdrop

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/17 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −2 | **Dagger:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Espionage +3

## FEATURES

***Gathering Secrets - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to describe how the Spy knows a secret about a PC in the scene.

***Fly on the Wall - Reaction:*** When a PC or group is discussing something sensitive, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to reveal that the Spy is present in the scene, observing them. If the Spy escapes the scene to report their findings, you gain **1d4** Fear.
