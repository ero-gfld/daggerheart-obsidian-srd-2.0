# Giant Recruit

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A giant fighter wearing borrowed armor.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Batter, make a living, overwhelm, terrify

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Warhammer:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 5 phy

## FEATURES

***Minion (7) - Passive:*** The Recruit is defeated when they take any damage. For every 7 damage a PC deals to the Recruit, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Giant Recruits within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 5 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
