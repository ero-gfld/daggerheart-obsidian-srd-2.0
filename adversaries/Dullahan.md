# Dullahan

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A horse-mounted specter holding their own severed head in one hand and wielding a whip fashioned from a human spine in the other.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Doom with a glance, gallop across water, kick with heavy hooves

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/40 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Spine Whip:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10+1 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Nocturnal +3

## FEATURES

***Specter - Passive:*** The Dullahan has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects.

***Spectral Mount - Passive:*** While atop their steed, the Dullahan can move up to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range instead of [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range before taking an action. If the Dullahan is unhorsed, their mount disappears until you **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon it again. The Dullahan is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* while not on their horse.

***Bone Whip Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes **4d4+10** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Death Glare - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The target must make an [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (19). On a success, they mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). On a failure, they mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and activate a Doom [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (4). It ticks down when the Dullahan is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). When it triggers, the target makes a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH). If the Dullahan is defeated or the PCs take a rest, all Doom Countdowns end.
