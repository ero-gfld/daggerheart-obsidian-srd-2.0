# Shapeshifting Fiend Revealed

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A towering humanoid who has an insectile head and multiple sets of glowing red eyes.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Claim souls, confuse and misdirect, escape to the Circles Below, teleport

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/32 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Mantis Claws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d12+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hellbound +3, Immortal +2

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Fiend can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Fiendish Resistance - Passive:*** The Fiend has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and can’t be fooled by magic illusions.

***Demonic Rage - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make a standard attack with a bonus to the [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal the amount of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) the Fiend has marked. On a success, the attack deals [direct damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Hall of Mirrors - Action:*** **Mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Fiend summon four illusory duplicates and activate a Duplicate [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (4). When the Fiend is targeted by an attack, they gain a bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) equal to the countdown die’s current value. The countdown ticks down when a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) fails an attack against the Fiend. When it triggers, the duplicates vanish.

***Flash Claw - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Fiend unleash an arc of magical energy. Make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the attack succeeds against take **3d20** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Mind Games - Reaction:*** When an attack targeting the Fiend from beyond [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range succeeds with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Fiend instantly switch places with a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within Very Close range. The PC takes the damage instead.
