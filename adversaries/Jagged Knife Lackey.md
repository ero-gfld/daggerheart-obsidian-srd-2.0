# Jagged Knife Lackey

***Tier 1 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A thief with simple clothes and small daggers, eager to prove themselves.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Escape, profit, throw smoke

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 9 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −2 | **Daggers:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Thief +2

## FEATURES

***Minion (3) - Passive:*** The Lackey is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to the Lackey, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Jagged Knife Lackeys within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
