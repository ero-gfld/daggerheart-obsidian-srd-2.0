# Common Ruffian

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A local laborer or artisan pushed by circumstances to assail others.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Do violence, seek security and survival

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/10 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Improvised Weapon:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Desperate +3

## FEATURES

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Ruffians within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. The Ruffians move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal **1d8** physical damage each. Combine this damage.

***Survival Instinct - Reaction:*** When the Ruffian marks half their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), roll a **d6**. On a result of 4 or higher, the Ruffian flees the scene. Otherwise, you gain a Fear.
