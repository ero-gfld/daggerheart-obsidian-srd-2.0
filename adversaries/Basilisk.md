# Basilisk

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A six-legged monitor lizard that has the head of a vulture, a spiked backbone, and a petrifying gaze.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Move slow and steady, savor petrified prey, turn everyone to stone, watch and wait

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/19 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Jaws & Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+4 phy

## FEATURES

***Petrify - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a success, the target marks a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). On a failure, the target becomes *Stiff*. If the target is already *Stiff*, they become *Petrified.* While *Stiff*, the PC must mark a Stress each time they act until they take a rest or the Basilisk is defeated. While *Petrified*, the PC can’t act. The *Petrified* condition can be cleared only by applying Basilisk saliva to the target.

***CRONCH! - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Basilisk chomp down on a *Petrified* [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The target must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH).
