# Mountain Troll

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A hideous giant born out of a massive rock. They wear purloined armor and a belt of severed heads or skulls.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, bellow, hurl large boulders, smash, stomp

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Skull Flail:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Mountains +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Troll can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Stolen Armor - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS).* When combat begins, activate a countdown with a value equal to the number of PCs. When the Troll takes damage, reduce the severity by one threshold, then tick down the countdown. When it triggers, the Troll evolves (see “Enraged Mountain Troll”).

***Flail Swipe - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets in front of the Troll within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Troll succeeds against take **2d8+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Enraged Mountain Troll - Evolution:*** The Troll loses their “Stolen Armor” and “Flail Swipe” features, then gains a +1 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). Additionally, they gain the “Double Swipe” feature and replace “Skull Flail” with the following standard attack: **Claw Swipe:** Very Close | 1d10+3 phy

***Double Swipe - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to move up to Close range and make two standard attacks against a target within Melee range. If both attacks succeed, combine the damage, and the target loses a Hope.
