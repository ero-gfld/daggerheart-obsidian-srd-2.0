# Ruby Dragon

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A huge winged dragon that has visible flames beneath its glittering, gem-encrusted scales. Its body heat melts stone.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Crack the earth, melt, protect treasure

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 30/50 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +6 | **Tooth & Claw:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+8 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Mountains +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Dragon can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it.

***Fearsome - Passive:*** When the Dragon succeeds on an attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) or a PC within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range fails an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Melting Breath - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Dragon unleash a blast of fire on all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in front of it within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each target must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d12+10** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without gaining its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).

***Volcanic Fissure - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Dragon snap its tail against the ground, opening a lava rift that flows over the area within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within that area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who succeed immediately move out of the area. Targets who fail take **4d8+5** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Until the Dragon is defeated, a creature who enters the area takes **4d8+5** magic damage.

***Hot-Blooded - Reaction:*** When a creature makes a successful attack against the Dragon within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, the Dragon’s blood erupts from the wound and burns the attacker, forcing them to mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR). If they can’t mark an Armor Slot, they mark **1d4** [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
