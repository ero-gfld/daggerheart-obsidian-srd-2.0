# Scarecrow

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A bundle of straw bound into a humanoid shape, clothed in rags, and animated by a trapped soul.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Pull yourself together, scratch and claw

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/15 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Sickle:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Birds +2, Fields +2

## FEATURES

***Made of Straw - Passive:*** The Scarecrow has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage but takes double damage from fire.

***Terror Vision - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Scarecrow lock eyes with a PC within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, dealing **2d8** magic damage and making them *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they roll with Hope.
