# Berserker Alpha

***Tier 2 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A veteran commander who quaffs magic elixirs to change into a beast and lead their packmates on a hunt.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Embrace the change, howl at the moon, lead the pack, run down enemies

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Tooth & Claw:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt +3, Keen Senses +2

## FEATURES

***Pack Fury - Passive:*** The Alpha gains a +1 bonus to their [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of their attack’s target.

***Bark at the Moon - Action:*** Once per scene, **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have each ally within Close range of the Alpha clear a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Berserker Rage - Reaction:*** The first time in a scene the Alpha marks their last Stress, they gain a +6 bonus to damage rolls.
