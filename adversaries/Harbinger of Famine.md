# Harbinger of Famine

***Tier 4 [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A dried-out husk of a body riding atop a withered white horse. They are covered in mouths and surrounded by swarming flies.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Drain, impoverish, weaken, wither

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 26/42 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Barbed Lash:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunger +2, Thirst +2

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Harbinger can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Mount - Passive:*** While the Harbinger is on their mount, they gain a +2 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). When the Harbinger takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they’re knocked from their mount. If the Harbinger is unhorsed, their mount disappears until you **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon it again.

***Hunger Pangs - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Decreasing 6).* When the Harbinger appears, activate the countdown. It ticks down when the Harbinger is spotlighted. When it triggers, all PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range mark a Stress and become *Ravenous*. While *Ravenous*, a PC can’t spend Hope until they clear the condition with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.

***Drain Essence - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to choose up to three PCs. Each target takes **4d6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Too Many Mouths - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to attack a PC within Melee range. On a success, the Harbinger grabs the target and bites them, dealing **4d6** physical damage and *Restraining* the target until they break free with a successful Strength Roll.

***Withering Touch - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Harbinger caress the face of a *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* PC within Melee range. The target marks 2 Stress and either loses a Hope or marks **1d4** HP.
