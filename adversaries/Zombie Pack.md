# Zombie Pack

***Tier 1 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (2/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A group of shambling corpses instinctively moving together.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Consume flesh, hunger, maul

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 8 | **Thresholds:** 6/12 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d4+2) - Passive:*** When the Zombies have marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **1d4+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Overwhelm - Reaction:*** When the Zombies mark HP from an attack within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against the attacker.
