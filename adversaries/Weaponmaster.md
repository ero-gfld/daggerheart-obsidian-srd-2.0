# Weaponmaster

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A master-at-arms wielding a sword twice their size.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Act first, aim for the weakest, intimidate

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Claymore:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Goading Strike - Action:*** Make a standard attack against a target. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to *Taunt* the target until their next successful attack. The next time the *Taunted* target attacks, they have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against targets other than the Weaponmaster.

***Adrenaline Burst - Action:*** Once per scene, **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to clear 2 [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Weaponmaster makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
