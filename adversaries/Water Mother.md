# Water Mother

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A colossal fur-covered snake that hunts in large bodies of water.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Constrict the world, control the battlefield, draw enemies into the water

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 40/70 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Rivers +4

## FEATURES

***Segmented - Passive:*** The Water Mother’s body is divided into three segments: Head, Center, and Tail. The Water Mother always moves from the Head first, pulling the Center and then Tail behind it. A creature can target the Water Mother with an attack as long as at least one of her segments is within range, but the Water Mother can make a standard attack only against a target within range of her Head. If an attack deals damage to the Water Mother and her Head is within range, the attack deals an extra **1d10** damage.

***Eat the World - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Water Mother inhale sharply. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of her Head must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and be pulled within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Head. The Water Mother then makes a standard attack against all targets within Melee range of her Head.

***Constrict - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Center segment. Targets the attack succeeds against take **4d8+8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are temporarily *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. At the beginning of each [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md), you can **mark a Stress** to force all targets *Restrained* by the Water Mother to mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Venom Surge - Reaction:*** When the Water Mother makes a successful standard attack, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make the target *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the end of the scene.
