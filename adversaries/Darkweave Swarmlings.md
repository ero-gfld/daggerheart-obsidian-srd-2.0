# Darkweave Swarmlings

***Tier 1 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (8/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A teeming swarm of tiny spiders woven out of shadow-silk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Burst out of holes, climb up legs, crawl into ears, scuttle under clothes

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 9 | **Thresholds:** 4/8 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Nibble:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Climb +2

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d4) - Passive:*** When the Swarmlings have marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **1d4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***“Get ’em Off, Get ’em Off!” - Reaction:*** When an attack from the Swarmlings causes a target to mark HP, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make the target temporarily *Covered in Spiders.* While *Covered* *in Spiders,* the target must roll a **d6** when they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). On a result of 4 or higher, they must mark a Stress or you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
