# Redcap Butcher

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A halfling-sized fey creature who hacks their victims apart with a variety of cutting implements. They wear a conical red hat sealed on their head with candle wax.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Grin disconcertingly, hack and slash, scramble

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/10 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Meat Cleaver:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Scurry +2

## FEATURES

***Chop Happy - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against up to three targets. For each target who marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Knife Thrower - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. If the Butcher is *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS),* they make the attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).
