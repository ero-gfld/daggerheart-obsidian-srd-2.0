# Battle Box

***Tier 2 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cube-shaped construct with a different rune on each of their six sides.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Change tactics, trample foes, wait in disguise

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10/20 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Slam:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +2

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Box can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Randomized Tactics - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** and roll a **d6**. The Box uses the corresponding move: 
1. ***Mana Beam.*** The Box fires a searing beam. Make an attack against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **2d10+2** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). 
2. ***Fire Jets.*** The Box shoots into the air, spinning and releasing jets of flame. Make an attack against all targets within Close range. Targets the Box succeeds against take **2d8** physical damage. 
3. ***Trample.*** The Box rockets around erratically. Make an attack against all PCs within Close range. Targets the Box succeeds against take **1d6+5** physical damage and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until their next roll with Hope. 
4. ***Shocking Gas.*** The Box sprays out a silver gas sparking with lightning. All targets within Close range must succeed on a [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark 3 Stress. 
5. ***Stunning Clap.*** The Box leaps and their sides clap, creating a small sonic boom. All targets within Very Close range must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or become *Vulnerable* until the cube is defeated. 
6. ***Psionic Whine.*** The Box releases a cluster of mechanical bees whose buzz rattles mortal minds. All targets within Close range must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll or take **2d4+9** direct magic damage.

***Overcharge - Reaction:*** Before rolling damage for the Box’s attack, you can **mark a Stress** to add a **d6** to the damage roll. Additionally, you gain a Fear.

***Death Quake - Reaction:*** When the Box marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), the magic powering them ruptures in an explosion of force. All targets within Close range must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll or take **2d8+1** magic damage.
