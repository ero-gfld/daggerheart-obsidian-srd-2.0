# Chimera

***Tier 3 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A giant fire-breathing monster that has a lion’s head, a goat’s body, and a serpent’s tail.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Play with food, prowl territory, slay cattle

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/40 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +5 | **Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d12 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Predator +3

## FEATURES

***Rending Jaws - Passive:*** When the Chimera makes a successful attack against a PC, the target must mark 2 [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) to reduce the severity by one threshold.

***Double Claw - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.

***Breath of Fire - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to target up to three PCs in front of the Chimera within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each target must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (20). Targets who fail take **3d12** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed must choose to mark either an Armor Slot or a Stress.

***Serpent Strike - Reaction:*** When a PC within Close range targets the Chimera with an attack, you can **mark a Stress** to attack the PC first. On a success, deal **4d4+10** physical damage and *Poison* them until their next rest or they succeed on a Knowledge Roll (20). While *Poisoned,* the target must roll a **d6** before they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). On a result of 3 or lower, they must mark a Stress.
