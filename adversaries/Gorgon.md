# Gorgon

***Tier 2 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A snake-headed, scaled humanoid with a gilded bow, enraged that their peace has been disturbed.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Corner, hit-and-run, petrify, seek vengeance

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/25 | **HP:** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Sunsear Shortbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d20+3 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Stealth +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Gorgon can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Sunsear Arrows - Passive:*** When the Gorgon makes a successful standard attack, the target *Glows* until the end of the scene and can’t become *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. [Attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) made against a *Glowing* target have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).

***Crown of Serpents - Action:*** Make an attack roll against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range using the Gorgon’s protective snakes. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **2d10+4** physical damage and the target must mark a Stress.

***Petrifying Gaze - Reaction:*** When the Gorgon takes damage from an attack within Close range, you can **spend a Fear** to force the attacker to make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, they begin to turn to stone, marking a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and starting a Petrification [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (4). This countdown ticks down when the Gorgon is attacked. When it triggers, the target must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH). If the Gorgon is defeated, all petrification countdowns end.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Gorgon makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
