# Masked Thief

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cunning thief with acrobatic skill and a flair for the dramatic.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Evade, hide, pilfer, profit

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/17 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Backsword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Acrobatics +3

## FEATURES

***Quick Hands - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **1d8+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and the Thief steals one item or consumable from the target’s inventory.

***Escape Plan - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to reveal a snare trap set anywhere on the battlefield by the Thief. All targets within Very Close range of the trap must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13) or be pulled off their feet and suspended upside down. A target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Vulnerable* until they break free, ending both conditions, with a successful Finesse or Strength Roll (13).
