# Gargantuan Sea Turtle

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A monstrous sea turtle that has an adamantine shell and beak.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Acquire riches, capsize trading vessels, lie in wait, use the waves as cover

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 21/41 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Crushing Jaws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d12+7 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Open Sea +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Turtle can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***Piercing Bite - Passive:*** When the Turtle makes a successful standard attack against a PC, the PC must either mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without receiving its benefit or take double damage.

***Tail Bash - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all PCs behind the Turtle within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, a target takes an extra **1d12** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and is pushed away from the Turtle to Close range.

***Gigaton Splash - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Turtle use its weight to unleash an unstoppable wall of water that targets all creatures within Far range. Each PC must make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) to navigate the tide. Targets who fail are pushed away from the Turtle to Close range, take **4d8** physical damage, and become temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. Targets who succeed must mark a Stress. For each target who failed, you gain a Fear.

***Steam Breath - Action:*** **Spend 2 Fear** to have the Turtle release a blast of superheated steam targeting all PCs in front of it within Close range. Each target must make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who fail take **4d8** magic damage and become temporarily *Vulnerable.* Targets who succeed must choose to either mark a Stress or take half damage.
