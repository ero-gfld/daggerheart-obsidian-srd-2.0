# Sylvan Soldier

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A faerie warrior adorned in armor made of leaves and bark.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, hide, overwhelm, protect, trail

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 11 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/11 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Scythe:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Tracker +2

## FEATURES

***Pack Tactics - Passive:*** If the Soldier makes a standard attack and another Sylvan Soldier is within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target, deal **1d8+5** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead of their standard damage.

***Forest Control - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to pull down a tree within Close range. A creature hit by the tree must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15) or take **1d10** physical damage.

***Blend In - Reaction:*** When the Soldier makes a successful attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to become *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the Soldier’s next attack or a PC succeeds on an Instinct Roll (14) to find them.
