# Tangle Bramble Swarm

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (3/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A cluster of animate, blood-drinking tumbleweeds, each the size of a large gourd.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Digest, entangle, immobilize

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/11 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Thorns:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +2

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d4+2) - Passive:*** When the Swarm has marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **1d4+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Crush - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **2d6+8** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to a target with 3 or more bramble tokens.

***Encumber - Reaction:*** When the Swarm succeeds on an attack, give the target a bramble token. If a target has any bramble tokens, they are *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. If a target has 3 or more bramble tokens, they are also *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. All bramble tokens can be removed by succeeding on a [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (12 + the number of bramble tokens) or dealing [Major](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or greater damage to the Swarm. If bramble tokens are removed from a target using a Finesse Roll, a number of [Tangle Bramble](Tangle%20Bramble.md) [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) spawn within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range equal to the number of tokens removed.
