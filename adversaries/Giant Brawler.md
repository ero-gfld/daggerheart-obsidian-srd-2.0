# Giant Brawler

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An especially muscular giant wielding a warhammer larger than a human.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Make a living, overwhelm, slam, topple

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Warhammer:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intrusion +2

## FEATURES

***Battering Ram - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Brawler charge at an inanimate object within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range they could feasibly smash (such as a wall, cart, or market stand) and destroy it. All targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the object must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **2d4+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from the shrapnel.

***Bloody Reprisal - Reaction:*** When the Brawler marks 2 or more [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you can make a standard attack against the attacker. On a success, the Brawler deals **2d6+15** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead of their standard damage.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Brawler makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
