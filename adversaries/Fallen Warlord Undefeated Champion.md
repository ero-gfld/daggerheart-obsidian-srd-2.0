# Fallen Warlord: Undefeated Champion

**_Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)._** _That which only the most feared have a chance to fear._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Dispatch merciless death, punish the defiant, secure victory at any cost
- **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/58 | **HP:** 11 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5
- **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Heart-Shattering Sword:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+13 phy
- **[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Conquest +3, History +2, Intimidation +3

### FEATURES

**_Relentless (3) - Passive:_** The [Undefeated Champion](Undefeated%20Champion.md) can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

**_Faltering Armor - Passive:_** When the Undefeated Champion takes damage, reduce it by **1d10**.

**_Shattering Strike - Action:_** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. PCs the Champion succeeds against lose a number of Hope equal to the [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) they marked from this attack.

**_Endless Legions - Action:_** **Spend a Fear** to summon a number of [Fallen Shock Troops](Fallen%20Shock%20Troop.md) equal to twice the number of PCs. The Shock Troops appear at Far range.

**_Circle of Defilement - Reaction: Countdown (1d8):_** When the Undefeated Champion is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS). When it triggers, activate a magical circle covering an area within Far range of the Champion. A target within that area is _Vulnerable_ until they leave the circle. The circle can be removed by dealing Severe damage to the Undefeated Champion.

**_Momentum - Reaction:_** When the Undefeated Champion makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

**_Doombringer - Reaction:_** When a target marks HP from an attack by the Undefeated Champion, all PCs within Far range of the target lose a Hope.
