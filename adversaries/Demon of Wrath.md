# Demon of Wrath

***Tier 3 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A hulking demon with boulder-sized fists, driven by endless rage.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Fuel anger, impress rivals, wreak havoc

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/40 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Fists:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+1 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intimidation +2

## FEATURES

***Anger Unrelenting - Passive:*** The Demon’s attacks deal [direct damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Battle Lust - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to boil the blood of all PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. They use a d20 as their [Fear Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) until the end of the scene.

***Retaliation - Reaction:*** When the Demon takes damage from an attack within Close range, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against the attacker.

***Blood and Souls - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 6)*. Activate the first time an attack is made within sight of the Demon. It ticks down when a PC takes a violent action. When it triggers, summon **1d4** [Minor Demons](Minor%20Demon.md), who appear at Close range.
