# Jagged Knife Kneebreaker

***Tier 1 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An imposing brawler carrying a large club.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Grapple, intimidate, profit, steal

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/14 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −3 | **Club:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d4+6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Thief +2, Unveiled Threats +3

## FEATURES

***I’ve Got ’Em - Passive:*** Creatures *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* by the Kneebreaker take double damage from attacks by other adversaries.

***Hold Them Down - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes no damage but is *Restrained* and *Vulnerable*. The target can break free, clearing both conditions, with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll or is freed automatically if the Kneebreaker takes Major or greater damage.
