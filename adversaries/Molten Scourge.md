# Molten Scourge

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*Enraged by their wounds, the dragon bursts into molten lava.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Douse with lava, incinerate, repel Invaders, reposition

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 30/58 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +9 | **Lava-Coated Claws:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt from Above +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Molten Scourge can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Cracked Scales - Passive:*** When the Molten Scourge takes damage, roll a number of **d6s** equal to [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) marked. For each result of 4 or higher, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Shattering Might - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes **4d8+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), loses a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), and is knocked back to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The Molten Scourge clears a Stress.

***Eruption - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to erupt lava from beneath the Molten Scourge’s scales, filling the area within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with molten lava. All targets in that area must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d6+6** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and be knocked back to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. This area remains lava. When a creature other than the Molten Scourge enters that area or acts while inside of it, they must mark 6 [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Volcanic Breath - Reaction:*** When the Molten Scourge takes [Major damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), roll a **d10**. On a result of 8 or higher, the Molten Scourge breathes a flow of lava in front of them within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. All targets in that area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d10+4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), mark **1d4 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)**, and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a Stress. Targets who succeed take half damage and must mark a Stress.

***Lava Splash - Reaction:*** When the Molten Scourge takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, molten blood gushes from the wound and deals **2d10+4** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to the attacker.

***Ashen Vengeance (Phase Change) - Reaction:*** When the Molten Scourge marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), replace them with the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md) and immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.
