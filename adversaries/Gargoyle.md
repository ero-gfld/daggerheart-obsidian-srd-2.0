# Gargoyle

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A magically animated statue resembling a terrifying beast or demon.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Hide in plain sight, overwatch, swoop down

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Stone Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +4, Ruins +3

## FEATURES

***Unliving - Passive:*** The Gargoyle has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to magic damage.

***Swooping Strike - Action:*** Choose a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The Gargoyle moves to that point and makes a standard attack against one target along its path.

***Petrifying Slash - Reaction:*** When the Gargoyle deals damage, the target must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. If the target is already *Vulnerable,* they become *Restrained*. If the target is already *Vulnerable* and *Restrained,* they must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH). The PC can spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) per condition to clear it.
