# Cave Ogre

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A massive humanoid who sees all sapient life as food.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bite off heads, feast, rip limbs, stomp, throw enemies

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Club:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Throw +2

## FEATURES

***Ramp Up - Passive:*** You must **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Ogre. While spotlighted, they can make their standard attack against all targets within range.

***Bone Breaker - Passive:*** The Ogre’s attacks deal [direct damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Hail of Boulders - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to pick up heavy objects and throw them at all targets in front of the Ogre within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Make an attack against these targets. Targets the Ogre succeeds against take **1d10+2** physical damage. If they succeed against more than one target, you gain a Fear.

***Rampaging Fury - Reaction:*** When the Ogre marks 2 or more [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they can rampage. Move the Ogre to a point within Close range and deal **2d6+3** direct physical damage to all targets in their path.
