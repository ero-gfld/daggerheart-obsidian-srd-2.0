# Cephilith Novitiate

***Tier 4 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A luminary of the Mortal Realm who has given their mind to extradimensional entities in exchange for eldritch abilities.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Help others see the Truth, make way for It, seek forbidden knowledge

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Eldritch Might:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 12 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Visions +2

## FEATURES

***Minion (12) - Passive:*** The Novitiate is defeated when they take any damage. For every 12 damage a PC deals to the Novitiate, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Cephilith Novitiates within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 12 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

***Sacrifice Self - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Novitiate charge at a target within Far range and melt into them. Deal 12 direct magic damage to the target and defeat the Novitiate.
