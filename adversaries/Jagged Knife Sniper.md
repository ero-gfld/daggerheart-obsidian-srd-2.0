**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, hide, profit, reposition

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/7 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Shortbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Stealth +2

## FEATURES

***Unseen Strike - Passive:*** If the Sniper is *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* when they make a successful standard attack against a target, they deal **1d10+4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead of their standard damage.
