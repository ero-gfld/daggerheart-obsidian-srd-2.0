# Archer Squadron

***Tier 2 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (2/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A group of trained archers bearing massive bows.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Stick together, survive, volley fire

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **Thresholds:** 8/16 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Longbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+3 phy

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d6+3) - Passive:*** When the Squadron has marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **1d6+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Focused Volley - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to target a point within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Make an attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against all targets within Close range of that point. Targets the Squadron succeeds against take **1d10+4** physical damage.

***Suppressing Fire - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to target a point within Far range. Until the next roll with Fear, a creature who moves within Close range of that point must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, they take **2d6+3** physical damage. On a success, they take half damage.
