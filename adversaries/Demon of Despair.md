# Demon of Despair

***Tier 3 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cloaked demon-creature with long limbs, seeping shadows.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Make fear contagious, stick to the shadows, undermine resolve

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 18/35 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Miasma Bolt:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d6+1 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Manipulation +3

## FEATURES

***Depths of Despair - Passive:*** The Demon deals double damage to PCs with 0 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Your Struggle Is Pointless - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to weigh down the spirits of all PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. All targets affected replace their [Hope Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) with a **d8** until they roll a success with Hope or their next rest.

***Your Friends Will Fail You - Reaction:*** When a PC fails with Fear, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to cause all other PCs within Close range to lose a Hope.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Demon makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
