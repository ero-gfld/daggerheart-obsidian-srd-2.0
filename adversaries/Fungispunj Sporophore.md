# Fungispunj Sporophore

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A giant mushroom creature that can command the dead.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Awaken the fallen, defend the colony, spread spores

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12/24 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Spongy Fist:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Commander +5, Guardian +4

## FEATURES

***Neurotoxic Spore Cloud - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Sporophore spray a cloud of spores on all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must succeed on a [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *Poisoned* until they spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to clear the condition. While *Poisoned*, a target must roll a **d6** before they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). On a result of 4 or lower, they must mark a Stress.

***Reanimator - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Sporophore spray a corpse within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with a cloud of psychic spores. The corpse clears all [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), then reanimates under the psychic control of the Sporophore. When the Sporophore is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT), you can also [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all corpses it controls.

***Colony - Action:*** **Mark any number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Sporophore summon an equal number of [Fungispunj Sporelings](Fungispunj%20Sporeling.md) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Mind Meld - Reaction:*** All Fungispunj can telepathically communicate with one another. When the Sporophore would be forced to mark [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) from an attack, it can cause Fungispunj allies in the scene to mark that Stress in its place, distributed among allies as it chooses.
