# Sandwyrm

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A gargantuan burrowing worm that has armor plating and gnashing jaws. This intelligent and opportunistic hunter can swim through sand as quickly as a cheetah runs on land.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush from below, eat and leave, tunnel and erupt, use both ends

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 21/40 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Tremor Sense +4

## FEATURES

***Venomous Tail Stinger - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to attack a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes **3d10+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they roll with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Devour - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the Sandwyrm *Swallows* the target. While *Swallowed*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and marks a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) when the Sandwyrm is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). The Sandwyrm disgorges all *Swallowed* creatures when it takes [Major](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or greater damage.

***Hungry, Not Stupid - Reaction:*** When the Sandwyrm marks half its [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or *Swallows* a creature, roll a **d6**. On a result of 3 or lower, the Sandwyrm retreats to safety.
