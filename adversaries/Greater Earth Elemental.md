# Greater Earth Elemental

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A living landslide of boulders and dust, as large as a house.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avalanche, knock over, pummel

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/40 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Boulder Fist:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10+1 phy

## FEATURES

***Slow - Passive:*** When you [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Elemental and they don’t have a token on their stat block, they can’t act yet. Place a token on their stat block and describe what they’re preparing to do. When you spotlight the Elemental and they have a token on their stat block, clear the token and they can act.

***Crushing Blows - Passive:*** When the Elemental makes a successful attack, the target must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without receiving its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage). If they can’t mark an Armor Slot, they must mark an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Immovable Object - Passive:*** An attack that would move the Elemental moves them two fewer ranges (for example, [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) becomes [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT)). When the Elemental takes [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), reduce it by 7.

***Rockslide - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to create a rockslide that buries the land in front of Elemental within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with rockfall. All targets in this area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (19). Targets who fail take **2d12+5** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until their next roll with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Elemental makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
