# Fallen Sorcerer

***Tier 4 [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A powerful mage bound by the bargains they made in life.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Acquire, dishearten, dominate, torment

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 26/42 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Corrupted Staff:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d6+10 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ancient [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +2

## FEATURES

***Conflagration - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to unleash an all-consuming firestorm and make an attack against all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Sorcerer succeeds against take **2d10+6** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Nightmare Tableau - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to trap a target within Far range in a powerful illusion of their worst fears. While trapped, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Vulnerable* until they break free, ending both conditions, with a successful [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.

***Slippery - Reaction:*** When the Sorcerer takes damage from an attack, they can teleport up to Far range.

***Shackles of Guilt - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 2d6)*. When the Sorcerer is in the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) for the first time, activate the countdown. When it triggers, all targets within Far range become *Vulnerable* and must mark a Stress as they relive their greatest regrets. A target can break free from their regret with a successful Presence or Strength Roll. When a PC fails to break free, they lose a Hope.
