# Darkweave Spinner

**_Tier 1 Standard._** _A boar-sized arachnid woven from primordial shadow-silk._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Bite, stick to the darkness
- **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 6/9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
- **ATK:** +1 | **Fangs:** Melee | 1d8+1 phy
- **Experience:** Holes +2, Webs +2

### FEATURES

**_Wrap in Shadow-Silk - Action:_** **Mark a Stress** to make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the target is _Vulnerable_ and _Restrained_ until they succeed on a Strength Roll (10).

**_Shadow Fang - Reaction:_** When the Spinner makes a successful attack against a target within Melee range, you can **spend a Fear** to make the target _Shaky_ until they succeed on an Instinct Roll (10). While _Shaky_, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls.
