# Darkweave Spinner

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A boar-sized arachnid woven from primordial shadow-silk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bite, stick to the darkness

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/9 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Fangs:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Holes +2, Webs +2

## FEATURES

***Wrap in Shadow-Silk - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Restrained* until they succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (10).

***Shadow Fang - Reaction:*** When the Spinner makes a successful attack against a target within Melee range, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make the target *Shaky* until they succeed on an Instinct Roll (10). While *Shaky*, the target has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
