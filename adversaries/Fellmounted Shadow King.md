# Fellmounted Shadow King

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The shade of a forgotten ruler, cursed to patrol the skies atop a winged eel in the service of an even greater evil.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Find magic items, inspire fear, patrol skies

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 24/38 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Cursed Lance:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10+6 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Seeker +3

## FEATURES

***Fellmount - Passive:*** While the Shadow King is on his mount, he gains a +2 bonus to his [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). When the Shadow King takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), he’s knocked from his mount. If the Shadow King is unmounted, his mount disappears until you **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon it again.

***Light and Shadow - Passive:*** The Shadow King has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) while in shadow or darkness and [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attack rolls while in bright light.

***Relic Hunter - Passive:*** The Shadow King has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who have magic weapons or armor equipped.

***Hellsong - Action:*** **Spend 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Shadow King’s mount unleash a spine-chilling screech that echoes over the battlefield. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (18). Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). Targets who fail must mark a Stress and lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If a PC can’t lose a Hope, you gain 2 Fear.

***Air Support - Reaction:*** Three times per scene, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have an ally within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range reroll a failed attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).
