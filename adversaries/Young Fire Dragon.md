# Young Fire Dragon

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A crimson dragon with four powerful limbs and smoldering wings.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Burn, defend lair, erupt, fly, shred, take

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 21/34 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Bite & Claws:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Burning Rage +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Dragon can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***Intimidating - Passive:*** When you spend a Fear to spotlight the Dragon, all PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or lose a Hope.

***Flame Breath - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Dragon unleash a blast of flame against all targets in front of it within Close range. Each target must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **4d8+3** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed must mark a Stress or take half damage. A target who marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from this attack also becomes *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a HP.

***Eruption - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Dragon crack the earth open with its tail, unleashing an explosion of lava. Choose a point within Far range. Each PC on the line between the Dragon and the chosen point must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll or get burned, becoming *Vulnerable* until their next rest. You gain a Fear for each PC who fails.

***Blazing Scales - Reaction:*** When a creature within Very Close range attacks the Dragon, the attacker must mark a Stress.

***Slash and Burn - Reaction:*** When the Dragon makes a successful standard attack, you gain a Fear and can **mark a Stress** to have the attack deal an extra 10 magic damage.
