# Guahalan Alebrujo

***Tier 2 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A huge, colorful, chimeric beast formed from many animals.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Destroy, give no quarter, ravage

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Tooth & Claw:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+4 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Brutal +2, Quick +2

## FEATURES

***The Beast Unleashed - Passive:*** The Alebrujo gains a bonus to [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal to the number of [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) you have.

***Guahalan Sacrifice - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Alebrujo devour a Minion within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, then clear a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Howl of the Guahala - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to have the Alebrujo emit a howl. Each PC within Far range must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become temporarily *Panicked*. While *Panicked*, a PC must mark a Stress when they take the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Rip & Tear - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to make a standard attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE). On a success, you can **mark a Stress** to make another standard attack against an additional target within range. You can continue marking Stress to make additional attacks until the Alebrujo fails an attack roll.

***Brutal Spectacle - Reaction:*** When the Alebrujo deals Severe damage, you can **spend a Fear** to force all targets within Close range to mark a Stress.
