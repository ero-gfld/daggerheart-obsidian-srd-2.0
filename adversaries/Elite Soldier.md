# Elite Soldier

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An armored squire or experienced commoner looking to advance.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Gain glory, keep order, make alliances

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Spear:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

## FEATURES

***Reinforce - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of an ally and make a standard attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal **2d10+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and the ally can clear a Stress.

***Vassal’s Loyalty - Reaction:*** When the Soldier is within Very Close range of a knight or other noble who would take damage, you can **mark a Stress** to move into Melee range of them and take the damage instead.
