# Ahuizotl

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A doglike ambush predator that has a clawed hand on the end of a long prehensile tail and drags victims into rivers to drown them.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, drown, restrain

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/9 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Stealth +2, Swimming +2

## FEATURES

***Aquatic Attacker - Passive:*** When the Ahuizotl attacks from the water, it has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the attack and deals an extra **1d6** damage.

***Tail Swat - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **1d8+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Drag and Bag - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Ahuizotl grab a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with its tail, pull the target into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, and temporarily *[Restrain](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* them. The Ahuizotl has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attacks against targets *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* in this way.
