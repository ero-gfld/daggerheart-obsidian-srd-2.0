# Storm Titan

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An ancient, wise, and temperamental goliath who has hair made of mist and electricity crackling across their skin.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Erupt violently, wait, watch

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **HP:** 11 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +7 | **Lightning Bolt:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d20+10 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Power of the Storm +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Titan can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Momentum - Passive:*** When the Titan makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

***Ball Lightning - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Titan conjure **1d4** spheres of electrical energy within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. When the Titan is spotlighted, they can move each sphere up to Close range. When a sphere moves within Very Close range of a PC, the PC takes **4d6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), and the sphere disappears. Each sphere has the same [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) as the Titan and disappears if a creature deals magic damage to it.

***Storm Bringer - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Titan conjure a raging storm. Each PC within Close range of the Titan must make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **3d12** magic damage and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they are spotlighted. Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Thunderclap - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to target up to three PCs in front of the Titan within Close range. Each target marks a Stress and becomes *Vulnerable* until they roll with Hope.
