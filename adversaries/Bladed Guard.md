# Bladed Guard

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An armored guard bearing a sword and shield painted in the settlement’s colors.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Arrest, close gates, make it through the day, pin down

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/9 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Longsword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Local [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +3

## FEATURES

***Shield Wall - Passive:*** A creature who tries to move within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Guard must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. If additional Bladed Guards are standing in a line alongside the first, and each is within Melee range of another guard in the line, the [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) increases by the total number of guards in that line.

***Detain - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to *Restrain* the target until they break free with a successful attack, Finesse Roll, or Strength Roll.
