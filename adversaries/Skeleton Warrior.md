# Skeleton Warrior

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A dirt-covered skeleton armed with a rusted blade.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Feign death, gang up, steal skin

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Sword:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+2 phy

## FEATURES

***Only Bones - Passive:*** The Warrior is resistant to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Won’t Stay Dead - Reaction:*** When the Warrior is defeated, you can [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them and roll a **d6**. On a result of 6, if there are other adversaries on the battlefield, the Warrior re-forms with no marked [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
