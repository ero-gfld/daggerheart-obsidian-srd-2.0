# Giant Octopus

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A monstrous, aquatic mollusk that has eight sucker-bearing arms and a hard, beaklike jaw.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Crush and bite, grapple, harry sailing vessels, hunt prey

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Arm Whip:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intelligent +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Octopus can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it.

***Armed and Dangerous - Passive:*** When the Octopus first appears, place a **d8** on this stat block with the 8 value facing up. When the Octopus makes a successful standard attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you can add the die’s value to the damage dealt. After you add its value to a roll, decrease the die’s value by 1.

***Grapple - Action:*** When the Octopus succeeds on a standard attack, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Octopus *Grapple* the target. While *Grappled*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they break free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.

***Crush - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **3d8** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to a target *Grappled* by the Octopus.
