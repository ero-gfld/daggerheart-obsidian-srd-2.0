# Hill Titan

***Tier 2 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A goliath clad in animal skins and surrounded by a swarm of insects.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Eat, make a mess, patrol stomping grounds

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Tree Stump:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Survival +3, Vengeful +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Titan can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Momentum - Passive:*** When the Titan makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

***Gullible - Passive:*** PCs have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to trick or emotionally manipulate the Titan.

***Bug Swarm - Passive:*** PCs are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* while within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Mighty Swing - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to make a standard attack against all targets within Very Close range.
