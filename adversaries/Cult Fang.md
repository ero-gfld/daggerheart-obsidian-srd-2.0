# Cult Fang

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A professional killer-turned-cultist.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Capture sacrifices, isolate prey, rise in the ranks

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/17 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Long Knife:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

## FEATURES

***Shadow’s Embrace - Passive:*** The Fang can climb and walk on vertical surfaces. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move from one shadow to another within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Pick Off the Straggler - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to cause a target within Melee range to make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, the target must mark 2 Stress and is teleported with the Fang to a shadow within Far range, making them temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*. On a success, the target must mark a Stress.
