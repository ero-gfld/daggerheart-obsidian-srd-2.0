# Severed Shadow

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A two-dimensional living shadow bound to flat planes and surfaces.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Curse the light, slip between the cracks, split and reform

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Strangle:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 10 mag

## FEATURES

***Minion (12) - Passive:*** The Shadow is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 12 damage a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) deals to the Shadow, the PC defeats an additional [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within range that the attack would succeed against.

***Shadow Grapple - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target becomes *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and must mark a Stress when they take an action until the Shadow is defeated.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Severed Shadows within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those [Minions](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 10 [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) each. Combine this damage.
