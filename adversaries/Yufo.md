# Yufo

***Tier 1 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large, interdimensional jellyfishlike creature that floats in and out of the timestream.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Exchange information about possible timelines, feed on heightened emotions, watch from the shadows

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/10 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −3 | **Tentacle Lash:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Secrets +3, Floating Oddity +3

## FEATURES

***Telepathic - Passive:*** The Yufo can mentally communicate with creatures within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Psychic Strongbox - Passive:*** The Yufo is [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to abilities and features that would affect or alter its thoughts or emotions.

***Glitch Wave - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and choose up to three targets within Close range. Each target must succeed on a [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become desynced from reality and have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on Instinct Rolls until they use a [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) to ground their psyches in the now.

***Temporal Corrosion - Reaction:*** When the Yufo succeeds on a standard attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Yufo excrete an entropic acid through its skin, giving the target a −2 penalty to their [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) until they use a downtime move to repair their armor.
