# Head Vampire

***Tier 3 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A captivating undead dressed in aristocratic finery.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Create thralls, charm, command, fly, intimidate

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/42 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +5 | **Rapier:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d20+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Aristocrat +3

## FEATURES

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Vampire makes a successful attack, all PCs within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a Fear.

***Look into My Eyes - Passive:*** A creature who moves into Melee range of the Vampire must make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, you gain **1d4** Fear.

***Feed on Followers - Action:*** When the Vampire is within Melee range of an ally, they can cause the ally to mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). The Vampire then clears a HP.

***The Hunt Is On - Action:*** **Spend 2 Fear** to summon **1d4** Vampires, who appear at Far range and immediately take the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Lifesuck - Reaction:*** When the Vampire is spotlighted, roll a **d8**. On a result of 6 or higher, all targets within Very Close range must mark a HP.
