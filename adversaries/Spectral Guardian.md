# Spectral Guardian

***Tier 2 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A ghostly fighter with spears and swords, anchored by duty.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Move through solid objects, protect treasure, rehash old battles

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/15 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Spear:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ancient [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +2

## FEATURES

***Ghost - Passive:*** The Guardian has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects.

***Grave Blade - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal **2d10+6** physical damage and the target must mark a Stress.
