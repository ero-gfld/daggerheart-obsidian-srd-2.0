# Undefeated Champion

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*That which only the most feared have a chance to fear.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Dispatch merciless death, punish the defiant, secure victory at any cost

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/58 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 11 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Heart-Shattering Sword:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+13 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Conquest +3, History +2, Intimidation +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Undefeated Champion can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Faltering Armor - Passive:*** When the Undefeated Champion takes damage, reduce it by **1d10**.

***Shattering Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) the Champion succeeds against lose a number of [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) equal to the [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) they marked from this attack.

***Endless Legions - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon a number of [Fallen Shock Troops](Fallen%20Shock%20Troop.md) equal to twice the number of [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). The Shock Troops appear at [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Circle of Defilement - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (1d8)*. When the Undefeated Champion is in the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) for the first time, activate the countdown. When it triggers, activate a magical circle covering an area within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Champion. A target within that area is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they leave the circle. The circle can be removed by dealing [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) to the Undefeated Champion.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Undefeated Champion makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Doombringer - Reaction:*** When a target marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack by the Undefeated Champion, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
