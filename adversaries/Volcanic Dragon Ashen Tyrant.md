# Volcanic Dragon: Ashen Tyrant

**_[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)._** _No enemy has ever had the insolence to wound the dragon so. As the lava settles, it's ground to ash like the dragon's past foes._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Choke, fly, intimidate, kill or be killed
- **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 29/55 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5
- **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +10 | **Claws and Teeth:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+15 phy
- **[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt from Above +5

### FEATURES

**_Relentless (4) - Passive:_** The [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md) can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to four times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

**_Cornered - Passive:_** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** instead of spending a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md).

**_Injured Wings - Passive:_** While flying, the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md) gains a +1 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

**_Ashes to Ashes - Passive:_** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) rolls a failure while within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md), they lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If the PC can't lose a Hope, they must mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

**_Desperate Rampage - Action:_** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md) succeeds against take **2d20+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), are knocked back to Close range of where they were, and must mark a Stress.

**_Ashen Cloud - Action:_** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to smash the ground and kick up ash within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. While within the ash cloud, a target has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). The ash cloud clears the next time an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

**_Apocalyptic Thrashing - Action: Countdown (1d12):_** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to activate. It ticks down when a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) rolls with Fear. When it triggers, the [Ashen Tyrant](Ashen%20Tyrant.md) thrashes about, causing environmental damage (such as an earthquake, avalanche, or collapsing walls). All targets within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d10+10** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are _[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)_ by the rubble until they break free with a successful Strength Roll. Targets who succeed take half damage. If the Ashen Tyrant is defeated while this [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) is active, trigger the countdown immediately as the destruction caused by their death throes.
