# Juvenile Flickerfly

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A horse-sized insect with iridescent scales and crystalline wings moving faster than the eye can see.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Collect shiny things, hunt, swoop

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Wing Slash:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+4 phy

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Flickerfly can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Peerless Accuracy - Passive:*** Before the Flickerfly makes an attack, roll a **d6**. On a result of 4 or higher, the target’s [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) is halved against this attack.

***Mind Dance - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to create a magically dazzling display that grapples the minds of nearby foes. All targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). For each target who failed, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and the Flickerfly learns one of the target’s fears.

***Hallucinatory Breath - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (Loop 1d6)*. When the Flickerfly takes damage for the first time, activate the countdown. When it triggers, the Flickerfly breathes hallucinatory gas on all targets in front of them up to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or be tormented by fearful hallucinations. Targets whose fears are known to the Flickerfly have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on this roll. Targets who fail must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
