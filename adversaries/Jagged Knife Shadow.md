# Jagged Knife Shadow

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A nimble scoundrel bearing a wicked knife and utilizing shadow magic to isolate targets.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, conceal, divide, profit

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/8 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Daggers:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d4+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intrusion +3

## FEATURES

***Backstab - Passive:*** When the Shadow succeeds on a standard attack that has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE), they deal **1d6+6** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead of their standard damage.

***Cloaked - Action:*** Become *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until after the Shadow’s next attack. Attacks made while *Hidden* from this feature have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).
