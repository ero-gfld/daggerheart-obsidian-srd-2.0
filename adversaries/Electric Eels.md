# Electric Eels

***Tier 2 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (2/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A swarm of eels that encircle and electrocute.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid larger predators, shock prey, tear apart

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Shocking Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+4 phy

## FEATURES

***Horde (2d4+1) - Passive:*** When the Eels have marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **2d4+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Paralyzing Shock - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. You gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) for each target that marks HP.
