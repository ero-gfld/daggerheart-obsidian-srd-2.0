# Entombed Necropriest

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Support](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A necromancer charged with preserving the empress’s life beyond death.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Boost allies, conquer time, hold down enemies, serve the empress, wield death as a weapon

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/16 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 7  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Death Bolt:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+2 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Dark Magic +4, Protect +2

## FEATURES

***Invigorate - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to choose up to three allies within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each one gains [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on their next [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Last Grasp - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Skeletal hands burst out of the ground at the PC’s feet and *[Restrain](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* them until the PC breaks free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. When a target *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* in this way spends [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you gain a Fear.

***Chill Touch - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes standard damage and must mark **1d4** Stress. If the target marks their last Stress from this attack, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
