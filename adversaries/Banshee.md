# Banshee

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A corrupted fey spirit that haunts the night in the form of a willowy specter wearing a luminous gossamer gown.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush from inside solid objects, frighten to death, mourn life and beauty lost, scream balefully

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Fingernails:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+1 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Vain +3, Wrathful +3

## FEATURES

***Specter - Passive:*** The Banshee has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects.

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Banshee makes a successful attack, all PCs within Close range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), and you gain a Fear.

***Scare Tactic - Action:*** Up to three times per scene, **spend a Fear** to have the Banshee teleport up to Very Far range and scare a PC within Melee range. The PC marks **1d4** Stress. If this causes them to mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they also lose a Hope.

***Wail of Despair - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to have the Banshee unleash a mournful scream of pain and anguish. Each PC within Close range must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who succeed take **1d12+1** magic damage. Targets who fail take double damage and are knocked back to Close range.
