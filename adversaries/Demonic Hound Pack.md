# Demonic Hound Pack

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (1/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*Unnatural hounds lit from within by hellfire.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Cause fear, consume flesh, please masters

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 11/23 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Claws and Fangs:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Scent Tracking +3

## FEATURES

***Horde (2d4+1) - Passive:*** When the Pack has marked half or more of their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), their standard attack deals **2d4+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Dreadhowl - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If a target is not able to lose a Hope, they must instead mark 2 Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Pack makes a successful attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
