# Octopus

***Tier 1 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An aquatic, soft-bodied mollusk that has eight sucker-bearing arms and a hard, beaklike jaw.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Crawl along the ocean floor, eat fish, squirt ink to escape

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3/None | **HP:** 2 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Beak:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Dexterity +3, Water +3

## FEATURES

***Grapple - Action:*** Make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target becomes *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Vulnerable* until they escape with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.

***Squirt Ink - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Octopus squirt ink. On land, the ink covers the ground within Very Close range. Each creature who moves through that area must succeed on an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or slip and fall, becoming *Vulnerable* until they exit the area. If this feature is used while the Octopus is underwater, it instead creates a cloud of darkness that fills an area within Very Close range and blocks line of sight.
