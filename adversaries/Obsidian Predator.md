# Obsidian Predator

***Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A massive winged creature with obsidian scales and impossibly sharp claws.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Defend lair, dive-bomb, fly, hunt, intimidate

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 33/65 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Obsidian Claws:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt from Above +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Obsidian Predator can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Flying - Passive:*** While flying, the Obsidian Predator gains a +3 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Obsidian Scales - Passive:*** The Obsidian Predator is resistant to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Avalanche Tail - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Obsidian Predator succeeds against take **4d6+4** physical damage and are knocked back to Far range and *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until their next roll with Hope.

***Dive-Bomb - Action:*** If the Obsidian Predator is flying, **mark a Stress** to choose a point within Far range. Move to that point and make an attack against all targets within Very Close range. Targets the Obsidian Predator succeeds against take **2d10+6** physical damage and must mark a Stress and lose a Hope.

***Erupting Rage (Phase Change) - Reaction:*** When the Obsidian Predator marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), replace them with the [Molten Scourge](Molten%20Scourge.md) and immediately spotlight them.
