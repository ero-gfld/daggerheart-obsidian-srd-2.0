# Merchant

***Tier 1 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A finely dressed trader with a keen eye for financial gain.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Buy low and sell high, create demand, inflate prices, seek profit

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −4 | **Club:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d4+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Shrewd Negotiator +3

## FEATURES

***Preferential Treatment - Passive:*** A PC who succeeds on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll against the Merchant gains a discount on purchases. A PC who fails on a Presence Roll against the Merchant must pay more and has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on future Presence Rolls against the Merchant.

***The Runaround - Passive:*** When a PC rolls a 14 or lower on a Presence Roll made against the Merchant, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
