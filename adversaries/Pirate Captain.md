# Pirate Captain

***Tier 1 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A charismatic sea dog with an impressive hat, eager to raid and plunder.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Command, make ’em walk the plank, plunder, raid

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/14 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Cutlass:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Commander +2, Sailor +3

## FEATURES

***Swashbuckler - Passive:*** When the Captain marks 2 or fewer [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, the attacker must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Reinforcements - Action:*** Once per scene, **mark a Stress** to summon a [Pirate Raiders](Pirate%20Raiders.md) Horde, which appears at Far range.

***No Quarter - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target who has three or more Pirates within Melee range of them. The Captain leads the Pirates in hurling threats and promises of a watery grave. The target must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, the target marks **1d4+1** Stress. On a success, they must mark a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Captain makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
