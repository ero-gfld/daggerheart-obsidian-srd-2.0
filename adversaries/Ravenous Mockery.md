# Ravenous Mockery

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A shapeshifting predator that assumes the appearance of inanimate objects to lure unwary prey into its threat range.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Ambush, lie in wait, swallow without chewing, take on an unassuming shape

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/19 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Gnashing Teeth:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Mimicry +5, Hungry +3

## FEATURES

***Tongue Attack - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to attack a creature within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target is pulled into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with the Mockery and *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they escape with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll, and you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to activate the Mockery’s Devour feature against the target.

***Devour - Action:*** Attack a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the Mockery *Swallows* the target. While *Swallowed*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and marks a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) when the Mockery is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). The Mockery disgorges all *Swallowed* creatures when it takes Major or [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Never Just One - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to reveal that another object on the battlefield has been a Ravenous Mockery the whole time, then immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it.
