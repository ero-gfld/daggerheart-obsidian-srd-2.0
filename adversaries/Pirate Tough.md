# Pirate Tough

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A thickly muscled and tattooed pirate with melon-sized fists.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Plunder, raid, smash, terrorize

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Massive Fists:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Sailor +2

## FEATURES

***Swashbuckler - Passive:*** When the Tough marks 2 or fewer [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, the attacker must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Clear the Decks - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target, dealing **3d4** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and knocking the target back to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.
