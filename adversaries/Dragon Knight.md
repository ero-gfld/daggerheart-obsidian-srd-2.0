# Dragon Knight

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A knight clad in dragonplate armor and a helmet in the shape of a dragon’s head.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Exploit an opening, get some distance, knock enemies off-balance, strike from above

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 15/30 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Lance:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Jousts +2

## FEATURES

***High Ground - Passive:*** The Knight gains [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attacks made against targets below them.

***Leaping Strike - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Knight leap into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a target within Far range and make a standard attack against them. On a success, the attack deals an extra **1d12** damage, and the target must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without receiving its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).

***Comeback - Reaction:*** When the Knight fails a standard attack, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to reroll the attack with advantage.
