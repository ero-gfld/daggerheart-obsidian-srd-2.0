# Cryptimoth

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large red-eyed moth creature that walks upright and portends mysterious or evil events.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Echolocate, fly silently, melt into the shadows

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/30 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Dark Omens +3

## FEATURES

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Cryptimoth makes a successful attack, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Psychic Screech - Action:*** **Spend 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Cryptimoth unleash a burst of psychic energy that causes each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to suffer visions of their horrifying fate. Each target must make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (16). Targets who fail take **3d8** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until their next rest. Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or take half damage.

***Shadow Swarm - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Cryptimoth conjure a swarm of mothlike shadow creatures within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The swarm fills a [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range area and blocks [line of sight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT). Creatures that aren’t Cryptimoths are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* while inside that area and must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to move through it. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) can dispel the swarm with a successful [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (12).

***Paranoia Glare - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Cryptimoth gaze into the eyes of a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and make them *Paranoid*. While *Paranoid*, the PC is suspicious of other PCs and can’t [Help an Ally](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or take part in [Tag Team Rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). To clear this condition, the PC must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (16) or participate in the Prepare [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) with one or more members of their party during a rest.
