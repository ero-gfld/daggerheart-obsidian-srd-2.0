# Head Guard

***Tier 1 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A seasoned guard with a mace, a whistle, and a bellowing voice.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Arrest, close gates, pin down, seek glory

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/13 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Mace:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Commander +2, Local [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +2

## FEATURES

***Rally Guards - Action:*** **Spend 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Head Guard and up to **2d4** allies within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***On My Signal - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (5)*. When the Head Guard is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC makes an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). When it triggers, all [Archer Guards](Archer%20Guard.md) within Far range make a standard attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against the nearest target within their range. If any attacks succeed on the same target, combine their damage.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Head Guard makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
