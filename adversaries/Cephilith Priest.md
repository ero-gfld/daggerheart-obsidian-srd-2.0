# Cephilith Priest

***Tier 4 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A perpetually moist, faceless humanoid from the Outer Realms who has elongated fingers and translucent skin, under which one can see wriggling worms.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Break the unbelievers’ minds, prepare this world for the [Cephilith Titan](Cephilith%20Titan.md)’s arrival, sing its praises

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 37/70 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Psychic Strike:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+10 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Outer Realms +4

## FEATURES

***Psychic Blast - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose up to three PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and force them to make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who succeed take **2d10+5** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who fail take **4d10+10** direct magic damage and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Cerebral Incursion - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to choose a *Vulnerable* target within Melee range. The Priest grabs the target and inserts their elongated fingers into the target’s ears, nose, and mouth. The target must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or mark **1d6** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and the Priest clears an equal number of Stress.

***Telekinetic Grasp - Reaction:*** When the Priest makes a successful standard attack against a PC, you can **mark a Stress** to *Restrain* the target until they spend a Hope.
