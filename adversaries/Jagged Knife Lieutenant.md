# Jagged Knife Lieutenant

***Tier 1 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A seasoned bandit in quality leathers with a strong voice and cunning eyes.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bully, command, profit, reinforce

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/14 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Javelin:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Local [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +2

## FEATURES

***Tactician - Action:*** When you [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Lieutenant, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to also spotlight two allies within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***More Where That Came From - Action:*** Summon three [Jagged Knife Lackeys](Jagged%20Knife%20Lackey.md), who appear at Far range.

***Coup de Grace - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an attack against a *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* target within Close range. On a success, deal **2d6+12** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and the target must mark a Stress.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Lieutenant makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
