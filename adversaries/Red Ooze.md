# Red Ooze

***Tier 1 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A moving mound of translucent flaming red slime.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Camouflage, consume and multiply, ignite, start fires

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/11 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Ooze Appendage:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+3 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +3

## FEATURES

***Creeping Fire - Passive:*** The Ooze can only move within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range as their normal movement. They light any flammable object they touch on fire.

***Ignite - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, the target takes **1d8** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and is *Ignited* until they’re extinguished with a successful [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (14). While *Ignited*, the target takes **1d4** magic damage when they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Split - Reaction:*** When the Ooze has 3 or more [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) marked, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to split them into two [Tiny Red Oozes](Tiny%20Red%20Ooze.md) (with no marked HP or [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)). Immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) both of them.
