# Minor Demon

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A crimson-hued creature from the Circles Below, consumed by rage against all mortals.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Act erratically, corral targets, relish pain, torment

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+6 phy

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Demon can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***All Must Fall - Passive:*** When a PC rolls a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) while within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Demon, they lose a Hope.

***Hellfire - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to rain down hellfire within Far range. All targets within the area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **1d20+3** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage.

***Reaper - Reaction:*** Before rolling damage for the Demon’s attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to the Demon’s current number of marked [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Demon makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
