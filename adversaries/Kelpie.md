# Kelpie

***Tier 1 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A river-dwelling shapeshifter who prefers an enchanting equine form in and out of the water.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Adopt an attractive or useful shape, drown and devour, entice unwary travelers

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −2 | **Hooves:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Entanglement +3, Mind Reader +3

## FEATURES

***Captivating - Passive:*** A PC must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to move out of the Kelpie’s [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Heart’s Desire - Passive:*** After the Kelpie interacts with or watches a creature for at least a hundred heartbeats, the Kelpie knows the physical form that creature would find most pleasing or alluring.

***Shapeshifter - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to change the Kelpie’s physical form into any creature or object smaller than a wagon. A creature has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on rolls against the Kelpie while the Kelpie is in a form the creature finds pleasing or alluring.

***Enchant - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Kelpie beguile a PC within Close range. The PC must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become *Enchanted* until they take damage. While *Enchanted*, the PC perceives the Kelpie as a trusted friend or ally and will do what the Kelpie says unless it contradicts the PC’s most deeply held morals.
