# Shapeshifting Fiend

***Tier 3 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An evil entity who impersonates a trusted figure.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Impersonate, sow discord, tempt betrayal

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/32 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS) Blade:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d8+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Manipulate +3, Mimic +2

## FEATURES

***Fiendish Nature - Passive:*** The Fiend has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to magic damage and can’t be fooled by magic illusions.

***Impersonate - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Fiend magically transform into a humanoid they have previously interacted with. A PC who targets the Fiend while they’re in the form of a person the PC cares for must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or the attack fails and the PC must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Turn Invisible - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to make the Fiend *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) or take damage.

***Backhanded Guidance - Reaction:*** When a PC makes an action roll while the Fiend is in the scene, you can **mark a Stress** to add **1d6** to the result of their [Fear Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).

***Exposed! (Phase Change) - Reaction:*** When the Fiend is defeated or the PCs discover its true identity, replace the Fiend with [Shapeshifting Fiend Revealed](Shapeshifting%20Fiend%20Revealed.md) and immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.
