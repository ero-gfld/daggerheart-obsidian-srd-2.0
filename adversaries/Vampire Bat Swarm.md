# Vampire Bat Swarm

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (5/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A cloud of blood-sucking bats.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Blot out the moon, murmur, spread disease

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/30 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Fangs:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d6+6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Blood +3

## FEATURES

***Horde (2d6) - Passive:*** When the Swarm has marked half or more of its [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), its standard attack deals **2d6** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Blinding Multitude - Passive:*** The Swarm is so thick it blocks the vision of anyone it interacts with. Creatures within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attacks made against [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) other than the Swarm.

***Bloodsuckers - Reaction:*** When the Swarm deals damage to a target, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Swarm feed. The target marks an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), and the Swarm clears a HP.
