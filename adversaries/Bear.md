# Bear

***Tier 1 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A large bear with thick fur and powerful claws.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Climb, defend territory, pummel, track

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/17 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Claws:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ambusher +3, Keen Senses +2

## FEATURES

***Overwhelming Force - Passive:*** Targets who mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from the Bear’s standard attack are knocked back to [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Bite - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal **3d4+10** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they break free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll.

***Momentum - Reaction:*** When the Bear makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
