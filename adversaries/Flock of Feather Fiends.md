# Flock of Feather Fiends

***Tier 2 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (3/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*A flock of undead crow corpses, eyes aglow with necroplasmic light.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Caw incessantly, claw faces, flutter through the air, peck eyes

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Peck and Claw:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+2 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Fly +2, Swarm +2

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d6+1) - Passive:*** When the Flock has marked half or more of its [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), its standard attack deals **1d6+1** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Maddening Cacophony - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force each PC within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail must spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they deal damage to the Flock or the Flock is defeated.
