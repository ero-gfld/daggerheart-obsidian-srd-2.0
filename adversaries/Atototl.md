# Atototl

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A majestic green water bird that has a ten-foot wingspan and is hunted for the fortune-telling stone inside their stomach.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid, escape, misdirect

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/12 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Talons:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Flight +3, Jungles +3

## FEATURES

***Wind Lord - Passive:*** While the Atototl is flying, attacks against it is made with [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).

***Stone of Omens - Passive:*** Inside the Atototl is a stone that foretells good or ill fortune, depending on its color. A [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who searches the Atototl’s remains finds the stone and makes a [fate roll](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#OPTIONAL%20GM%20MECHANICS). On an even result, the PC gains [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) equal to half the result, which they can distribute among the PCs however they wish. On an odd result, you gain [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) equal to half the result.

***Archer’s Bane - Reaction:*** When a creature beyond [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range would deal damage to the Atototl with a weapon attack, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against them. On a success, whirling winds reflect the attack and deal the attacker’s damage back to them.
