# Secret-Keeper

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A clandestine leader with a direct channel to the Fallen Gods.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Amass great power, plot, take command

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Sigil-laden Staff:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Coercion +2, Fallen Lore +2

## FEATURES

***Seize Your Moment - Action:*** **Spend 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) **1d4** allies. Attacks they make while [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) in this way deal half damage.

***Our Master’s Will - Reaction:*** When you [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) an ally within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Summoning Ritual - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. When the Secret-Keeper is in the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) for the first time, activate the countdown. When they mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), tick down this countdown by the number of HP marked. When it triggers, summon a [Minor Demon](Minor%20Demon.md) who appears at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Fallen Hounds - Reaction:*** Once per scene, when the Secret-Keeper marks 2 or more [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon a [Demonic Hound Pack](Demonic%20Hound%20Pack.md), which appears at [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and is immediately [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).
