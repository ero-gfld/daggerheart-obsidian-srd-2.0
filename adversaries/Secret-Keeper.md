# Secret-Keeper

***Tier 2 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A clandestine leader with a direct channel to the Fallen Gods.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Amass great power, plot, take command

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 13/26 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Sigil-laden Staff:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d12 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Coercion +2, Fallen Lore +2

## FEATURES

***Seize Your Moment - Action:*** **Spend 2 [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) **1d4** allies. Attacks they make while spotlighted in this way deal half damage.

***Our Master’s Will - Reaction:*** When you spotlight an ally within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to gain a Fear.

***Summoning Ritual - Reaction:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (6)*. When the Secret-Keeper is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the countdown. When they mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), tick down this countdown by the number of HP marked. When it triggers, summon a [Minor Demon](Minor%20Demon.md) who appears at Close range.

***Fallen Hounds - Reaction:*** Once per scene, when the Secret-Keeper marks 2 or more HP, you can **mark a Stress** to summon a [Demonic Hound Pack](Demonic%20Hound%20Pack.md), which appears at Close range and is immediately spotlighted.
