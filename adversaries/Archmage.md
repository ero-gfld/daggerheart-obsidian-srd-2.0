# Archmage

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A seeker of secret knowledge hidden away in a tome-filled tower.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Corrupt, destroy, overwhelm with evil power

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 24/45 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 8  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +5 | **Archmage’s Greatstaff:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Esoterica +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Archmage can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Orbiting Aegis - Passive:*** When the Archmage appears, they conjure six discs of magical energy that orbit around them. Place a **d6** Aegis Die on this stat block with the 6 value facing up. When a PC makes a successful attack against the Archmage, roll a **d6**. If the result is equal to or less than the value of the Aegis Die, tick the Aegis Die down and ignore the damage. A PC can make a [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll to tick down the Aegis Die by 1 on a success or by 2 on a [success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).

***Force Salvo - Action:*** **Spend any number of Fear** and roll that many **d4s**. The Archmage unleashes a number of magical homing force bolts equal to the total result. For each bolt, a creature within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range marks a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). The same creature can be targeted by up to three force bolts.

***Fireball - Action:*** **Mark any number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to target the same number of PCs within Far range. Each target must succeed on an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or take **4d10** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Counterspell - Reaction:*** When a PC succeeds on a Spellcast Roll, you can **spend a Fear** to have the Archmage make a reaction roll with a [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) equal to the Spellcast Roll’s result. On a success, the PC’s Spellcast Roll fails instead, and you place a token on this stat block. Using this feature again costs additional Fear equal to the number of tokens on this stat block. You can’t Counterspell a critical success.
