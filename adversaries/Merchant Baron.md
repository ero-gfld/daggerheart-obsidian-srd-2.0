## FEATURES

***Everyone Has a Price - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to offer a target a dangerous bargain for something they want or need. If used on a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), they must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (17). On a failure, they must mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or take the deal.

***The Best Muscle Money Can Buy - Action:*** Once per scene, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon **1d4+1** [Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md), who appear at [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, to enforce the Baron’s will.
