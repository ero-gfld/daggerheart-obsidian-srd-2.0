# Hallowed Archer

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*Spirit soldiers with sanctified bows.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Focus fire, obey, reposition, volley

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 25/45 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Sanctified Longbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+8 phy

## FEATURES

***Punish the Guilty - Passive:*** The Archer deals double damage to targets marked *Guilty* by a [High Seraph](High%20Seraph.md).

***Divine Volley - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against up to three targets.
