# Giant Eagle

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A giant bird of prey with blood-stained talons.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Hunt prey, stay mobile, strike decisively

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/19 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Claws and Beak:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+3 phy

## FEATURES

***Flight - Passive:*** While flying, the Eagle gains a +3 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Deadly Dive - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to attack a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **2d10+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and knock the target over, making them *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they next act.

***Take Off - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal **2d4+3** physical damage and the target must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or become temporarily *Restrained* within the Eagle’s massive talons. If the target is *Restrained*, the Eagle immediately lifts into the air to Very Far range above the battlefield while holding them.

***Deadly Drop - Action:*** While flying, the Eagle can drop a *Restrained* target they are holding. When dropped, the target is no longer *Restrained* but starts falling. If their fall isn’t prevented during the PCs’ next action, the target takes **2d20** physical damage when they land.
