# Cephilith Abomination

***Tier 4 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A lumbering, bog-grown frog beast that has tadpolelike leeches visibly wriggling underneath their translucent skin.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Do the heavy lifting, leap menacingly, move surprisingly fast, smash and grab

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 19 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Meaty Fists:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Brute +3

## FEATURES

***Toxic Skin - Passive:*** A PC who comes into physical contact with the Abomination must roll a **d6**. On a result of 3 or lower, the PC must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) or 2 [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Bear Hug - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes standard damage and is *Grappled* until they escape with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. While *Grappled*, the target is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and *Restrained*.

***Crunch! - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to deal **4d12** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to a creature *Grappled* by the Abomination.

***Tongue Attack - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Abomination pull a target within Far range into Melee range, then immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Abomination again.

***Burrowing Leechpoles - Reaction:*** When the Abomination takes Major or greater damage, their skin erupts in an explosion of subcutaneous wrigglers. Each PC within Close range must make an Agility [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail are covered in **2d4** leechpoles, which burrow under their skin. Until the leechpoles are dug out with a successful Finesse Roll (21), any damage the target takes gains a +1 bonus for each leechpole under their skin.
