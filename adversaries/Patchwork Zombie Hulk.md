# Patchwork Zombie Hulk

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A towering gestalt of corpses moving as one, with torso-sized limbs and fists as large as a grown halfling.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Absorb corpses, flail, hunger, terrify

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Too Many Arms:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d20 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intimidation +2, Tear Things Apart +2

## FEATURES

***Destructible - Passive:*** When the Zombie takes [Major](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or greater damage, they mark an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Flailing Limbs - Passive:*** When the Zombie makes a standard attack, they can attack all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Another for the Pile - Action:*** When the Zombie is within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a corpse, they can incorporate it into themselves, clearing a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Tormented Screams - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to cause all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13). Targets who fail lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) for each. Targets who succeed must mark a Stress.
