# Unicorn

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A celestial avatar of goodness in the form of a preternaturally beautiful horse with a long, spiraled horn emerging from its head.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Keep evil at bay, protect the sacred, sense emotions and intent

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 19/36 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Hoof & Horn:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10+3 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Magic +3, Nature +3

## FEATURES

***Celestial Lance - Passive:*** The Unicorn’s standard attack deals double damage to a creature that has dealt damage to it.

***Healing Touch - Action:*** The Unicorn touches its horn to an ally within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and channels healing energy into them. **Mark any number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to clear an equal number of the ally’s [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Protective Aura - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Unicorn conjure a sparkling magical aura. While the aura is in effect, [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) made against the Unicorn or an ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range are made with [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE). This effect lasts until the Unicorn takes damage.

***Teleport - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Unicorn teleport up to [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The Unicorn can bring up to three allies within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with it.
