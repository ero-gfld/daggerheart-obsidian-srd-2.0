# Harbinger of Death

***Tier 4 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A desiccated corpse riding atop a skeletal horse. Their eyes are sewn shut and their body is wrapped in a burial shroud of dust and ash.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bring judgment, cut them down, move swiftly, seek relentlessly, take souls

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 28/52 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Great Scythe:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Judgment +3

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Harbinger can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Mount - Passive:*** While the Harbinger is on their mount, they gain a +2 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). When the Harbinger takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they’re knocked from their mount. If the Harbinger is unhorsed, their mount disappears until you **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon it again.

***Fear the Reaper - Passive:*** When a creature is defeated within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Harbinger, you gain a Fear.

***Decapitate - Action:*** **Spend 2 Fear** to have the Harbinger make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against a PC within Melee range. On a success, roll a **d6**. On a result of 6, the target marks all HP and must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH).

***Wake the Fallen - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** and **mark any number of Stress** to have the Harbinger resurrect a defeated ally within Far range. The resurrected ally clears a number of HP equal to the number of Stress marked. Immediately spotlight the ally.

***Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust - Reaction:*** When the Harbinger would mark HP, you can **mark a Stress** to have an ally within Far range mark the HP instead.
