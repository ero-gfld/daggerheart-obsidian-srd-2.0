# Darkweave Crawler

***Tier 1 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A spider the size of a human head, woven out of shadow-silk.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Hide in shadow, strike the unwary, wait for an opening

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 10 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** –2 | **Bite:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Darkness +3

## FEATURES

***Minion (3) - Passive:*** The Crawler is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to the Crawler, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Skin-Crawling - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force all PCs within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Crawler to mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Darkweave Crawlers within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

***Darkweave Venom - Reaction:*** When the Crawler makes a successful attack, you can **mark a Stress** to *Exhaust* the target until they succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll (9). While *Exhausted*, the target must mark a Stress each time they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).
