# Harrier

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A nimble fighter armed with javelins.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Flank, harry, kite, profit

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/9 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Javelin:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Camouflage +2

## FEATURES

***Maintain Distance - Passive:*** After making a standard attack, the Harrier can move anywhere within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Fall Back - Reaction:*** When a creature moves into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make an attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** before the [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to move anywhere within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and make an attack against that creature. On a success, deal **1d10+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
