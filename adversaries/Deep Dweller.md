# Deep Dweller

***Tier 3 [Leader](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A huge psychic lamprey that has pitch-black eyes and toxic tentacles.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Abuse power, seek worship, stalk sunken ruins

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 17 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 22/42 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +5 | **Teeth & Tentacles:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ancient Mysteries +2, Dark Water +2

## FEATURES

***Psychic Blast - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose up to three targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and force them to make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or Knowledge [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who succeed mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). Targets who fail take **3d10+4** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Brain Drain - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to choose a *Vulnerable* PC within Very Close range. The target marks **1d4** Stress, and the Deep Dweller clears an equal number of [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Call of the Deep - Action:*** **Spend 2 Fear** to summon **1d4** [Cursed Merfolk](Cursed%20Merfolk.md), who appear at Far range and immediately take the spotlight.

***Mucosal Contamination - Action:*** Make an attack roll against a PC within Very Close range. On a success, the Deep Dweller uses its tentacles to immobilize the target, sliming them with toxic sludge. The target is *Restrained* until they break free or slip out with a successful Strength or Finesse Roll. Each time the Deep Dweller is spotlighted, all PCs *Restrained* in this way take **3d8** direct physical damage. The Deep Dweller releases all creatures *Restrained* by it when it’s defeated or takes Severe damage.
