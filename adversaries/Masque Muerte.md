# Masque Muerte

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The raging soul of a villainous masked wrestler.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Call out, embarrass, energize the audience, pin opponents to the ground

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/14 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Open-Handed Strike:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+2 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Grappler +3, Showboat +3

## FEATURES

***Libre - Passive:*** The Masque Muerte can’t be *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.

***Heel Turn - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target must mark a Stress and is temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* as the Masque Muerte assaults them with a string of insulting taunts.

***Spectral Suplex - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Masque Muerte suplex a *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), dealing **1d10+6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to them.

***Unmasking Death - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Masque Muerte momentarily remove their mask, revealing their horrifying face underneath. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Tag Team - Reaction:*** When the Masque Muerte deals damage to a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F), you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have a spectral wrestler appear and *Pin* the target until they escape with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. While *Pinned*, the target is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and takes an extra **1d12** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) from the Masque Muerte’s attacks.
