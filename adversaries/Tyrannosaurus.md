# Tyrannosaurus

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A huge carnivorous dinosaur that walks on two legs.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Break through the treeline, eat, stomp around

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/28 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Mighty Jaws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Predator +3

## FEATURES

***Earth-Shaking Roar - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force each PC within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). On a failure, they must choose to lose a Hope or mark **1d4** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Stomp - Action:*** **Mark a Stress** to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes **3d12** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Crushing Bite - Reaction:*** When the Tyrannosaurus makes a successful standard attack, you can **spend a Fear** to force the target to mark an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
