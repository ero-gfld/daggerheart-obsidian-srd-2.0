# Supreme Demiurge Adonix

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*The four-faced angel from the Hallows Above who manifests in the Mortal Realm as a floating tetrahedron with numerous eyes and multiple sets of metallic feathered wings.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Establish dominion, exile from reality, float above it all, speak into being

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 10  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Wing Strike:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Omnipotence +10, Be Not Afraid +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (4) - Passive:*** Adonix can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to four times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Elemental Archon - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to roll a **d4**. Adonix activates the corresponding effect: 
1. ***Fire Beam.*** Adonix manifests a beam of elemental fire with a [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) width from themself to the edge of the battlefield. Each target in the beam’s path must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). Targets who succeed take [Minor damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). 
2. ***Thunderclap.*** All creatures within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they succeed on an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) or the Demiurge is defeated. 
3. ***Lightning Bolt.*** Make an attack against a target within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, dealing **4d8** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) on a success. If all results are even, make the same attack against a different target, repeating the cycle until you roll an odd result or run out of targets. 
4. ***Ice Storm.*** Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or mark **1d4** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and become *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to escape.

***Alpha to Omega - Evolution:*** When Adonix is defeated, they resurrect in Omega form with all [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) cleared. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). You gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) for each Hope lost in this way. Adonix gains the following features in this form:

***Forsaken - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) and activate a Forsaken [Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (1d8) for them. It ticks down when Adonix is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT). When it triggers, the PC must mark all their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). The countdown ends if Adonix takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). You can have only one Forsaken Countdown active at a time.

***Armageddon - Reaction:*** When Adonix is defeated again, they unleash a reality storm that shakes the realms to their foundations. Roll a **d6**. On a result of 5–6, each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must make a [death move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DEATH).
