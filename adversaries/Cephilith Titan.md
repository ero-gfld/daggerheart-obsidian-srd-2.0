# Cephilith Titan

***Tier 4 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A transdimensional entity in the form of an impossibly large, winged humanoid with an octopus-shaped head and rows of glowing eyes.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Call and consume followers, corrupt reality, invade the Mortal Realm, spew forth chaos

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 38/68 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 10  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Pseudoclaw:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d8+10 mag

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Chaos +5, Psychic +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (X) - Passive:*** The Titan can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to X times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md), where X equals the number of PCs in the scene. Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Titan makes a successful attack, all PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a Hope, and you gain a Fear.

***Merely a Projection - Passive:*** The Titan appears in the Mortal Realm as a psychic projection approximating their true form. They have [immunity](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage and resistance to magic damage.

***Psychic Scream - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Titan unleash a mind-shattering onslaught of nightmarish visions from the Outer Realms. Each PC within Far range must make an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (22). Targets who fail take **4d12** direct magic damage. Targets who succeed must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Summon Worshippers - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to summon **1d4** [Cephilith Abominations](Cephilith%20Abomination.md), which appear within Far range, then choose one to immediately spotlight.

***“It’s Here…” - Evolution:*** When the Titan has marked half their [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), they manifest in their full form, clearing all HP and losing the “Merely a Projection” feature. While the Titan is in this form, a creature who marks HP from the Titan’s standard attack must mark an additional HP.
