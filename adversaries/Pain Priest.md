# Pain Priest

***Tier 3 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A member of an other-dimensional religious order devoted to exploring the boundaries of mortal suffering and ecstasy.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Feed on emotions, locate the innocent or curious, tempt them with their deepest desires

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/32 | **HP:** 6 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Torture Implements:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d6+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Experimentation +4

## FEATURES

***Walk Between Worlds - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to place the Priest anywhere within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and immediately [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them again.

***Pleasure and Pain - Reaction:*** When a PC within Close range of the Priest gains a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), roll a **d8**. On a result of 8, the Priest opens a portal to their original dimension and flees there with the closest party member. You gain **1d6** Fear. The portal remains open until the Priest is defeated or spotlighted again.
