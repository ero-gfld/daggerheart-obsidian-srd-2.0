# Courtier

***Tier 1 [Social](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An ambitious and ostentatiously dressed socialite.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Discredit, gain favor, maneuver, scheme

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −4 | **Daggers:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d4+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Socialite +3

## FEATURES

***Mockery - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to say something mocking and force a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (14) to see if they can save face. On a failure, the target must mark 2 Stress and is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the scene ends.

***Scapegoat - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and target a PC. The Courtier convinces a crowd or prominent individual that the target is the cause of their current conflict or misfortune.
