## FEATURES

***Mockery - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to say something mocking and force a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (14) to see if they can save face. On a failure, the target must mark 2 Stress and is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until the scene ends.

***Scapegoat - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and target a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F). The Courtier convinces a crowd or prominent individual that the target is the cause of their current conflict or misfortune.
