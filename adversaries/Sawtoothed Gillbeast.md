# Sawtoothed Gillbeast

***Tier 1 [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A stocky fish creature that has rows of razor-sharp teeth.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Clamber ashore, hit and run, peek above the waterline

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 12 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5/10 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +1 | **Waterlogged Weaponry:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Aquatic +2

## FEATURES

***Ka-Chomp - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to attack a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target takes **1d12** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without gaining its benefit (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).

***Feeding Frenzy - Reaction:*** When a creature marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Sawtoothed Gillbeasts within Very Close range of them. Those Gillbeasts move into Melee range of the creature and each make a standard attack against them. If any attacks succeed, combine their damage.

***Scaly - Reaction:*** When the Gillbeast would mark any number of HP, roll a **d6**. On a result of 6, you can mark a Stress instead.
