# Xero the Castle Killer

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A castle-sized, bipedal reptile.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Leave a wake of destruction, level buildings, stomp through settlements

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 20 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/70 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 10  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Tail Swipe:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+6 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Destruction +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (X) - Passive:*** Xero can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to X times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md), where X is the number of [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in the scene. Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it.

***Collateral Damage - Passive:*** Xero’s movement can’t be hindered, impeded, or affected by anything other than natural terrain. All destructible terrain or obstacles in Xero’s path are destroyed when Xero moves through them.

***Gigaton Stomp - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have Xero bring its foot down hard, creating a localized earthquake. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range takes **4d12** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they wriggle out with a successful [Finesse](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. Each creature beyond Melee range but within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or be knocked back to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. If a target is knocked back, they must either mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) or take **4d12** physical damage as they land.

***Power Slide - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) Xero moves through must succeed on a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or choose to either mark 2 [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) or take [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Radioactive Breath - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have Xero charge up a nuclear beam. Place a token on this stat block. The next time Xero is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT), you can **spend this token** and unleash a beam to [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The beam has a width of [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in this area must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **4d12+12** direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who succeed take half damage. Xero can’t use this feature again until it takes [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Regeneration - Action:*** Up to three times per scene, **spend any number of [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to clear an equal number of [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
