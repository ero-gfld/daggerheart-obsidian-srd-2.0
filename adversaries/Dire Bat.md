# Dire Bat

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A wide-winged pet endlessly loyal to their vampire owner.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Dive-bomb, hide, protect leader

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 16/30 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Claws and Teeth:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d6+7 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Bloodthirsty +3

## FEATURES

***Flying - Passive:*** While flying, the Bat gains a +3 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Screech - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to send a high-pitch screech out toward all targets in front of the Bat within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Those targets must mark **1d4** Stress.

***Guardian - Reaction:*** When an allied [Vampire](Vampire.md) marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to fly into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the attacker and make an attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against them. On a success, deal **2d6+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
