# Minotaur Wrecker

***Tier 2 [Bruiser](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A massive bull-headed firbolg with a quick temper.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Consume, gore, navigate, overpower, pursue

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 14/27 | **HP:** 7 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Battleaxe:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Navigation +2

## FEATURES

***Ramp Up - Passive:*** You must **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Minotaur. While spotlighted, they can make their standard attack against all targets within range.

***Charging Bull - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to charge through a group within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and make an attack against all targets in the Minotaur’s path. Targets the Minotaur succeeds against take **2d6+8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are knocked back to Very Far range. If a target is knocked into a solid object or another creature, they take an extra **1d6** damage (combine the damage).

***Gore - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range, moving the Minotaur into Melee range of them. On a success, deal **2d8** direct physical damage.
