# Entombed Elite Guard

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Standard](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*An elite undead soldier buried with their empress to defend the tomb from theft and desecration.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Form up, protect the empress, strike as one

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 15 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9/18 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Curved Blade:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Soldier +3

## FEATURES

***Battle Formation - Passive:*** The Guard gains a +1 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) for each ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Javelin - Action:*** Make an attack against a target within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **2d6+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
