# Jack-O’-Lantern

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A floating pumpkin or other gourd that has a face carved into it. The soul trapped within it emits an eerie glow.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Bite, float, laugh, tell spooky stories

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Chomp:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 6 phy

## FEATURES

***Minion (4) - Passive:*** The Jack-o’-Lantern is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Jack-o’- Lantern, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Jack-o’-Lanterns within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). On a success, they deal 6 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

***Spine-Chilling Cackle - Reaction:*** When a PC within Very Close range rolls with Fear, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to gain an additional Fear.
