# Rabble Mawb

***Tier 1 [Horde](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) (3/[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS))***
*These cat-sized balls of hair, limbs, and teeth travel in a “mawb” of a dozen.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Chitter and chew, clump together, roll around

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 8 | **Thresholds:** 4/8 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 2  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −2 | **Chomp:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d6+3 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Underground +2

## FEATURES

***Horde (1d4+1) - Passive:*** When the Rabble Mawb has marked half or more of its [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), its standard attack deals **1d4+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) instead.

***Come Back Worse - Reaction:*** When the Rabble Mawb is defeated, you can **spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to bring it back to life, clearing all HP and [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS). The Rabble Mawb gains a bonus to all rolls equal to the number of times this feature has been used by this Rabble Mawb.
