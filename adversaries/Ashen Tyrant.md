# Ashen Tyrant

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*No enemy has ever had the insolence to wound the dragon so. As the lava settles, it’s ground to ash like the dragon’s past foes.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Choke, fly, intimidate, kill or be killed

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 29/55 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 5  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +10 | **Claws and Teeth:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d12+15 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Hunt from Above +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (4) - Passive:*** The Ashen Tyrant can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to four times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Cornered - Passive:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** instead of spending a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) the Ashen Tyrant.

***Injured Wings - Passive:*** While flying, the Ashen Tyrant gains a +1 bonus to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS).

***Ashes to Ashes - Passive:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) rolls a failure while within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Ashen Tyrant, they lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If the PC can’t lose a Hope, they must mark a [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

***Desperate Rampage - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Ashen Tyrant succeeds against take **2d20+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), are knocked back to Close range of where they were, and must mark a Stress.

***Ashen Cloud - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to smash the ground and kick up ash within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. While within the ash cloud, a target has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). The ash cloud clears the next time an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) is [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT).

***Apocalyptic Thrashing - Action:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (1d12)*. **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to activate. It ticks down when a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) rolls with Fear. When it triggers, the Ashen Tyrant thrashes about, causing environmental damage (such as an earthquake, avalanche, or collapsing walls). All targets within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make a [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail take **2d10+10** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* by the rubble until they break free with a successful Strength Roll. Targets who succeed take half damage. If the Ashen Tyrant is defeated while this countdown is active, trigger the countdown immediately as the destruction caused by their death throes.
