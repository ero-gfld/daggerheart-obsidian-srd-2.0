# Redcap Skinner

***Tier 1 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cat-sized fey creature wielding a straight razor and wearing a conical red cap sealed on their head with candle wax.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Flense, giggle, scrape, slice

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 9 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** −1 | **Razor:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Leatherwork +3

## FEATURES

***Minion (4) - Passive:*** The Skinner is defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Skinner, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Shallow Cuts - Passive:*** When a PC fails a roll, they take 1 [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each Skinner within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them. Combine this damage.

***Group Attack - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a target and [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) all Redcap Skinners within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
