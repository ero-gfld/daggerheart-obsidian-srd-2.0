# Soul-Shattered Mage

**_Tier 1 Solo._** _A spellcaster with a psyche broken by knowledge best left hidden._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Babble in an unknown language, laugh unsettlingly, wield power beyond control
- **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 8/15 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 5
- **ATK:** +2 | **Wormwood Staff:** Far | 1d12 mag
- **Experience:** Forbidden Knowledge +2

### FEATURES

**_Relentless (3) - Passive:_** The Mage can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

**_Broken Magic - Action:_** **Mark a Stress** to roll a **d6**. The Mage uses the corresponding move:

- 1-2: _Dimension Rift._ Choose a target within Far range. The target teleports to a point you choose within Close range of their original position and takes **2d6** direct magic damage as their body and mind reassemble.
- 3-4: _Time Dilation._ Choose a group of PCs within Far range. Each target is _Slowed_ until they roll with Hope. While _Slowed_, a target is _Vulnerable_ and must mark a Stress when they move.
- 5-6: _Discordant Visions._ Make an attack against up to three targets within Close range. Targets the Mage succeeds against take **2d8** direct magic damage.

**_Feel My Pain - Reaction:_** When the Mage marks HP, roll a number of **d6s** equal to the number of Stress the Mage has marked. The Mage deals direct magic damage equal to the total result to all PCs within Very Close range.
