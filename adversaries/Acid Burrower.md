# Acid Burrower

***Tier 1 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A horse-sized insect with digging claws and acidic blood.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Burrow, drag away, feed, reposition

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/15 | **HP:** 8 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Claws:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d12+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Tremor Sense +2

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Burrower can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight them.

***Earth Eruption - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have the Burrower burst out of the ground. All creatures within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or be knocked over, making them *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they next act.

***Spit Acid - Action:*** Make an attack against all targets in front of the Burrower within Close range. Targets the Burrower succeeds against take **2d6** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) without receiving its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage). If they can’t mark an Armor Slot, they must mark an additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) and you gain a Fear.

***Acid Bath - Reaction:*** When the Burrower takes Severe damage, all creatures within Close range are bathed in their acidic blood, taking **1d10** physical damage. This splash covers the ground within Very Close range with blood, and all creatures other than the Burrower who move through it take **1d6** physical damage.
