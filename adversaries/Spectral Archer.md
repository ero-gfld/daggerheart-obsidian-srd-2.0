# Spectral Archer

***[Tier 2](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A ghostly fighter with an ethereal bow, unable to move on while their charge is vulnerable.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Move through solid objects, stay out of the fray, rehash old battles

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 6/14 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 3 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Longbow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d10+2 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ancient [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) +2

## FEATURES

***Ghost - Passive:*** The Archer has [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to move up to [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range through solid objects.

***Pick Your Target - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an attack within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) who is within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of at least two other PCs. On a success, the target takes **2d8+12** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
