# Centaur Warden

***Tier 2 [Ranged](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A half-human, half-horse guardian who patrols the borders of idyllic lands uncorrupted by the touch of civilization.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Live in harmony with nature, protect the wilderness, use mobility and distance to drive off intruders

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10/19 | **HP:** 5 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 3  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +2 | **Recurved Bow:** [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+4 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Nature +2, Philosophy +2

## FEATURES

***Flanking Maneuver - Passive:*** When the Warden makes a standard attack against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of one or more of the Warden’s allies, the Warden gains a +2 bonus to the [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Eye of the Sage - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a standard attack. The Warden has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the attack roll and critically succeeds on a roll of 19–20.

***Trample - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Warden gallop up to Far range in a straight line. Each target in their path must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who succeed take **1d6+2** physical damage. Targets who fail take double damage and must mark a Stress.

***Quick Volley - Reaction:*** When the Warden makes a standard attack, you can **mark any number of Stress** to target an equal number of additional creatures within range.
