# Crimson Lepus

***Tier 3 [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A red-eyed rabbit that thinks only of murder.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Be cute, go for the throat, hop around

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/35 | **HP:** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Leaping Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d20 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Vicious +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Lepus can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to spotlight it.

***“Awww...” - Passive:*** When the Lepus first appears, it is *Too Cute*. To attack a *Too Cute* creature, a PC must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (20). Clear the *Too Cute* condition if a PC deals damage to the Lepus or if the Lepus attacks a PC.

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Lepus makes a successful attack, all PCs within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a Hope, and you gain a Fear.

***Leaping Jugular Strike - Action:*** **Spend a Fear** to have the Lepus leap into Melee range of a target within Far range and make an attack against them. On a success, deal **3d20** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). If the target marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from this attack, they become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a HP.

***Evasive - Reaction:*** When the Lepus would take damage, roll a **d6**. On a 4 or higher, halve the damage. If the damage was already halved, the Lepus takes no damage.

***“Run Away!” - Reaction:*** When a PC with no Hope takes the spotlight within Very Far range of the Lepus, the PC must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll or use their action to flee the battlefield until they spend a Hope to return.
