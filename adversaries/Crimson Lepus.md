# Crimson Lepus

***[Tier 3](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A red-eyed rabbit that thinks only of murder.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Be cute, go for the throat, hop around

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 18 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 20/35 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 10 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 6  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Leaping Bite:** [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 3d20 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Vicious +4

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** The Lepus can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) it.

***“Awww...” - Passive:*** When the Lepus first appears, it is *Too Cute*. To attack a *Too Cute* creature, a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must succeed on an [Instinct](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (20). Clear the *Too Cute* condition if a PC deals damage to the Lepus or if the Lepus attacks a PC.

***Terrifying - Passive:*** When the Lepus makes a successful attack, all [PCs](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range lose a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), and you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Leaping Jugular Strike - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to have the Lepus leap into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and make an attack against them. On a success, deal **3d20** direct [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). If the target marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from this attack, they become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a HP.

***Evasive - Reaction:*** When the Lepus would take damage, roll a **d6**. On a 4 or higher, halve the damage. If the damage was already halved, the Lepus takes no damage.

***“Run Away!” - Reaction:*** When a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) with no [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) takes the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the Lepus, the PC must succeed on a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) or use their action to flee the battlefield until they spend a Hope to return.
