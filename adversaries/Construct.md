# Construct

***[Tier 1](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A roughly humanoid being of stone and steel, assembled and animated by magic.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Destroy [environment](../core_rules/Using%20Environments.md), serve creator, smash target, trample groups

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 7/15 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 9 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +4 | **Fist Slam:** [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 1d20 phy

## FEATURES

***Relentless (2) - Passive:*** The Construct can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to two times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them.

***Weak Structure - Passive:*** When the Construct marks [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), they must mark an additional HP.

***Trample - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make an attack against all targets in the Construct’s path when they move. Targets the Construct succeeds against take **1d8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Overload - Reaction:*** Before rolling damage for the Construct’s attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to gain a +10 bonus to the [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The Construct can then take the [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) again.

***Death Quake - Reaction:*** When the Construct marks their last [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), the magic powering them ruptures in an explosion of force. Make an attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets the Construct succeeds against take **1d12+2** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
