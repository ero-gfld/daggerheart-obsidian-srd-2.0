# Sprite

***Tier 2 [Minion](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A tiny winged warrior that attacks trespassers with poison-tipped arrows.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Avoid detection, let the poison do its work, protect the wilderness

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 13 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** None | **HP:** 1 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 1  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +0 | **Needle-Sharp Arrows:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 5 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Stealth +2, Vengeance +2

## FEATURES

***Minion (5) - Passive:*** The Sprite is defeated when they take any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Sprite, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

***Art of Invisibility - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make the Sprite *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they attack.

***Dream Petal Poison - Action:*** When the Sprite succeeds on a standard attack, you can **spend a Fear** to make the target *Sleepy* until the target takes damage. While *Sleepy*, the target must make a [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) before they make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). On a failure, they must mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or fall asleep until they take damage.
