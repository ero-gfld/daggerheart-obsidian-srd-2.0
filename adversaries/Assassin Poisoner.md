# Assassin Poisoner

***Tier 2 [Skulk](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A cunning scoundrel skilled in both poisons and ambushing.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Anticipate, get paid, kill, taint food and water

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 14 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 8/16 | **HP:** 4 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 4  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +3 | **Poisoned Throwing Dagger:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 2d8+1 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Intrusion +2

## FEATURES

***Grindletooth Venom - Passive:*** Targets who mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from the Assassin’s attacks are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a HP.

***Out of Nowhere - Passive:*** The Assassin has [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attacks if they are *Hidden*.

***Fumigation - Action:*** Drop a smoke bomb that fills the air within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with smoke, *Dizzying* all targets in this area. *Dizzied* targets have disadvantage on their next [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), then clear the condition.
