# Dragon Mother Mitera

***[Tier 4](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md#ADVERSARIES%20BY%20TIER) [Solo](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md)***
*A gargantuan seven-headed dragon with dazzling diamond scales that shine with every color of the rainbow.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Birth destruction, control the battlefield, fly, repel invaders, use all available options

> **[Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS):** 16 | **[Thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 35/65 | **[HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS):** 12 | **[Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS):** 10  
> **[ATK](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING):** +8 | **Bite & Slash:** [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) | 4d10+10 phy

**[Experience](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207):** Ancient +5

## FEATURES

***Relentless (3) - Passive:*** Mitera can be [spotlighted](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) up to three times per [GM turn](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md). Spend [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) as usual to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) her.

***Diamond Hide - Passive:*** *[Countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (7).* When Mitera first appears, activate the countdown. Mitera gains a bonus to her [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) equal to the countdown die’s current value. It ticks down from Mitera’s “Seven-Headed” feature. When the countdown triggers, Mitera becomes *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and gains a +2 bonus to [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

***Elemental Breath - Action:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to have Mitera unleash elemental energy from one head of your choice: 
1. ***Fire.*** Deal **4d12** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to **1d4+1** targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets who take damage must mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) or 2 additional [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). 
2. ***Lightning.*** Deal **4d12** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to **1d6** targets within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, so long as each target beyond the first is within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the previous target. 
3. ***Acid.*** Each [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) in front of Mitera within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range must make an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS). Targets who fail must mark 2 [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) and 2 [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). Targets who succeed must mark an Armor Slot and a HP. 
4. ***Poison.*** A miasma of toxic swamp gas *Sickens* all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of Mitera. While *Sickened*, a target has [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) until they mark **1d4** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) to clear the condition. 
5. ***Ice.*** Each target in front of Mitera within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range takes **4d8+8** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and is *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* by ice until they break free with a successful [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll. Additionally, this area freezes over and a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) must succeed on an [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) to move through it. 
6. ***Darkness.*** A cloud of magical shadow fills an area within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Creatures inside the cloud except for Mitera are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* and have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) that rely on sight. The cloud dissipates when this Elemental Breath option is removed by Mitera’s “Seven-Headed” feature. 
7. ***Light.*** A beam of pure light energy deals 28 direct [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to each creature in a line within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

***Tail Swipe - Action:*** **Spend a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to attack all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets this attack succeeds against take **2d20+10** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are knocked back to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets who mark [HP](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from this attack become *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they clear a HP. Targets this attack failed against must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

***Fearsome - Reaction:*** When Mitera makes a successful [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) against a [PC](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) or a PC fails a roll within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you gain a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).

***Seven-Headed - Reaction:*** When Mitera takes damage, roll a **d8**. Permanently remove the “Elemental Breath” option that corresponds to the result. On a result of 8, or if the corresponding option has already been removed, tick down the Diamond Hide [countdown](../core_rules/Making%20Moves.md#COUNTDOWNS) (see “Diamond Hide”).
