# Encore

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range deals damage to an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md), you can make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against that same target. On a success, you deal the same damage to the target that your ally dealt. If your Spellcast Roll succeeds with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), place this card in your [vault](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).
