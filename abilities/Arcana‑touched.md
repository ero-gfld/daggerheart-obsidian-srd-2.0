# Arcana‑touched

***[Level 7](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *Arcana [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your [loadout](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) are from the Arcana domain, gain the following benefits:

- +1 bonus to your [Spellcast Rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)
- Once per rest, you can switch the results of your [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and [Fear Dice](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).
