# Gore and Glory

***Level 9*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When you critically succeed on a weapon attack, gain an additional [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) or clear an additional [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

Additionally, when you deal enough damage to defeat an enemy, gain a Hope or clear a Stress.
