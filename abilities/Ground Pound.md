# Ground Pound

***Level 8*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

**Spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to strike the ground where you stand and make a **[Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll** against all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against are thrown back to Far range and must make a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (17). Targets who fail take **4d10+8** damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.
