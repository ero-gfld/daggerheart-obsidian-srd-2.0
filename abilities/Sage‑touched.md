# Sage‑touched

***Level 7*** *Sage Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Sage domain, gain the following benefits:

- While you’re in a natural environment, you gain a +2 bonus to your [Spellcast Rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS).
- Once per rest, you can double your [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) or Instinct when making a roll that uses that trait. You must choose to do this before you roll.
