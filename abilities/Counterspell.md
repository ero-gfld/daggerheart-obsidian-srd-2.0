# Counterspell

***Level 3*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

You can interrupt a magical effect taking place by making a [reaction roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) using your Spellcast trait. On a success, the effect stops and any consequences are avoided, and this card is placed in your vault.
