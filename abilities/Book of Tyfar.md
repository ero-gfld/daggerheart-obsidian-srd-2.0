# Book of Tyfar

***Level 1*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

***Wild Flame:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against up to three adversaries within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against take **2d6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) as flames erupt from your hand.

***Magic Hand:*** You conjure a magical hand with the same size and strength as your own within Far range.

***Mysterious Mist:*** Make a **Spellcast Roll (13)** to cast a temporary thick fog that gathers in a stationary area within Very Close range. The fog heavily obscures this area and everything in it.
