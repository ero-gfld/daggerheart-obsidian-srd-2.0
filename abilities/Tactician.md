# Tactician

***Level 3*** *[Bone](../domains/Bone.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When you [Help an Ally](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), they can spend a Hope to add one of your [Experiences](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%207) to their roll alongside your [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) die.

When making a [Tag Team Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS), you can roll a **d20** as your [Hope Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).
