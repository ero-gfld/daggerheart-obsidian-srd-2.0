# Book of Vyola

***Level 8*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

***Memory Delve:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, peer into the target’s mind and ask the GM a question. The GM describes any memories the target has pertaining to the answer.

***Shared Clarity:*** Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose two willing creatures. When one of them would mark [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), they can choose between the two of them who marks it. This spell lasts until their next rest.
