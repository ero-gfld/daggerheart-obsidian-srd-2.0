# Goad Them on

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Describe how you taunt a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, then make a **[Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll** against them. On a success, the target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and the next time the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) [spotlights](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) them, they must target you with an attack, which they make with [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).
