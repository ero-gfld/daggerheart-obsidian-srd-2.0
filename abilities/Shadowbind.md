# Shadowbind

***Level 2*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against all adversaries within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against are temporarily *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* as their shadow binds them in place.
