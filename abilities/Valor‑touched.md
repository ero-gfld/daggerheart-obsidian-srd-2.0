# Valor‑touched

***Level 7*** *Valor Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Valor domain, gain the following benefits:

- +1 bonus to your [Armor Score](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR)
- When you mark 1 or more [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) without marking an Armor Slot, clear an Armor Slot.
