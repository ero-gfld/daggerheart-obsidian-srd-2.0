# Ferocity

***Level 2*** *[Bone](../domains/Bone.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When you cause an adversary to mark 1 or more [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to increase your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) by the number of Hit Points they marked. This bonus lasts until after the next attack made against you.
