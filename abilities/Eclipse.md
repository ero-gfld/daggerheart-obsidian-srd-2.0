# Eclipse

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (16)**. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, plunge the entire area within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range into complete darkness only you and your allies can see through. [Attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) have [disadvantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) when targeting you or an ally within this shadow.

Additionally, when you or an ally succeeds with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) against an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within this shadow, the target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).

This spell lasts until the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) spends a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) on their turn to clear this effect or you take [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
