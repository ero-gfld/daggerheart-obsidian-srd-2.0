# Boost

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Bone](../domains/Bone.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to boost off a willing ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, fling yourself into the air, and perform an aerial attack against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. You have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on the attack, add a **d10** to the [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING), and end your move within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the target.
