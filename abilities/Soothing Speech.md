# Soothing Speech

***Level 4*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

During a [short rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), when you take the time to comfort another character while using the Tend to Wounds downtime move on them, clear an additional [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) on that character. When you do, you also clear 2 Hit Points.
