# Cloaking Blast

***Level 7*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When you make a successful **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** to cast a different spell, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to become *Cloaked*. While *Cloaked*, you remain unseen if you are stationary when an adversary moves to where they would normally see you. When you move into or within an adversary’s [line of sight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) or make an attack, you are no longer *Cloaked*.
