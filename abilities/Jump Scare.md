# Jump Scare

***Level 6*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When you deal [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to a target, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to teleport into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range with them. When you do, they are *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* until they mark 1 or more [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
