# Healing Hands

***[Level 2](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13)** and target a creature other than yourself within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to clear 2 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or 2 Stress on the target. On a failure, **mark a Stress** to clear a Hit Point or a Stress on the target. You can’t heal the same target again until your next [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME).
