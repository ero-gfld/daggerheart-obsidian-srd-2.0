# Blink Out

***Level 4*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (12)**. On a success, **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to teleport to another point you can see within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. If any willing creatures are within Very Close range, **spend an additional Hope** for each creature to bring them with you.
