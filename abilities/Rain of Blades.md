# Rain of Blades

***Level 1*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** and conjure throwing blades that strike out at all targets within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against take **d8+2** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency.

If a target you hit is *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*, they take an extra **1d8** damage.
