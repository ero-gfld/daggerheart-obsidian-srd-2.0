# Blighting Strike

***Level 1*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success:

- On a roll with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), deal **d6+1** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency. On a roll with Fear, deal **d10+1** magic damage using your Proficiency.
- The target’s next successful attack deals half damage.

On a failure, you must spend a Hope or mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
