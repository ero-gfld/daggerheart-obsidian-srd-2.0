# Unyielding Armor

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When you would mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR), roll a number of **d6s** equal to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). If any roll a 6, reduce the severity by one threshold without marking an Armor Slot.
