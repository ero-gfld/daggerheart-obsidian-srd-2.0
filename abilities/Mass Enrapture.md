# Mass Enrapture

***[Level 8](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against all targets within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against become temporarily *Enraptured*. While *Enraptured*, a target’s attention is fixed on you, narrowing their field of view and drowning out any sound but your voice. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force all *Enraptured* targets to mark a Stress, ending this spell.
