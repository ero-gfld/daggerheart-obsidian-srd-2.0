# Hideous Retribution

***Level 2*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range takes damage from a target you can see, you can make a [reaction roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) against the target using your Spellcast trait. On a success, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to deal **d6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency.
