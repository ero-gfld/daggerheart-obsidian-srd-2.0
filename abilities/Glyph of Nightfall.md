# Glyph of Nightfall

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to conjure a dark glyph upon their body that exposes their weak points, temporarily reducing the target’s [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) by a value equal to your [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) (minimum 1).
