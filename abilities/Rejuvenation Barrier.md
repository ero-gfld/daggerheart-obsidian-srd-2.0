# Rejuvenation Barrier

***Level 8*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15)**. Once per rest on a success, create a temporary barrier of protective energy around you at [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. You and all allies within the barrier when this spell is cast clear **1d4** [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). While the barrier is up, you and all allies within have [resistance](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage from outside the barrier.

When you move, the barrier follows you.
