# Lead by Example

***Level 9*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

When you deal damage to an adversary, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** and describe how you encourage your allies. The next PC to make an attack against that adversary can clear a Stress or gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
