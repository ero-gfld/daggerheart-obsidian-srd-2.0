# Smite

***Level 5*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per rest, **spend 3 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to charge your powerful smite. When you next successfully attack with a weapon, double the result of your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). This attack deals magic damage regardless of the weapon’s damage type.
