# Corrosive Projectile

***Level 3*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, deal **d6+4** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency. Additionally, **mark 2 or more [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make them permanently *Corroded*. While a target is *Corroded*, they gain a −1 penalty to their [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) for every 2 Stress you spent. This condition can stack.
