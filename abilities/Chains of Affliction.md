# Chains of Affliction

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

**Mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to temporarily *Chain* a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. When a *Chained* creature deals damage, the target of their attack marks one fewer [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) than they normally would. You can’t have more than one creature *Chained* at a time.
