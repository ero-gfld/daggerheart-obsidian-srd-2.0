# Dread-Touched

***Level 7*** *Dread Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Dread domain, gain the following benefits:

- When you succeed with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to prevent the GM from gaining a Fear.
- Once per rest when making an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you can gain a bonus to the roll equal to the number of Fear in the GM’s pool.
