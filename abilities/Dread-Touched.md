# Dread-Touched

***[Level 7](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *Dread [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your [loadout](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) are from the Dread domain, gain the following benefits:

- When you succeed with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to prevent the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) from gaining a Fear.
- Once per rest when making an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you can gain a bonus to the roll equal to the number of [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) in the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s pool.
