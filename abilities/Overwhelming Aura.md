# Overwhelming Aura

***Level 9*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15)** to magically empower your aura. On a success, **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make your [Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) equal to your Spellcast trait until your next [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME).

While this spell is active, an adversary must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) when they target you with an attack.
