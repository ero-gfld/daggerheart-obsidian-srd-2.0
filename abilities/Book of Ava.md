# Book of Ava

***Level 1*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

***Power Push:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, they’re knocked back to Far range and take **d10+2** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency.

***Tava’s Armor:*** **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to give a target you can touch a +1 bonus to their [Armor Score](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR) until their next rest or you cast Tava’s Armor again.

***Ice Spike:*** Make a **Spellcast Roll (12)** to summon a large ice spike within Far range. If you use it as a weapon, make the Spellcast Roll against the target’s [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) instead. On a success, deal **d6** physical damage using your Proficiency.
