# Restoration

***Level 6*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

After a [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), place a number of tokens equal to your [Spellcast](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) trait on this card. Touch a creature and spend any number of tokens to clear 2 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) for each token spent.

You can also spend a token from this card when touching a creature to clear the *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* condition or heal a physical or magical ailment (the GM might require additional tokens depending on the strength of the ailment).

When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.
