# Eldritch Flesh

***[Level 8](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Gain a +1 bonus to your [damage thresholds](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) for each [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) you have marked.

Additionally, when you roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to clear an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR).
