# Midnight‑touched

***Level 7*** *Midnight Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Midnight domain, gain the following benefits:

- Once per rest, when you have 0 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) and the GM would gain a Fear, you can gain a Hope instead.
- When you make a successful attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to add the result of your [Fear Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW) to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
