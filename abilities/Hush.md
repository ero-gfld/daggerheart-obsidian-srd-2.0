# Hush

***[Level 5](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to conjure suppressive magic around the target that encompasses everything within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of them and follows them as they move.

The target and anything within the area is *Silenced* until the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) spends a [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) on their turn to clear this condition, you cast Hush again, or you take [Major damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). While *Silenced*, they can’t make noise and can’t cast spells.
