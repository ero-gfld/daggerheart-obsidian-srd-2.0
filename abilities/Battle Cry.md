# Battle Cry

***[Level 8](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), while you’re charging into danger, you can muster a rousing call that inspires your allies. All allies who can hear you each clear a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). Additionally, your allies gain [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) until you or an ally rolls a [failure with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).
