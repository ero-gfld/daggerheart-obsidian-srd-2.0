# Frenzy

***[Level 8](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can go into a *Frenzy* until there are no more [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within sight.

While *Frenzied*, you can’t use [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR), and you gain a +10 bonus to your [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and a +8 bonus to your [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) threshold.
