# Forager

***[Level 6](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

As an additional [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) you can choose, roll a d6 to see what you forage. Work with the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) to describe it and add it to your inventory as a consumable. Your party can carry up to five foraged consumables at a time. 1. A unique food (Clear 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)) 2. A beautiful relic (Gain 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)) 3. An arcane rune (+2 to a [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)) 4. A healing vial (Clear 2 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS)) 5. A luck charm (Reroll any die) 6. Choose one of the options above.
