# Mending Touch

***[Level 1](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

You lay your hands upon a creature and channel healing magic to close their wounds. When you can take a few minutes to focus on the target you’re helping, you can **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to clear a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) on them.

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), when you spend this healing time learning something new about them or revealing something about yourself, you can clear 2 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) on them instead.
