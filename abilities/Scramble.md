# Scramble

***[Level 3](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, when a creature within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range would deal damage to you, you can avoid the attack and safely move out of Melee range of the enemy.
