# Troublemaker

***[Level 2](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When you taunt or provoke a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, make a **[Presence](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) Roll** against them. Once per rest on a success, roll a number of **d4s** equal to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The target must mark [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) equal to the highest result rolled.
