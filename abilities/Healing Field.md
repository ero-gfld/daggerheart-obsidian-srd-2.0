# Healing Field

***Level 4*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), you can conjure a field of healing plants around you. Everywhere within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of you bursts to life with vibrant nature, allowing you and all allies in the area to clear a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).

**Spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to allow you and all allies to clear 2 Hit Points instead.
