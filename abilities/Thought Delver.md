# Thought Delver

***[Level 5](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

You can peek into the minds of others. **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to read the vague surface thoughts of a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against the target to delve for deeper, more hidden thoughts.

On a roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), the target might, at the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s discretion, become aware that you’re reading their thoughts.
