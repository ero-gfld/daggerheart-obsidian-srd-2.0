# Thought Delver

***Level 5*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

You can peek into the minds of others. **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to read the vague surface thoughts of a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against the target to delve for deeper, more hidden thoughts.

On a roll with Fear, the target might, at the GM’s discretion, become aware that you’re reading their thoughts.
