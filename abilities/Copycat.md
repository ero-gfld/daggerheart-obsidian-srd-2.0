# Copycat

***Level 9*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), this card can mimic the features of another domain card of level 8 or lower in another player’s loadout. **Spend [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) equal to half the card’s level** to gain access to the feature. It lasts until your next rest or they place the card in their vault.
