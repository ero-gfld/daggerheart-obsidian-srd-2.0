# Terrify

***Level 3*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the target marks **1d4** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS), and you can make the target flee one range away from you (such as Very Close to Close or Close to Far). On a [success with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), the target also becomes temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.
