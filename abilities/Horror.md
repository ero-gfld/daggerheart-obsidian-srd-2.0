# Horror

***Level 4*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per scene, **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to summon an otherworldly creature that deals **d8+1** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your [Spellcast](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) trait to a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. If the target marks any [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from this attack, they must succeed on a **Reaction Roll (12)** to steel themselves against the horror or mark an equal number of Stress.

After making the attack, the creature dissipates.
