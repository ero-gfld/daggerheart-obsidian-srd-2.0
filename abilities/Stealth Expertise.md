# Stealth Expertise

***Level 4*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

When you roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) while attempting to move unnoticed through a dangerous area, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to roll with Hope instead.

If an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range is also attempting to move unnoticed and rolls with Fear, you can **mark a Stress** to change their result to a roll with Hope.
