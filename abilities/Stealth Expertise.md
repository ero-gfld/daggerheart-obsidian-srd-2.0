# Stealth Expertise

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

When you roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) while attempting to move unnoticed through a dangerous area, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to roll with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) instead.

If an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range is also attempting to move unnoticed and rolls with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to change their result to a roll with [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
