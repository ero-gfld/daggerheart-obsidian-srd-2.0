# Book of Homet

***Level 7*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

***Pass Through:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13)**. Once per rest on a success, you and all creatures touching you can pass through a wall or door within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The effect ends once everyone is on the other side.

***Plane Gate:*** Make a **Spellcast Roll (14)**. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, open a gateway to a location in another dimension or plane of existence you’ve been to before. This gateway lasts until your next rest.
