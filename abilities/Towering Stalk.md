# Towering Stalk

***Level 3*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, you can conjure a thick, twisting stalk within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range that can be easily climbed. Its height can grow up to Far range.

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to use this spell as an attack. Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against an adversary or group of adversaries within Close range. The erupting stalk lifts targets you succeed against into the air and drops them, dealing **d8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency.
