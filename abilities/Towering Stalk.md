# Towering Stalk

***[Level 3](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, you can conjure a thick, twisting stalk within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range that can be easily climbed. Its height can grow up to [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to use this spell as an attack. Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) or group of [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The erupting stalk lifts targets you succeed against into the air and drops them, dealing **d8** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
