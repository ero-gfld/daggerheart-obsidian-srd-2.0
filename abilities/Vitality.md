***Level 5*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

- One [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) slot
- One [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) slot

Then place this card in your vault permanently.
