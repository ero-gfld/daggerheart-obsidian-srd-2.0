# Rousing Strike

***Level 5*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, when you critically succeed on an attack, you and all allies who can see or hear you can clear a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or **1d4** [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
