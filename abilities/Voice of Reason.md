# Voice of Reason

***Level 3*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

You speak with an unmatched power and authority. You have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on [action rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) to de‑escalate violent situations or convince someone to follow your lead.

Additionally, you’re emboldened in moments of duress. When all of your [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) slots are marked, you gain a +1 bonus to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for damage rolls.
