# Fane of the Wilds

***Level 9*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

After a [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), place a number of tokens equal to the number of Sage domain cards in your loadout and vault on this card.

When you would make a [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS), you can spend any number of tokens after the roll to gain a +1 bonus for each token spent.

When you critically succeed on a Spellcast Roll for a Sage domain spell, gain a token.

When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.
