# Support Tank

***Level 4*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range fails a roll, you can **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to allow them to reroll either their Hope or [Fear Die](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW).
