# Body Basher

***Level 2*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

You use the full force of your body in a fight. On a successful attack using a weapon with a [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, gain a bonus to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) equal to your [Strength](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203).
