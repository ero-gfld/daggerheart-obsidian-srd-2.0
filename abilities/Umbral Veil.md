# Umbral Veil

***Level 1*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to encase yourself in shadowy energy. When you do, place a number of tokens on this card equal to the number of [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) in the GM’s pool. After an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) is made against you, you can spend any number of tokens to give the result a −1 penalty per token spent.

At the end of the scene, clear all unspent tokens.
