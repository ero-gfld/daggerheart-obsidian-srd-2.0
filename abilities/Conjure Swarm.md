# Conjure Swarm

***Level 2*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

***Tekaira Armored Beetles:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to conjure armored beetles that encircle you. When you next take damage, reduce the severity by one threshold. You can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to keep the beetles conjured after taking damage.

***Fire Flies:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against all adversaries within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. **Spend a Hope** to deal **2d8+3** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to targets you succeeded against.
