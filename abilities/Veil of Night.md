# Veil of Night

***Level 3*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13)**. On a success, you can create a temporary curtain of darkness between two points within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Only you can see through this darkness. You’re considered *[Hidden](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* to adversaries on the other side of the veil, and you have [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE) on attacks you make through the darkness. The veil remains until you cast another spell.
