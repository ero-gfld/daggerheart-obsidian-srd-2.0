# Voice of Dread

***[Level 1](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *Dread [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

You can magically speak to a creature you can see, tormenting them with your words. Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against them. On a success, they must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) and are frozen with terror, making them temporarily *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.
