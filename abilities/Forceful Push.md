# Forceful Push

***Level 1*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

Make an attack with your [primary weapon](../core_rules/Equipment.md#WEAPONS) against a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, you deal damage and knock them back to Close range. On a [success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), add a **d6** to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

Additionally, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make them temporarily *[Vulnerable](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.
