# Invoke Torment

***Level 10*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

You deal double damage to targets that have all their [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) marked.

Additionally, when an adversary within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range is defeated with all its Stress marked, you gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)..
