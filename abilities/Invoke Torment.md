# Invoke Torment

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

You deal double damage to targets that have all their [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) marked.

Additionally, when an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range is defeated with all its [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) marked, you gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)..
