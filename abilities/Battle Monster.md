# Battle Monster

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

When you make a successful attack against an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md), you can **mark 4 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force the target to mark a number of [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) equal to the number of Hit Points you currently have marked instead of rolling for damage.
