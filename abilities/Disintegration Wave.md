# Disintegration Wave

***Level 9*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *4.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (18)**. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, the GM tells you which adversaries within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range have a [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) of 18 or lower. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** for each one you wish to hit with this spell. They are killed and can’t come back to life by any means.
