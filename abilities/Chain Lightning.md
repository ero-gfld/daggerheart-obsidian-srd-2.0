# Chain Lightning

***Level 5*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)**, unleashing lightning on all targets within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against must make a reaction roll with a [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) equal to the result of your Spellcast Roll. Targets who fail take **2d8+4** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Additional adversaries not already targeted by Chain Lightning and within Close range of previous targets who took damage must also make the reaction roll. Targets who fail take **2d8+4** magic damage. This chain continues until there are no more adversaries within range.
