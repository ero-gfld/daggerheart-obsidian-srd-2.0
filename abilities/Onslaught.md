# Onslaught

***Level 10*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

When you successfully make an attack with your weapon, you never deal damage beneath a target’s [Major damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) threshold (the target always marks a minimum of 2 Hit Points).

Additionally, when a creature within your weapon’s range deals damage to an ally with an attack that doesn’t include you, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force them to make a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15). On a failure, the target must mark a Hit Point.
