# Natural Familiar

***[Level 2](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon a small nature spirit or forest critter to your side until your next rest, you cast Natural Familiar again, or the familiar is targeted by an attack. If you **spend an additional Hope**, you can summon a familiar that flies. You can communicate with them, make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** to command them to perform simple tasks, and **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to see through their eyes.

When you deal damage to an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of your familiar, you add a **d6** to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
