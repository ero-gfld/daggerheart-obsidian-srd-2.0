# Bone‑touched

***[Level 7](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *Bone [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your [loadout](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) are from the Bone domain, gain the following benefits:

- +1 bonus to [Agility](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203)
- Once per rest, you can **spend 3 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to cause an attack that succeeded against you to fail instead.
