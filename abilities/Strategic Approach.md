# Strategic Approach

***Level 2*** *[Bone](../domains/Bone.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

After a [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), place a number of tokens equal to your [Knowledge](../core_rules/Character%20Creation.md#STEP%203) on this card (minimum 1). The first time you move within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of an adversary and make an attack against them, you can spend one token to choose one of the following options:

- You make the attack with [advantage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ADVANTAGE%20&%20DISADVANTAGE).
- You clear a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) on an ally within Melee range of the adversary.
- You add a **d8** to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.
