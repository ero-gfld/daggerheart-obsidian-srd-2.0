# Plant Dominion

***[Level 9](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (18)**. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, you reshape the natural world, changing the surrounding plant life anywhere within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of you. For example, you can grow trees instantly, clear a path through dense vines, or create a wall of roots.
