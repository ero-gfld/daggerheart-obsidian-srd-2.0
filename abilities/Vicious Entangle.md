# Vicious Entangle

***[Level 1](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, roots and vines reach out from the ground, dealing **1d8+1** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and temporarily *Restraining* the target.

Additionally on a success, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to temporarily *[Restrain](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)* another [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of your target.
