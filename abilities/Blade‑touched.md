# Blade‑touched

***[Level 7](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *Blade [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your [loadout](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) are from the Blade domain, gain the following benefits:

- +2 bonus to your [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING)
- +4 bonus to your [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) threshold
