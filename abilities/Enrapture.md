# Enrapture

***[Level 1](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, they become temporarily *Enraptured*. While *Enraptured*, a target’s attention is fixed on you, narrowing their field of view and drowning out any sound but your voice. Once per rest on a success, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to force the *Enraptured* target to mark a Stress as well.
