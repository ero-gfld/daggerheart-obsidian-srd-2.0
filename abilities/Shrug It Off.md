# Shrug It Off

***Level 7*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When you would take damage, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to reduce the severity of the damage by one threshold. When you do, roll a **d6**. On a result of 3 or lower, place this card in your vault.
