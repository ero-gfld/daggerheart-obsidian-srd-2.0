# Grace‑touched

***Level 7*** *Grace Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Grace domain, gain the following benefits:

- You can **mark an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR)** instead of marking a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
- When you would force a target to mark a number of [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), you can choose instead to force them to mark that number of Stress.
