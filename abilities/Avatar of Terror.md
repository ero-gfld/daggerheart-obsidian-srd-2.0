# Avatar of Terror

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to transform into a creature fueled by fear. While in this form, you gain a **1d6** bonus to your [damage rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) in the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F)’s pool. Additionally, you gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) when the GM spends a Fear to [spotlight](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#THE%20SPOTLIGHT) an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.

Before you make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you must spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR). If you can’t, you revert to your normal form.
