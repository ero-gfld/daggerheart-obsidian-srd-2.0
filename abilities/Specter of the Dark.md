# Specter of the Dark

***Level 10*** *[Midnight](../domains/Midnight.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to become *Spectral* until you make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) targeting another creature. While *Spectral*, you’re [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage and can float and pass through solid objects. Other creatures can still see you while you’re in this form.
