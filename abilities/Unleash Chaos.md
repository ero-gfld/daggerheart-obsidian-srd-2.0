# Unleash Chaos

***Level 1*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

At the beginning of a session, place a number of tokens equal to your [Spellcast](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) trait on this card.

Make a **Spellcast Roll** against a target within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and spend any number of tokens to channel raw energy from within yourself to unleash against them. On a success, roll a number of **d10s** equal to the tokens you spent and deal that much [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to the target. **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to replenish this card with tokens (up to your Spellcast trait).

At the end of each session, clear all unspent tokens.
