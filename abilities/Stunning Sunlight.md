# Stunning Sunlight

***[Level 8](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** to unleash powerful rays of burning sunlight against all [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) in front of you within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, **spend any number of [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and force that many targets you succeeded against to make a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (14).

Targets who succeed take **3d20+3** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). Targets who fail take **4d20+5** magic damage and are temporarily *Stunned*. While *Stunned*, they can’t use reactions and can’t take any other actions until they clear this condition.
