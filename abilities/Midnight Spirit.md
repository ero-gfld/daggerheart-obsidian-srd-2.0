# Midnight Spirit

***Level 2*** *Midnight Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

**Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to summon a humanoid‑sized spirit that can move or carry things for you until your next rest.

You can also send it to attack an adversary. When you do, make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Very Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, the spirit moves into Melee range with that target. Roll a number of **d6s** equal to your Spellcast trait and deal that much [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to the target. The spirit then dissipates. You can only have one spirit at a time.
