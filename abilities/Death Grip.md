# Death Grip

***[Level 4](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and choose one of the following options:

- You pull the target into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range or pull yourself into Melee range of them.
- You constrict the target and force them to mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
- All [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) between you and the target must succeed on a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (13) or be hit by vines, taking **3d6+2** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

On a success, vines reach out from your hands, causing the chosen effect and temporarily *Restraining* the target.
