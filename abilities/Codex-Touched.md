# Codex-Touched

**_Level 7_** _Codex Ability._ **_Recall Cost_** _2._

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your loadout are from the Codex domain, gain the following benefits:

- You can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to add your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to a [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS).
- Once per rest, replace this card with any card from your vault without paying its Recall Cost.
