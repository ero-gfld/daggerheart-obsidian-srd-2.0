# Second Wind

***Level 3*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per rest, when you succeed on an attack against an adversary, you can clear 3 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS). On a [success with Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), you also clear 3 Stress or a Hit Point on an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of you.
