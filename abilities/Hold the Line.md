# Hold the Line

***Level 9*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Describe the defensive stance you take and **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)**. If an adversary moves within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, they’re pulled into Melee range and *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.

This condition lasts until you move or fail a roll with Fear, or the GM spends 2 Fear on their turn to clear it.
