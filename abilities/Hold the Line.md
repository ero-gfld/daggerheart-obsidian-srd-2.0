# Hold the Line

***[Level 9](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Describe the defensive stance you take and **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)**. If an [adversary](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) moves within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, they’re pulled into [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range and *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.

This condition lasts until you move or fail a roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), or the [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) spends 2 Fear on their turn to clear it.
