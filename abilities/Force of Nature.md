# Force of Nature

***[Level 10](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

**Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to transform into a hulking nature spirit, gaining the following benefits:

- When you succeed on an attack or [Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS), gain a +10 bonus to the [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).
- When you deal enough damage to defeat a creature within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you absorb them and clear an [Armor Slot](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR).
- You can’t be *[Restrained](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STANDARD%20CONDITIONS)*.

Before you make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS), you must **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)**. If you can’t, you revert to your normal form.
