# Never Upstaged

***[Level 6](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Grace](../domains/Grace.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

When you mark 1 or more [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) from an attack, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to place a number of tokens equal to the number of Hit Points you marked on this card. On your next successful attack, gain a +5 bonus to your [damage roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) for each token on this card, then clear all tokens.
