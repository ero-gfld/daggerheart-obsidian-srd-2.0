# Healing Strike

***Level 7*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When you deal damage to an adversary, you can **spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to clear a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) on an ally within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.
