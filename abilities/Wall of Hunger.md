# Wall of Hunger

***[Level 7](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (10)**. On a success, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to create a visible wall of writhing necrotic energy between two points within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. The wall lasts until you mark a [Hit Point](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) or cast this spell again. A creature inside the wall when it appears or that passes through it must mark 2 [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
