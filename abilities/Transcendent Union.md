# Transcendent Union

***Level 10*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), **spend 5 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to cast this spell on two or more willing creatures. Until your next rest, when a creature connected by this union would mark [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS), the connected creatures can choose who marks it.
