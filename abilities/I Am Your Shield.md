# I Am Your Shield

***Level 1*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When an ally within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range would take damage, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to stand in the way and make yourself the target of the attack instead. When you take damage from this attack, you can mark any number of [Armor Slots](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR).
