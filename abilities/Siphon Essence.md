# Siphon Essence

***Level 2*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, the target takes **d12+4** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Proficiency. On a [success with Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#OVERVIEW), you gain a +1 bonus to your Proficiency for this attack.

You clear a number of [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) equal to the number of Hit Points the target marked from this attack.
