# Banish

***Level 6*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against a target within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. On a success, roll a number of **d20s** equal to your Spellcast trait. The target must make a reaction roll with a [Difficulty](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) equal to your highest result. On a success, the target must mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) but isn’t banished. Once per rest on a failure, they are banished from this realm.

When the PCs roll with [Fear](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR), the Difficulty gains a −1 penalty and the target makes another reaction roll. On a success, they return from banishment.
