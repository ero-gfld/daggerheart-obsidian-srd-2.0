# Conjured Steeds

***Level 6*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

**Spend any number of [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to conjure that many magical steeds (such as horses, camels, or elephants) that you and your allies can ride until your next [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) or the steeds take any damage. The steeds double your land speed while traveling and, when in danger, allow you to move within [Far](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range without having to roll. Creatures riding a steed gain a −2 penalty to [attack rolls](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and a +2 bonus to damage rolls.
