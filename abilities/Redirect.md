# Redirect

***Level 4*** *[Bone](../domains/Bone.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

When an attack made against you from beyond [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range fails, roll a number of **d6s** equal to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). If any roll a 6, you can **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to redirect the attack to damage an adversary within Very Close range instead.
