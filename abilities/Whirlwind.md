# Whirlwind

***[Level 1](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

When you make a successful attack against a target within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, you can **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to use the attack against all other targets within Very Close range. All additional [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) you succeed against with this ability take half damage.
