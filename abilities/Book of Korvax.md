# Book of Korvax

***Level 3*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

***Levitation:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** to temporarily lift a target you can see up into the air and move them within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of their original position.

***Recant:*** **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force a target within Melee range to make a Reaction Roll (15). On a failure, they forget the last minute of your conversation.

***Rune Circle:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to create a temporary magical circle on the ground where you stand. All adversaries within Melee range, or who enter Melee range, take **2d12+4** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are knocked back to Very Close range.
