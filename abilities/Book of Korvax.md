# Book of Korvax

***[Level 3](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) [Grimoire](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

***Levitation:*** Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** to temporarily lift a target you can see up into the air and move them within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of their original position.

***Recant:*** **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to force a target within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range to make a [Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15). On a failure, they forget the last minute of your conversation.

***Rune Circle:*** **Mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to create a temporary magical circle on the ground where you stand. All [adversaries](../core_rules/Using%20Adversaries.md) within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range, or who enter Melee range, take **2d12+4** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) and are knocked back to [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range.
