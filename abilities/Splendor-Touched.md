# Splendor-Touched

**_Level 7_** _Splendor Ability._ **_Recall Cost_** _2._

When 4 or more of the [domain cards](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS) in your loadout are from the Splendor domain, gain the following benefits:

- +3 bonus to your [Severe damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) threshold
- Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), when incoming damage would require you to mark a number of Hit Points, you can choose to mark that much [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or spend that much [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR) instead.
