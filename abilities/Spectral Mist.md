# Spectral Mist

***Level 5*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

**Spend 2 [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to conjure an eerie mist that turns you and allies of your choice within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range momentarily incorporeal. While a creature is incorporeal, they can move through solid objects and are [immune](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) to physical damage. They become corporeal again after they pass through a solid object or make an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS). Otherwise, this effect lasts until the end of the scene.
