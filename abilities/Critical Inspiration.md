# Critical Inspiration

***Level 3*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, when you critically succeed on an attack, all allies within [Very Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range can clear a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS) or gain a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR).
