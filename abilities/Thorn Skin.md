# Thorn Skin

***[Level 5](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) [Spell](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

Once per rest, **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to sprout thorns all over your body. When you do, place a number of tokens equal to your [Spellcast](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) trait on this card. When you take damage, you can spend any number of tokens to roll that number of **d6s**. Add the results together and reduce the incoming damage by that amount. If you’re within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range of the attacker, deal that amount of damage back to them.

When you take a rest, clear all unspent tokens.
