# Confusing Aura

***Level 8*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (14)**. Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME) on a success, you create a layer of illusion over your body that makes it hard to tell exactly where you are. **Mark any number of [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to make that many additional layers. When an adversary makes an attack against you, roll a number of **d6s** equal to the number of layers currently active. If any roll a 5 or higher, one layer of the aura is destroyed and the attack fails. If all the results are 4 or lower, you take the damage and this spell ends
