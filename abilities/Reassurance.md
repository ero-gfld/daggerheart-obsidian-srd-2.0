# Reassurance

***Level 1*** *[Splendor](../domains/Splendor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

Once per rest, after an ally attempts an [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) but before the consequences take place, you can offer assistance or words of support. When you do, your ally can reroll their dice.
