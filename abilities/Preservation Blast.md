# Preservation Blast

***Level 4*** *[Arcana](../domains/Arcana.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Make a **[Spellcast Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS)** against all targets within [Melee](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Targets you succeed against are forced back to Far range and take **d8+3** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Spellcast trait.
