# Armorer

***Level 5*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *1.*

While you’re wearing armor, gain a +1 bonus to your [Armor Score](../core_rules/Equipment.md#ARMOR).

During a rest, when you choose to repair your armor as a [downtime move](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), your allies also clear an Armor Slot.
