# Wild Surge

***Level 7*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), **mark a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS)** to channel the natural world around you and enhance yourself. Describe how your appearance changes, then place a **d6** on this card with the 1 value facing up.

While the Wild Surge Die is active, you add its value to every [action roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ACTION%20ROLLS) you make. After you add its value to a roll, increase the Wild Surge Die’s value by one. When the die’s value would exceed 6 or you take a rest, this form drops and you must **mark an additional Stress**.
