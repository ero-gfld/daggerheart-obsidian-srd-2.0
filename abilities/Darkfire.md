# Darkfire

***Level 6*** *[Dread](../domains/Dread.md) Spell.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Once per scene, **spend any number of [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to target an equal number of adversaries within [Close](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#MAPS,%20RANGE,%20AND%20MOVEMENT) range. Each target makes a **[Reaction Roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) (15)**. Targets who fail take **d8+6** [magic damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING) using your Spellcast trait as they are engulfed in dark fire. Targets who succeed take half damage.
