# Gifted Tracker

***Level 1*** *[Sage](../domains/Sage.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *0.*

When you’re tracking a specific creature or group of creatures based on signs of their passage, you can **spend any number of [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** and ask the GM that many questions from the following list.

- What direction did they go?
- How long ago did they pass through?
- What were they doing in this location?
- How many of them were here?

When you encounter creatures you’ve tracked in this way, gain a +1 bonus to your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) against them.
