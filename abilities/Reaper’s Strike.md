# Reaper’s Strike

***Level 9*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The GM tells you which targets within range it would succeed against. Choose one of these targets and force them to mark 5 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
