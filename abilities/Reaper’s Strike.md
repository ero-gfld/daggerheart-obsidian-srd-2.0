# Reaper’s Strike

***[Level 9](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *[Blade](../domains/Blade.md) [Ability](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS).* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

Once per [long rest](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#DOWNTIME), **spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to make an [attack roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). The [GM](../core_rules/Introduction.md#WHAT%20IS%20DAGGERHEART%3F) tells you which targets within range it would succeed against. Choose one of these targets and force them to mark 5 [Hit Points](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS).
