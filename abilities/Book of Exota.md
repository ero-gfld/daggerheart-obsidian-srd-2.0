# Book of Exota

***Level 4*** *[Codex](../domains/Codex.md) Grimoire.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *3.*

***Repudiate:*** You can interrupt a magical effect taking place. Make a [reaction roll](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#SPECIAL%20ROLLS) using your Spellcast trait. Once per rest on a success, the effect stops and any consequences are avoided.

***Create Construct:*** **Spend a [Hope](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HOPE%20&%20FEAR)** to choose a group of objects around you and create an animated construct from them that obeys basic commands. Make a **Spellcast Roll** to command them to take action. When necessary, they share your [Evasion](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#EVASION) and traits and their attacks deal **2d10+3** [physical damage](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING). You can only maintain one construct at a time, and they fall apart when they take any amount of damage.
