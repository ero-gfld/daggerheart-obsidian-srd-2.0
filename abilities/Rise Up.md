# Rise Up

***Level 6*** *[Valor](../domains/Valor.md) Ability.* ***[Recall Cost](../core_rules/Domains.md#DOMAIN%20CARDS)*** *2.*

Gain a bonus to your [Severe threshold](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#HIT%20POINTS%20&%20DAMAGE%20THRESHOLDS) equal to your [Proficiency](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#ATTACKING).

When you mark 1 or more Hit Points from an attack, clear a [Stress](../core_rules/Core%20Mechanics.md#STRESS).
